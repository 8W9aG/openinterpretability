Circuits in {{ model.name }}

Units in the registry joined by their wiring: each experiment records its unit's strongest direct connections, from the weights and from activations on its review's prompts, and an edge is kept when the unit at its other end has a page too. Units joined edge to edge form one circuit, laid out by layer (attention heads above neurons in a layer, since attention runs first). A hub, a unit wired to at least {{ (hub_share * 100) | round | int }}% of the model's studied units (and at least {{ hub_min }}), would join them all into one star, so its edges don't join circuits; hubs are listed after the circuits. Edges are direct effects only, and a unit's wiring lists only its top connections, so a missing edge is not evidence of no connection. Reported, not gated.
