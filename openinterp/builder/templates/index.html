Null results are published too: a well-tested hypothesis that fails tells us as much as one that passes.

{% if stats.baseline %}

Learned-basis baseline

Are a model's own neurons as explainable as the latents of a sparse dictionary trained on its MLPs? Randomly selected latents and randomly selected neuron poles of the same model and layers, scanned the same way and audited under the same protocol. Each unit counts once, by its first experiment, so follow-ups don't favour either side. Intervals are 95% Wilson intervals; p is a two-sided Fisher exact test of latents against positive poles (and against all poles). In a GELU MLP, such as GPT-2's, the negative pole sits in the activation's dip on most tokens, so no hard negative can pass; there the positive poles are the comparison.
