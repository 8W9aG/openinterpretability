Errata

Records are write-once, so a harness bug found later can't be fixed in them. Instead a maintainer adds an erratum to wiki/errata.jsonl and signs it. Each erratum names the records the bug affected, and says whether their readings still count (flagged) or no longer count toward any status (set aside). See the protocol, §5.
