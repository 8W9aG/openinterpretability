Sister neurons

Poles of other models whose activity over the corpus correlates best with this pole's, from openinterp sisters. The two models are compared where both have read the same text (where a token ends in both tokenizations), leaving out first tokens; coverage is the share of this model's tokens that pairs. r: Pearson correlation of the two poles' strengths. Back: the matched pole's best correlation with any pole of this model; ✓ when that best partner is this pole (a mutual best match). Rotated: the best correlation this pole reaches with a unit of a random rotation of the top match's layer. A mutual best match above it is a sister: a candidate for the same feature. A badge gives the verdict of a sister study, which audits the match with this pole's explanation, unchanged. Reported, not gated.
