## Hypothesis

> The positive pole fires retrospectively on 'which-one' specifiers - the token that picks out a specific but not-yet-filled referent, or fixes which instance or which time of the following noun is meant - and not on words that describe that noun. Its core members are the wh-words that head an interrogative or relative wh-phrase (' what' is the scan peak at 1.69 and is above the activity line on 70% of its 113 corpus occurrences; likewise 'which', 'who', 'whom', 'whose', 'how', 'why', 'where', 'when', 'whether', 'whatever'), followed by determiner-like modifiers of time or identity in front of a noun (' former' active on 95% of its 19 occurrences, then 'past', 'present', 'current', 'recent', 'new', 'old', 'prior', 'previous', 'existing', 'original', 'future', 'interim', and demonstrative 'that'/'this'); the same strings fall to the floor when they are only clause linkers (complementizer 'that' reads 0.15 where demonstrative 'that' reads 1.18 in the same lead-in carrier; conditional subordinator 'when' and embedded-question 'whether' are likewise silent) and descriptive or classifying modifiers stay at the floor (wooden, tired, tall, happy, local, national, senior, junior, main, acting all read <= 0.31 in the same 'The ___ manager of the regional office' frame).
