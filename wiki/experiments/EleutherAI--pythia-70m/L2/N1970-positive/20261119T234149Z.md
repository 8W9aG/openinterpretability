## Hypothesis

> The pole fires on the abstract, classificatory vocabulary that organizes a piece of exposition and on the link that carries such a head's content: abstract heads like aspect(s), way(s), kind, sort, type, example, variety, view, overview, means, approach, reasons, framework, statement, tips and advice, the verbs discuss, describe, explain and mean, and — only where a head of that kind governs them — the linking words of, for, about and to (an overview of, several ways of/to, reasons for, a framework for, a statement about) and the wh-words how, what, whether, when and where that introduce that head's content clause (explained how, know whether, the day when). It drops to the 0–0.3 band when the same linking words attach to a concrete, physical, quantified or proper head (the roof of the house, a loaf of bread, the length of the rope, the road to the town, the colour of the car) or when a wh-word opens an adverbial clause (he left when the war ended); the head words themselves fire in most of their senses, so the discrimination lives in the link rather than in the sense of the head, and a residue of peaks on subword pieces inside long abstract words (ly in strikingly, al in portrayal, otomy in dichotomy) is left uncovered.
