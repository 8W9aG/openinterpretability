## Hypothesis

> This is a retrospective attributive-modifier detector: it fires on the token that completes a modifier inside a noun phrase - most strongly superlative/degree words (' most' straight after ' the', ' best', '-est'/'biggest'/'largest'), then attributive adjectives and past participles sitting before the noun they qualify ('the terrible table', 'the broken window'), and weakly on prepositions/coordinators that link two nouns ('the N of N', 'N and N'). Its strength is set by the modifier token itself and not by the head noun that follows (activation is identical across five different head nouns), while head nouns, verbs, determiners and attributive noun compounds ('the music industry') stay at the neuron's floor.
