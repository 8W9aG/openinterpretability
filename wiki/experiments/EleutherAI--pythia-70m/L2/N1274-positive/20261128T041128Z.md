## Hypothesis

> A token-level detector with three clusters. (1) A word-internal Arabic 'ر' (r) that follows a prefix or letter inside the same word, as in يرغب or الركاب. The same holds for similar mid-word letter pieces in Hebrew and Thai. (2) Capitalised institutional nouns and titles after a determiner or label, such as ' Institute', ' Department', ' Society', ' University', ' Asst', ' Assistant' and ' critic'. Lowercase forms ('the society', 'the department') stay weak. (3) A stem piece completing a hyphen or prefix compound, such as self-[ref]lection, re[negot]iated and de-[en]ergized, plus ' as' after 'used' or 'served'. It stays near zero on ' as' after 'known', 'seen' or 'treated', and on other letters in the same Arabic slot.
