## Hypothesis

> N1942+ is a fixed-format template/boilerplate detector, strongest on the licence prologue of a source file: it fires on that prologue's structural tokens — the '://' of the licence-reference URL, the '\asf_license' markers, licence/warranty wording and its ';' punctuation, and the '#include'/'#ifndef' directive that closes the prologue — and on equivalent fixed-format field syntax elsewhere (an email 'Subject:' header, wiki section headings, 'HH:00' timestamps, a LaTeX '\begin'). It is retrospective and cumulative over the whole header (the peak token supplies about 30% of the attribution and licence/header wording up to ~20 tokens back the rest), so activity is predicted by membership in such a fixed-format template rather than by the bare token: the same colon, '://' or '#include' in free prose without that template is weaker.

- **Activating tokens:** `://`, `include`, `as`, ` LICENSE`, `licenses`, ` WARRANTIES`, `00`, `:`, `;`, `begin`
- **Mechanism claimed:** retrospective
