## Hypothesis

> The pole is a copyright-and-licence notice register detector: it fires on tokens that carry the legal terms of a software licence (License / LICENSE / licenses, rights and reserved in 'All rights reserved', warranty, permission / redistribution language), it also fires on ordinary words such as 'copy', 'AUTHORS' or 'distributed' while they sit inside an established notice block, and the preceding cue word ('All', 'SPDX-') is itself a strong driver of the 'rights' and 'License' peaks. It is a retrospective register feature rather than a code-syntax, whitespace or newline-lookahead feature: it is indifferent to language, corpus source, comment marker, copyright holder and year, and in ordinary prose it can still be triggered by the surface sequence 'all ... right'.
