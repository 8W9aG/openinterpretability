## Hypothesis

> English only: fires most on the last word of a conditional or frequency clause opener: 'case' in 'in case' / 'in that case' / 'in case of', 'time(s)' right after every/each/many/the first/by the, and 'if' after a comma, 'that', 'what', 'so', 'and' or 'but' ('few, if any'; 'what if'). Weaker but still active: sentence-initial If/When/Once/Whenever/Suppose. It also fires at about 0.35-0.8 of max on a digit numeral that counts discrete things before a noun ('145 patients', '7 dogs', '1,000 years', '90 percent of'). It stays quiet on other uses of 'time' and 'case' ('a great time', 'this time', 'the case was'), on 'if' meaning 'whether' or after as/even/only ('asked if', 'as if'), on spelled-out small numbers ('two', 'twelve'), and on digits used as measurements, prices, labels or dates ('30 kg', '$45', 'Table 3', 'May 14'). Years are weak and uneven, usually below 0.35 of max.
