## Hypothesis

> Fires on the last token of an opener of a fronted conditional or temporal clause ('case' in 'in case', 'time' in 'every/each time', 'if' after a comma, 'what', 'that' or 'so', and sentence-initial If/Once/When/Whenever), but only weakly on 'if' straight after a verb ('call me if') or on noun uses of 'case' and 'time'. It also fires, at about 0.6-0.8 of max, on multi-digit cardinal numerals used as counts before a noun ('190 lakhs', '776 patients', '1,000 years'), and much more weakly on years and one- or two-digit numbers.
