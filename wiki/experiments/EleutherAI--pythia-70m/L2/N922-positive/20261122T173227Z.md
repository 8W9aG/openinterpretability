## Hypothesis

> This pole fires retrospectively when the current token is a highly predictable continuation that completes a multi-token chunk begun in the preceding one or two tokens: most often a word-internal subword piece finishing a split word (Asp+al, cont+en, rep+ud, hydraz+one), with the same completion logic extending to formulaic phrase tails (month/season + of + year), structural newlines after headings, and URL punctuation. It is driven by the preceding chunk onset rather than by preparation of any specific upcoming token, so identical peak tokens activate strongly in chunk context and weakly elsewhere.
