Predict non-English Latin-script prose (Turkish, Polish, Portuguese, German, Spanish) active on nearly every token. It is strongest (0.5-0.75 of max) on and after diacritic or byte-fragment tokens such as ' ç', 'ż', ' iç', 'ö' or 'ð', even when only one such word sits in an English sentence. The head is also strong (0.4-1.0) on the newline that ends a code or block-comment line when earlier lines began with the same indentation or ' *' token (it attends back to those tokens), and on chained ' -'/' +' operators in 'Let d(j) = 0 - j - 2*j - 9 + ...' arithmetic. The bridge was closed for repairs last summer.'). 27/27 such texts measured below the line, with a highest peak of 0.86 (0.19 of max).
