## Hypothesis

> L2/N1540+ fires on the token that *closes* a memorised multi-token unit, i.e. a token the preceding words have already fixed rather than one the model must choose: an auxiliary/copula after a pronoun or modal ('she was', 'might be', 'have been', 'had been'), a preposition after its governor ('because of', 'due to', 'thanks to'), the second word of a stock adverbial or connective ('in particular', 'as a result', 'in many ways', 'in abstract terms', 'at best', 'whether or not', 'as for', 'except for'), a unit or plural marker after a number ('5pm', '30 ml', '1930s', '1980s'), and the final piece of a multi-piece word ('flust|ered', 'princ|iples', 'Thank|fully', 'disadvant|aged'). It is retrospective, not predictive: activation collapses for the very same token when the left context leaves the choice open ('The ship was' vs 'She was', 'instead of' vs 'because of', 'This particular problem' vs 'in particular', 'output the result' vs 'as a result', 'cannot be' vs 'might be'), and it tracks neither general surprisal nor bigram frequency nor the model's own next-token probability, so it marks a completed stored unit (which units it stores is idiosyncratic: 'in particular' fires but 'in practice' does not).
