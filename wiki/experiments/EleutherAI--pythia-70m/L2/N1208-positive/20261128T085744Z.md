## Hypothesis

> Fires on the token right after a short word-initial fragment while the word is still unfinished. Mostly this is a hyphen after a short bound prefix or letter/abbreviation (ex-, co-, anti-, neo-, T-, p-, St-), or a short mid-word subword continuation after a 1-3 letter word start (St|am, ch|ak, e|ep, ath|l, J|ak, ком|оз). The trigger is a specific bigram, keyed mainly on the preceding fragment: it is weak or silent when the hyphen follows a full free-standing word (long-, high-, red-, self-, multi-), when the prefix is written as a separate word or fused into the word, and when the same fragment takes another continuation (St|ef).
