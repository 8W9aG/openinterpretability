## Hypothesis

> N624+ is a retrospective layer-1 lexical detector: it fires on an open-class word or word-piece that is the memorised second half of a two-token phrase (norm, promoter, composite, amount, means, ways, dishes; the 'ş' of cumhurbaşkanı, the 'स' of मित्सुबिशी, the 'rim' of matrimonial) and stays at the GELU floor on function words (a, the, to, my) and on most other open-class words (want, point, code, kind, telephone). The one general rule is the determiner: holding the word fixed, activation is 0.1-0.5 of max higher after an indefinite, quantifier or possessive determiner (a, an, another, some, one, my, his, your) than after a definite or demonstrative one (the, this, these, those, all, both), and it does not track surprisal - 'cup' is more surprising after 'the' (9.5 nats) than after 'a' (7.8 nats) yet fires lower (0.89 vs 1.72).
