## Hypothesis

> Pole N390+ fires on the token that opens a new word, and most strongly where the model has to supply that word itself rather than read it off the left context: it peaks on the first subword piece of a word the tokenizer must spell out in several pieces (a bare 'Ġ' space piece, or first pieces such as ' d|luż', ' n|agels', 'g|ekon|fijte', 'un|ters|chriebener', 'ant|idis|establ|ishment|arian'), and peaks second on a whole word sitting directly after a definite article or possessive determiner ('the other', 'the following', 'The next', 'the great', 'the only', 'The stone'). It stays near zero on 'the'/'of'/'and'/'with' themselves, on punctuation, on the later pieces of a split word, and on words that follow a preposition or conjunction (whole words after ' the' fire at 26% of scanned positions versus 0.9% after ' of').
