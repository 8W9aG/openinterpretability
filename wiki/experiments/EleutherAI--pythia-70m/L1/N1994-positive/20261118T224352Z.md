## Hypothesis

> A token-identity detector for a fixed, semantically mixed word list: it fires on the word itself, in any syntactic role (after 'was', after 'a'/'the', as subject or object), once at least about three tokens of text precede it: the deceit and mortality words lie/lies/lying/lied, dies/dying/death/suicide/mourning, guilty, punishment, arrest, and also comforting, nearest, winning, statement, holding, donating, and the second subword piece of split words such as b|itten, st|umbling, how|ling, Can|oe, To|ys, Compar|isons, plus LaTeX \sum. These reach about 0.3-1.0 of max, and an immediately preceding copula or linking verb (was, seemed, found him) adds roughly a third more. It stays below about 0.15 of max on function words, punctuation, colour words (red, blue, green, white, black), ready/early/late/busy/big, the crime-neighbour words trial, verdict and liar, and the same list words when they are the first or second token of the text. Text-initial My/Our/Your/Three/When also reach about 0.3 of max.
