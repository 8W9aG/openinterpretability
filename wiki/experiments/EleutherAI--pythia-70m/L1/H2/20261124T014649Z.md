## Hypothesis

> L1/H2 is a dense local-value head: at each measured position its attention lands on one nearby token — nearly always the token just before it, sometimes the position itself, otherwise the text's first token — and it writes one fixed linear image of that token's layer-0 value into the residual stream, so its strength above the 0.83 floor is just how far that single attended token's value sits from the head's mean write. It crosses 0.25x max where the token it lands on is a high-value one: existential 'there' before a verb anywhere in the text ('there is/are/was/were/will be/has been/goes', including mid-sentence in 'The report says there is a problem'), 'here'/'where' before a verb ('here is/are', 'where is/are/did'), a locative 'there' after a verb in a short text that does not open with 'The' ('He ran there', 'She lives there now'; 8 of 10 measured), the second piece of a rare split word or a non-Latin byte ('Mc|Av|oy', 'Mac|mill|an', 'Sch|ir|mer', 'McL|ach|lan', bark|ed, cheer|ed, сп|ос, Thai/Japanese/Cyrillic pieces), a numeral ('3.50', 'Line 12', '42 years'), or one of a short mid set — 'all' before a noun ('all day', 'all week', 'all the way'), 'up'/'down' after a verb, 'last' before a noun, and 'not' after do/did/does/could/can. It stays at 0.00-0.20x max wherever every position's sharpest attention falls only on 'the'/'The', ',', '.', 'is'/'was'/'are', 'in'/'to'/'at'/'for'/'with' or the text's first token, which is the standard state of one-word texts (0 of 10 measured) and of short (2-8 token) 'The'-opened declaratives with no prepositional phrase, split piece, numeral, 'there/here/where' or mid-set word (0 of 39 measured, median 0.03x max); ordinary prose of 10+ tokens straddles the line (median peak exactly at 0.25x max, 15 of 30 sentences active) and a verb followed by 'on the' also sits on it (7 of 20 active), so neither of those is a reliable negative.
