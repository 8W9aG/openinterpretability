## Hypothesis

> The pole reads the token at the current position and fires on lexical content words - nouns, verbs, adjectives and adverbs, including the word-initial sub-token of such a word in non-English text - while staying near zero on function words, pronouns, punctuation, digits, whitespace and code operators (each under 0.5% of its scan tokens). The strength is graded by the word rather than binary: it is reliably above the 0.25*max activity line on specific content words of written prose (view, views, viewing, reading, gazing, experiencing, teaching, thought, tour, holiday, outbreak, literacy, uncertainty, 1.0-1.6) and below it on short high-frequency generic words (tree, money, car, book, hand, face, make, see, take, think); the immediately preceding token only scales the magnitude (up to ~3x, higher after a reporting verb such as 'said', lower after a preposition such as 'of'/'on') and never switches firing on or off.
