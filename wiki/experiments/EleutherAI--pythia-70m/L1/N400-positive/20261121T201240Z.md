## Hypothesis

> L1/N400+ is a description-attribute detector: it fires (0.4-2.4) on the open-class word that carries a perceptual or measurable property of a concrete thing - colour words (green, gray, purple, brown, yellow, blue), position/orientation words (side, left, centre, corner, bottom, front, top) and duration/unit words (hours, minutes, days, degrees, '°', teaspoon) - plus the part/quantity/equipment nouns such properties attach to (pieces, sides, bodies, elements, panels, rack, bowl, sheet, line); it stays at about 0 on every determiner and other function word (0 of 8706 determiners in the scan are active), on abstract relational nouns (idea, problem, policy, company, value, cost, price), and on verbs. Within that class the strength is set by the current token together with the one before it: it peaks when the word completes a two-part unit begun by the previous token - the head of a modifier+head compound ('right side' 1.9 vs 'the side' 1.0, 'water quality' 1.5 vs 'the quality' 0.04, 'juice box' 1.55 vs 'box' 0.02) or a BPE continuation after a short first fragment (' b'|'ays' 2.1 vs ' bay' 1.3, ' P'|'INK', ' pip'|'ing', mid-word CJK/kana characters) - and falls to or below its baseline when a determiner or adverb occupies that slot ('other side' 0.6, 'very green' 0.65).
