## Hypothesis

> N1548+ is a two-token detector: Pythia's layer-1 MLP reads the residual before layer-1 attention (parallel residual, so zeroing layer-1 attention leaves its input bit-identical), meaning the pole sums a term for the current token with a term that layer-0 attention passes down from the preceding token, and it fires when that sum clears threshold. The current-token term is high for pieces that continue a longer word or a non-lexical string ('rog' in Ostrogoths/petroglyphs, 'bb' in pebbles, 'umber' in plumber, 'mm' in 9mm, '</', 'bib', a Devanagari vowel sign) and for a fixed set of content tokens ('offer', 'account', 'mind', 'arm', 'thumb', 'mate', 'Biden'); the preceding-token term is large after possessive pronouns and physical action verbs ('his arm' 1.87 vs 'the arm' 0.76, 'my mind' 1.47 vs 'the mind' 0.65, 'picked up' 1.60 vs 'hooked up' 0.51, 'ifer' 2.14 after 'con' vs 0.51 after 'aqu') and near zero after determiners, prepositions and conjunctions, so the same content word after 'the/a/of/with', reporting verbs ('said', 'asked', 'replied') and institutional or kinship nouns ('company', 'government', 'mother') stay inactive.
