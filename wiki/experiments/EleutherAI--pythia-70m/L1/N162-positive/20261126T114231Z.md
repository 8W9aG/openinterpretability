## Hypothesis

> Fires retrospectively on a mid-sentence token with a leading space that is title-case (initial capital) and whose lowercase form is an ordinary English word: reliably for high-frequency function words (The, And, That, Of, In, To, An) and typically for common content words (Night, Factor, Stars, Future, One, Come), including when the token is only the first subword fragment of a longer word (That-cher, In-glis, One-india).
