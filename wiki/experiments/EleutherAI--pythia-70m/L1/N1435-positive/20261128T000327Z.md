## Hypothesis

> A previous-token-gated bigram detector. It fires most strongly on an ASCII hyphen '-' placed directly after a number or alphanumeric code (page and age ranges like 449-456 or 6-10, years 2010-2015, phone numbers, DOIs, ISBNs, SARS-CoV-2), on 'abs' as a function name (Math.abs, fabsf), on the uppercase acronym ' UP', and on ' up' or ' of' right after a verb like 'given', 'made' or 'wrapped' ('given up', 'made of', 'made up'). It is weak or silent on the same tokens elsewhere: a hyphen inside an ordinary word ('well-known'), an en dash '–', ' up' after motion verbs ('looked up', 'went up', 'set up'), and ' of' after ordinary nouns ('cup of').
