## Hypothesis

> This pole fires when the current subword token continues a word begun in the preceding token(s) — a non-word-initial piece extending a stem — peaking on short 2-3 character continuations such as 'or' (Dec/or/ative, esc/rit/or, se/or/ang, port/ac/or, Cur/ve). The same letters starting a new word (with a leading space, e.g. word-initial 'or' or 've') do not activate it. It reacts to the just-read stem-plus-continuation (retrospective), not to any upcoming token; whole-word single tokens with the same letters (e.g. lowercase 'interests', 'are') are predicted inactive, with capitalized long whole-words like 'Interests' left as acknowledged exceptions.
