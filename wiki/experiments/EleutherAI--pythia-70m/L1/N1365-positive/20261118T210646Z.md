## Hypothesis

> Fires on an abstract quantity, time-unit or manner noun (ways, time, point, moment, seconds, minutes, weeks, months, terms, areas, million, billion) when it heads a quantified or bare prepositional phrase, i.e. right after a quantifier, number or preposition such as 'a couple of', 'a lot of', 'a few', 'many', 'several', 'any', 'three', 'in', 'for' ('a couple of ways', 'at any time', 'in terms of', 'a few seconds', 'three billion').
