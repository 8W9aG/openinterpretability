## Hypothesis

> This negative pole is the GELU-floor half of one MLP unit (strength = -gelu(pre-activation), capped at 0.170), and it saturates on nominal function words: the definite article, possessive determiners, quantifiers/partitives and object pronouns (the, my, his, her, its, their, our, your, some, any, both, each, many, every, one, other, multiple, them, him, me, us), which are active on 97% of their occurrences (mean 0.137 = 0.8 of max) against a 38% base rate, with the indefinite article and demonstratives only partly activating ('a' 0.089, 'this' 0.062). It is at zero on predication and on plain content: verbs and auxiliaries (need, get, would, should, have, could, do, see, has, had, will, make) are active on only 22% of occurrences (mean 0.028), and that is where the positive pole fires and promotes a verb set (could, might, is, needs, answer, happens), while nouns, adjectives and most prepositions (box, blue, of, to, in, on, with) stay near zero.
