## Hypothesis

> The pole fires on clause seams: separator marks that divide or close a clause (a comma before a new item or relative clause, a period that repeats an earlier period in the context, semicolons, closing quotes/brackets, dashes and hyphens) and the function words that open the new clause or coordinated item after such a mark ('the', 'which', 'But', 'They', 'It'). It is silent inside a continuous phrase (nouns, verbs, adjectives, numerals, prepositions, determiners, 'of'), and it also reads near zero on a passage's first period and on bare 'and'/'or', because the pole strength is a bump of the pre-activation, maximal at GELU's floor of 0.170, and falls back to zero once the pre-activation is driven past that minimum.
