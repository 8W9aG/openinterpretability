## Hypothesis

> L3/N981+ fires on closed-class 'linker' tokens that open a new phrase or clause inside running prose: prepositions (to, in, of, at, with, into, from, about), coordinators (and, or), subordinators and wh-words (if, how, who, which, what), do-support/modals (would, could, has, do), second/first person pronouns (you, I) and opening punctuation or '?' ('(', '\u201c', '?'), with its strength rising when that linker opens a phrase inside a longer multi-clause sentence. It is near-silent on the closed-class tokens that close or refer back -- the definite article, anaphoric third-person pronouns and possessives (the, it, them, their, its, this), copulas (is/are/was/were), ')' and '\u201d' -- and on the content words those phrases introduce, and on grammaticalised uses of the same word (infinitive 'to' after want/need/have/used stays at ~0.0-0.07 while the 'to' of 'from X to Y' or 'back to' reaches 1.5-1.8).
