## Hypothesis

> Delimited-span head: it is active on the content tokens inside a span opened by a quotation mark (" ' “ « ‘), a markdown italic/emphasis marker (_ or *), a parenthesis or a square bracket, attending back to the opening mark. It is strongest on the first one to three word tokens after the opener (often 2-3.5 in short probes, up to 5.1 in the scan) and decays further into the span. The opener itself and the closing mark sit below or near the line, and so does the text after the close (except after ] where it can stay active). Plain prose with none of these delimiters is predicted inactive: 45 of 45 varied 6-16-token sentences without quotes, parentheses, brackets, underscores or asterisks peaked at 0.78 or less (line 1.28). Apostrophes in contractions or possessives (don't, it's, patient's) do not open a span. Code argument lists and ALL-CAPS licence text can reach about 1.1-1.7, so they are not an inactive class.
