## Hypothesis

> Pole N2014- is the trough readout of a near-default-on layer-3 unit: its strength is -GELU(pre-activation), so it sits at the 0.17 ceiling whenever the pre-activation lies near GELU's minimum (about -0.75, where a text's first token sits; the band -0.9 to -0.6 gives 0.164 or more) and stays above its 0.042 active line across the whole trough from about -2.0 to -0.09, which is exactly where ordinary content words land - hence any text of a dozen or more words that contains ordinary content words (' road', ' price', ' tree', ' rain', ' room', ' 1953') peaks at or near the ceiling, in every language and script, in code, and in prose made only of leading-space tokens.
