## Hypothesis

> Pole N2014- is not a feature detector but the trough (negative) pole of a GELU unit: its strength is exactly -GELU(z_t), where z_t = w·LN2(h_t) is a fixed direction in the layer-3 MLP input, so it fires at a token iff z_t lies in GELU's trough band (-2.04, -0.09) and reads 0 everywhere else; every text's first token lands in that band (the position-0 residual is token-independent: z = -0.62 +/- 0.15 over 200 random vocabulary tokens, range -1.71 to -0.21, and 98.9% of the scan's window-first tokens fire), and at later tokens z_t is 72% context -- token identity explains only 28% of its variance and position 1% -- because at layer 3 seven of the eight heads put 0.5-1.0 of their weight on one anchor position (position 0, or the most recent newline or full stop) at every later token, so a later token's value is the anchor's trough state plus that token's own offset. Measured over its 200k-token scan 44.9% of tokens fire, the below-line tokens are spread across every type-frequency bucket (types firing <=5% of the time supply only 10% of them; of 1239 types with >=20 occurrences only 9 never fire and only 6 fire >=95%), and no word, syntax or language class keeps a 10-14-token prompt quiet -- 400/400 natural sentences and every reviewer holdout peak at 0.15-0.17, with the peak position spread evenly over all positions -- so a prompt falls below the 0.042 line only as an artificial all-function-word string whose opening already lands deep ('it was not the one that they had been in' 0.010, 'it has not been the of and a in' 0.014, 'of the of the of the of the of' 0.009), and even that class fires about a third of the time when a later ' has'/' were'/' is' returns to the band.
