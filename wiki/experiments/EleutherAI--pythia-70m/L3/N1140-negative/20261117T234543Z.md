## Hypothesis

> The negative pole of L3/N1140 is a diffuse closed-class detector: it fires when the current token is a grammatical function token - a determiner, pronoun, auxiliary or copula, preposition or particle, conjunction, possessive 's, punctuation mark, or a code whitespace/indentation run - and stays near zero on open-class content words, word-medial letters and mid-word fragments. It is a dial rather than a selective detector: about 40% of scan tokens and nearly every ordinary English sentence clear the activity line, and closed-class-free word lists still fire about 40% of the time, so a text is predicted inactive only when it is a short punctuation-free span of open-class words.
