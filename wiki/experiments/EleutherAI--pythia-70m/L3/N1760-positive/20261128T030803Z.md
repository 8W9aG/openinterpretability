## Hypothesis

> Mostly a lexical detector, firing on the current token with little help from context. It fires on kinship nouns, female ones most strongly (niece, mother, wife, mom, daughter, sister, uncle: about 0.5-0.75 of max; father, son, husband, dad, friend: about 0.3-0.4; non-kin roles like lawyer or boss and objects like house or office stay near zero). It also fires on ' and' when it coordinates a personal-pronoun subject ('He and', 'they and', 'You and': about 0.5-0.6 of max, versus about 0.25 after a proper name and about 0 in 'apples and pears'), and on some medical/anatomical subwords and words ('omet' in myometrial, 'umat' in rheumatoid, ' endometrial', ' breast'). A preceding possessive gives a modest boost (his wife > the wife), but no possessive is needed.
