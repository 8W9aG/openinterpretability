## Hypothesis

> Pole N541+ fires on grammatical/functional material rather than lexical heads: predicted active are determiners and articles (the, a, an, my, its, uma, una, los, Les, i, een, deze, hun), degree adverbs (nearly, completely, rather, extraordinarily) and copula 'be' (is, are, be, been, was, sono, sont, è), while English content-word heads (nouns, lexical verbs) are predicted inactive at ~0, as are English conjunctions, prepositions, infinitival 'to' and modals (has, will, would, may, can).
