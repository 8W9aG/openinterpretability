## Hypothesis

> The pole goes quiet inside an instrumental 'use' frame: on the verb use/uses/used/using/utilized/utilizing (not the noun 'use', as in 'the use of', and not habitual 'used to') and on the next few tokens of the object or instrument phrase after it ('the hammer', 'a knife'), and also on the article after a sentence-initial 'Using'/'Utilizing'. It is on almost everywhere else, so a whole text is quiet only when every token after the first is in that frame, e.g. 'I used the hammer', 'He uses a knife', 'They use the tool', 'Using a spoon'. The silence fades about four to six tokens after the use verb, and 'used to' brings the pole straight back on.

- **Activating tokens:** ` use`, ` uses`, ` used`, ` using`, ` utilized`, ` a`, ` the`, ` hammer`, ` knife`, ` tool`
- **Mechanism claimed:** retrospective
