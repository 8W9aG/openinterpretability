## Hypothesis

> A sink-parked head: on fluent English prose it attends to the first token and stays below the line (predict ordinary English sentences inactive or weak, under ~0.25 of max). It fires when it leaves the sink for hard attention to the current or previous token. This happens once a text has built up a run of fragmented, low-frequency tokenization: digit groups and separators (-, :, .) of phone numbers, clock times and decimals, subword pieces of non-English words (Swahili, Indonesian, Polish), or terse code. It is strongest on the second and later items of such a run, for example the dashes of a second phone number or later Swahili subwords in a sentence, and weak on the first number or first foreign word in a text.
