## Hypothesis

> A dense, context-accumulating word-content dial: once a few words of natural-language text have been read, it fires on nearly every non-final token, and it grows with how many varied abstract, academic or clinical content words the preceding window holds (study, patients, results, consisted, evaluated, inhibited, antibody, mechanisms). Word order barely matters, so a jumbled or comma-separated list of such terms also fires. It is near zero on the first token, on sentence-final periods, on fragments under about six tokens, and on streams of ordered or repeated closed-class items: counting words or numerals, dates, days of the week, months, letters of the alphabet, one word repeated, import lines and bracket runs. Plain concrete narrative about children, food, toys or animals fires more weakly than academic text of the same length, but still crosses a quarter of max once a sentence runs past about a dozen words.
