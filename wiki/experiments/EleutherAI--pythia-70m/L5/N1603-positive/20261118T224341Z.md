## Hypothesis

> A topic dial for English prose about people and their lives (news, biography, entertainment, family, personal anecdote). Inside such prose it fires on the word that opens or links the next noun phrase: 'a'/'the', numerals ('two', 'three'), quantifiers ('several'), 'by' after 'written'/'followed', 'to' after 'married', 'and' between two people's names. It also fires on person-role words ('former', 'president', 'spokesman', 'wife', 'daughter', 'debut', 'born'). The same function words in code, software or technical documentation, legal clauses and engineering or product descriptions stay well below the active line (about 0.3-0.6 of 3.1). The quantifiers 'a few' and 'a couple' fire in any prose. General non-human descriptive prose (weather, geography, markets) is only weakly to moderately active, and math text is uneven and not claimed.
