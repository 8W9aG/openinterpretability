## Hypothesis

> Pole N111+ fires when the model reaches the first sentence or line terminator of the text it is reading: the first '.' or '\n' token in the input, at any position and whether or not it ends a sentence ('Mr.' and the point of '3.5' count the same), and on every '<|endoftext|>' document separator (~2.0); the second and later '.'/'\n', and every '?', '!', ';', ':' and ',', stay near zero. These positions share one internal state (the layer-5 MLP input at the first period is cosine 0.95 with position 0 and 0.93 with EOS, versus 0.50 with a second period), so the pole is an opening/boundary-state detector: it is near zero on ordinary narrative prose, dialogue and code, with a weaker second mode that lifts scattered tokens to ~0.5-1.5 inside long technical or encyclopedic passages.
