## Hypothesis

> L5/H7 is a committed-continuation head: it attends one-hot to the current token or the one just before it and writes a single fixed direction into the residual whose size tracks how strongly that position's context has already pinned the next token, so its peaks (4-10 of the 10.4 max) sit inside memorized near-verbatim strings - the standard copyright/licence notices ('except in compliance with the License', 'regarding copyright ownership', 'you should have received a copy'), old-HTML tag, attribute and page-furniture runs (VALIGN="top", BGCOLOR=", NavBarFont1, classes_navbar, skip-navigation, help-doc) - and the smaller peaks land on the pieces of such runs.
