## Hypothesis

> A dense, token-local dial for English words outside natural-science, mathematical and medical/lab vocabulary. Each token's strength depends on its last few words. Runs about people, homes and property, customers and users, landlords and tenants, courts, sports, weather damage or plain narrative pass a quarter of max on most sentences of 8+ words. Lists of everyday words also do. It peaks (half of max or more) on linking words: ' without', ' with', ' on', ' unless', sentence-opening ' On'/' As', 'who[ want]'/'[ need]'. It is strongest around 'homeowners', 'owners', 'tenants', 'users'. A science, maths, medicine or lab-method term (enzyme, ligand, eigenvalue, renal, incubated, integral) drops it to about 0 for the next few tokens. So a sentence whose clauses are all such jargon stays below a quarter of max. So do digit strings and short code, while non-English text stays near or under a quarter of max. A mixed sentence peaks at its everyday stretch ('We had a big ...', 'who want'), and that stretch can pass a quarter of max even inside STEM text.
