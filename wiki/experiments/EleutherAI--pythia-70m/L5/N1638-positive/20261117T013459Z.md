## Hypothesis

> Fires on the content tokens of a semicolon-terminated statement that is the second or later statement of a contiguous statement run - the final subword of an identifier (height, Config, fraction, Msg, Url, b), a numeric or literal value (0, 30, none, YES) - but not on the `;` itself, and only when a `;`-terminated statement precedes it by a line or two. It is at floor on a block's first statement, on newline-terminated Python-style code, on comma-separated lists and on prose with no recent semicolon, and it rises with each additional preceding `;`-statement, saturating by about three; its output holds the statement open (lowers `;`, `);`, `";`) and favours newline-plus-indentation whitespace.
