## Hypothesis

> This pole reads a completed word: it is high on a token that finishes a lexical word (a whole-word token such as ' one', ' important', ' consists', ' believed', ' easy', or the last piece of a multi-token word) and near zero on a token that is continued inside the same word (the first or middle piece of a split word, e.g. 'eng' and 'ag' at 0.0 in 'engagements', which is why Polish, Italian and Tamil text, whose words are cut into such fragments, leaves it at 0.00-0.01).
