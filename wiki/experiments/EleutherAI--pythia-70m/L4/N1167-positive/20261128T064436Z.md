## Hypothesis

> A context-depth dial that is mostly blind to content: the pole stays weak (under about 0.15 of max) on the first ~10 tokens of any text, and from about the 20th token it goes above a quarter of max on ordinary tokens of any kind (English prose, code, numbers, non-Latin script), so almost any probe of 20+ tokens is predicted active and almost any probe under ~10 tokens inactive.
