## Hypothesis

> A retrospective lexical dial whose firings add up with text length. It peaks (about 1.3-3.0) on identity words after a determiner or verb ('the same', 'identical', 'Taken together'), on non-initial pronoun or complementizer 'that' ('I know that', 'said that', 'Is that true?') and on numerals of two or more digits giving a count, year, label or range end ('scored 23', 'born in 1921', '2002-2015', '102 e 103'). It reaches about 0.8-1.6 on research-report nouns (results, study, levels, researchers), weekday names and scattered mid-frequency words, so most texts longer than about 15 tokens (expository or news prose, non-English text, markup or code with literals) cross 0.8 somewhere. It stays below about 0.75 (mostly the 0.47 floor on '.') on short sentences under about 10 tokens that describe everyday concrete scenes in common words ('The cat sat on the mat', 'She drank a glass of water') with none of those triggers, including single-digit numerals ('3 apples'), spelled-out small numbers and sentence-initial 'That'.
