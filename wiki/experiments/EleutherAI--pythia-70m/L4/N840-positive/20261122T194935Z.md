## Hypothesis

> A retrospective lexical dial for value, identity and report content. It peaks (about 1.5-3.2) on 'same' after a determiner or 'are', on pronoun or complementizer 'that' after a verb or conjunction, on the second word of fixed technical terms ('standard deviation', 'blues-derived', 'Taken together') and on numerals of two or more digits giving a year, count, label or range end ('born in 1921', '2002-2015', 'scored 23'). It reaches about 0.8-1.5 on report, measurement and cognition words (results, report, data, study, evidence, said, thought, claimed, measured, calculated), on perception and arrival verbs ('looked', 'came', 'went home'), on time words (day, tomorrow), 'back', 'rain', 'letter', on non-English prose, and on code or list markup with digits ('range(0, 5)', '<li>'). It stays below about 0.75 (the 0.47 floor on '.') on English narration of people or animals handling concrete objects or moving through places, built from verbs like opened, sat, chased, pushed, carried, fed, poured, dropped, tied, threw and nouns like door, box, cat, yard, cart, spoon, coat, bowl, with none of the listed words; about one in five such sentences still crosses on one odd token.
