## Hypothesis

It is at or near zero on nouns and proper nouns (all 8 nouns in 'They announced a ___ decision.' measured exactly 0.000, and the scan's confound profile has NOUN 0.5% and PROPN 0.7% active against ADJ 13.2%), on base-form and finite verbs ('the town limit/divide/measure/license/combine the policy' 0.00), on adverbs ('spoke honestly/quickly' 0.00, 'widely' 0.13, 'beautifully' 0.32), and on relational/classifying adjectives that file the noun under a category instead of describing it ('legal', 'political', 'national', 'financial', 'social', 'cultural', 'annual', 'rural', 'medical', 'judicial', 'nuclear', 'digital' 0.00-0.30; only 1 of 38 over the active line in a designed battery and 1 of 16 in natural collocations, the exceptions being 'verbal' 0.60 and 'previous' 0.67, with 'technical' 0.47 just under the line).
