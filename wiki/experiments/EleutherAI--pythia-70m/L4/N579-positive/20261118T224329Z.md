## Hypothesis

> A register dial for chatty first/second-person web text (personal messages, greetings, thanks, reviews, forum posts, e.g. 'I hope you are doing well.', 'Let me know what works for you.'): most words in such text are active, and the peak (0.6-0.95 of max) falls on intensifiers and praise words ('very', 'really', 'highly', 'incredibly', 'great', 'Just wanted'); counting and frequency adverbs ('twice', 'for an hour', 'at all', 'still ... there') also give moderate activity (0.3-0.6 of max) in otherwise neutral sentences. Third-person past-tense narration of a person or animal acting physically on a concrete object or place ('The dog buried the bone in the garden.', 'The cat knocked the vase off the shelf.') and passive wet-lab method sentences ('The DNA was extracted and amplified by PCR.') stay inactive (about 0.07 of max, peaking on the final period) when they contain no intensifier, evaluation, 'you'/'I', number, frequency or duration word, and no 'gave/sent/carried X to <someone>' transfer.
