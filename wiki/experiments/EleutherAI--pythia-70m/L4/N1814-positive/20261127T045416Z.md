## Hypothesis

> L4/N1814+ reacts to the onset of a low-frequency or unfamiliar word: it peaks on the first token of technical/scientific terms, code identifiers, proper names, foreign words and nonce words, most reliably when the tokenizer splits the word into several pieces (innervation -> ' inn'+'ervation', fracking -> ' fr'+'acking', flimbex -> ' fl'+'imb'+'ex'). It also peaks on possessive determiners in prepositional frames ('harmful to your ...'), and its output direction boosts word-internal continuation pieces such as 'ervation', 'ology' and 'acking', while it stays quiet on common whole words and on the continuation pieces themselves.
