## Hypothesis

> A broad head whose activity grows with how much text it has read: on the first ~8 tokens of plain prose it is silent (attention parked on the first token), but past ~40 tokens of running text of any kind it fires weakly to moderately (0.45-1.0) on scattered tokens while attending ~1.0 to one earlier content word, so any passage of three or more sentences or 40+ tokens is predicted active; even in short texts it fires (0.5-1.3) at the second member of a series - the 'and'/'or'/'nor'/'until'/'than' or comma right after a first conjunct noun ('the cats and dogs', 'between noon and midnight', 'iron, copper, zinc') - and on code, markup and JSON rows ('x = a + b; y = a - b;', '<li>One</li><li>Two</li>'), and it is strongest (1.2-1.8) deep in long documents on lists of names, titles, citations and code. Predicted inactive: plain prose of up to about 25 tokens (one or two simple sentences, short questions, headings) that has no comma, no 'and'/'or'/'nor'/'but', no 'than', no list, no code symbols and no repeated word; about 1 in 15 such texts crosses weakly at a stray word.
