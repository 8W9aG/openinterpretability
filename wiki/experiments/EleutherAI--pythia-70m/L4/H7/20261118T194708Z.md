## Hypothesis

> A sequence-continuation head that is silent (strength ~0, attention parked on the text's first token) on ordinary prose. It fires (0.5-1.8) only when the current token continues something already in context, attending ~1.0 to one earlier token: (1) inside the second copy of a repeated phrase, line or name of two or more tokens (from the copy's second token on, attending to the token after the earlier copy), and (2) at the separator that starts the next member of a list, table row or range - commas, 'and', '&', ';', '||', a newline between three or more parallel items or rows, or 'to' in 'from X to Y' - attending to the previous item. Plain sentences with no repeated phrase and no list are predicted inactive (about one in four crosses weakly at a stray content word). Also inactive: a single word repeated with different neighbours ('the bank' ... 'river bank'), two-item pairs such as 'tea and coffee', comparatives with 'than', and space-separated word lists.
