## Hypothesis

> Fires where a sentence stops merely mentioning something and starts characterising it: the slot just before a predicate nominal after a copula or copula-like verb ('the film was a disaster', 'the memo was a clear example', 'This paper offers an analysis', 'counted as a clear sign'), after an as/like construal ('widely seen as a turning point', 'read him like an open scroll', 'more than just a marker'), or after a minimiser that reduces a subject to a bare category ('merely', 'just', 'only', 'simply', 'nothing but', 'no more than'). It does not care which noun fills the slot (swapping 'example' for 'photo', 'table', 'disaster' or 'evidence' leaves the peak unchanged) and the same noun phrase in subject or object position is nearly silent, so the mechanism is retrospective: it reads the ascriptive frame already built by the preceding words, while its Jacobian lens and ablation read out the abstract characterisation nouns that typically fill such a slot (evidence, example, indication, acknowledgment, manifestation, testimony) rather than one specific upcoming token.

- **Activating tokens:** `mere`, `merely`, `just`, `only`, `simply`, `nothing but`, `as`, `like`, `than`, `a`, `some`, `was`, `is`, `provides`, `offers`
- **Mechanism claimed:** retrospective
