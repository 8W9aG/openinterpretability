## Hypothesis

> Fires on a numeral that states an amount or count of something (money, patients, percent, viewers, copies), and also on the bounding or approximating word just before it ('just', 'only', 'nearly', 'almost', 'more than $'). It is strongest when such a word precedes the numeral ('more than $3 billion', 'just 41 percent'), weaker on a bare quantity ('3 people', '42 cats'), and near zero on numerals that are labels or points in time: years after 'in'/'born in', page/figure/table/chapter/version/jersey/channel numbers, street numbers, clock times. Caveat: in very short texts a sentence-final period also reaches about 0.45 of max, so probes should leave the final period out.
