> The pole fires retrospectively on familiar, high-frequency words of edited expository and literary prose: academic verbs and adjectives (investigate, ethical, causative), abstract nouns (diagnosis, treatment, future), possessive determiners (his, their, my), and proper-name or subword completions (Hector, abol-ish).
