## Hypothesis

> N418's negative pole is the neuron's GELU floor: strength = -GELU(z) is maximal (0.170, exactly |min GELU|) when the pre-activation z sits at the GELU minimum (about -0.75) and falls away on either side, so the pole reports how close z is to -0.75 rather than how hard the neuron is driven.
