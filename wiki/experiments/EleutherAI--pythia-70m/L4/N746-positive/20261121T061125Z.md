## Hypothesis

> N746+ fires on the preposition token that opens a circumstantial setting phrase, a point or span of time, place or state, overwhelmingly on the token ' At'/' at' (the largest share of its firings) and also on ' on' in dates and times and on ' by'/' until'/' around'/' near'/' between', while ' in', ' with', ' of', ' to' and ' for' in the same slots leave it at baseline.
