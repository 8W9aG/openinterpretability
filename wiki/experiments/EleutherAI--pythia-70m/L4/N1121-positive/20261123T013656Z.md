## Hypothesis

> A dense dial over English narrative prose. It peaks (0.5-1.0 of max) on a count or rank word in the slot before a noun: a digit, number word or ordinal ('has 4 brothers', 'owns 40 paintings', 'bought 5 apples', 'No. 3', 'third place', 'the second attempt'; not 'first', 'last' or 'final'), and on the partitive 'one of the'. It sits in a 0.3-0.65 band on: the first sentence-ending full stop of a text; a posture or drift verb and the place preposition after it ('lay on', 'hid under', 'drifted toward', 'the wind blew'); 'was'/'were'/'is' just after an opening pronoun or 'There'; a quantifier, demonstrative or colour/rarity adjective before a plural noun ('several', 'some', 'those', 'brown cows', 'rare paintings'); and most multi-clause narration over about 12 tokens. It stays below 0.25 on short unpunctuated clauses without these: copular sentences with a full-noun subject ('The movie was really good'), clauses whose object is bare or takes 'the'/'a'/'no' ('She bought the apples', 'John has no brothers'), questions, imperatives, greetings, 'I think' opinions, German or French text, and import lines.
