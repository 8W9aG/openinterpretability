## Hypothesis

> Fires on a number token that gives a count or rank in running prose: a small cardinal digit or number word ('3', 'three', 'No. 3', '3 games') or an ordinal adjective ('third', 'fourth', 'sixth'). A weaker secondary trigger is the partitive ' of'/' the' after 'one'/'several' ('one of the') in long narrative contexts. A sentence-final '.' gives a flat floor of about 0.45 of max, so it does not count as a trigger.
