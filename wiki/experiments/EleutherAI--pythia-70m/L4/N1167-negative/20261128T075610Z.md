## Hypothesis

> A shallow GELU-dip pole (ceiling 0.17, the GELU minimum) that, in plain English prose, fires on a dynamic lexical main verb after a full noun-phrase subject (gives, provides, moved, lost, opened, offered, saw) and on the function word right after such a verb or a passive participle, above all the head of a complex preposition ('closed due to', 'power prior to', 'owing to', 'ahead of').
