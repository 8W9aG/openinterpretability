## Hypothesis

> This pole fires on the token that names a thing or a quantity: bare numerals are its strongest class (digit tokens such as ' 7', ' 3', ' 0', '083' and ' 103' are 6-9x over-represented among its top activations and are spelled-out-proof, e.g. ' 7' 1.46 vs ' seven' 0.35), and content nouns heading a referring phrase (' details', ' features', ' fields', ' value', ' stream', ' fluids') fire reliably (19/20 nouns above 0.49 in a fixed frame), while verbs (0/20 above 0.16), adjectives, pronouns other than ' this'/' it'/' which'/' that', determiners, prepositions and word-internal fragments sit at the neuron's off floor. The response is mostly a property of the token itself (token identity explains ~60% of its variance and the nominal reading of a word beats its verbal reading, ' value' 0.74 vs 0.12, ' stream' 1.60 vs 1.32), with only a weak preference for word-final over mid-word positions, so it reads the token already present rather than preparing an upcoming one.
