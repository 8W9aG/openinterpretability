## Hypothesis

> This pole sits at the GELU trough, so it saturates near 0.17 on any token where the neuron's pre-activation is mildly negative. In English that happens on abstract, non-physical content: abstract or technical nouns ('idea', 'information', 'discussion', 'theory', 'calibration', 'data'), mental verbs ('thought about'), adjective or participle predicates after was/were ('was sad', 'were angry', 'were analyzed'), and licence or academic boilerplate. It also fires on most tokens of non-English text, especially non-Latin scripts. It stays below the line on short concrete physical-event clauses, which are built from concrete objects or places, motion and location prepositions, dates and counts ('He opened the door.', 'She went to the store in May.', 'He finished fourth in the race.'). There the neuron is positive. It is also silent on sentence-initial words, '.' and newlines.
