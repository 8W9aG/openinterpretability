## Hypothesis

> The pole fires when the current token belongs to formal, technical, academic, literary, or institutional vocabulary: abstract nominals (liberation, training, knowledge, education), domain adjectives (protective, social), technical terms (homolog, coefficients, habilitation), and structured strings such as codes and figures inside formal passages, with graded strength by domain specificity. It stays near zero on everyday concrete nouns (dog, market, car), highest-frequency function words (in, of, as, to), and punctuation, whitespace, and brackets; the response is retrospective, driven by the current token with decaying carryover onto the next one or two tokens, and its output direction suppresses whitespace and line breaks, i.e. it expects continued substantive content rather than a structural break.
