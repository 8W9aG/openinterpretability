## Hypothesis

> The pole fires when the current token is an ordinary clause-linking or content word: subordinating conjunctions (Because/Since/Although/When/After), discourse connectives (However/Therefore/sentence-initial By), pronouns (They/you), and common nouns, verbs and numbers (water, contrast, results, estimated, folks, ten), reaching its -0.17 GELU-dip maximum there. It goes quiet both for degree/manner modifiers, quantifiers and negators (completely, very, quickly, always, often excepted, not, still), which instead drive this neuron's positive pole, and for pure grammatical glue and punctuation (the, of, to, lowercase by, Without, .); a few concrete nouns (dog, ball, bread) idiosyncratically pattern with the modifiers rather than the lexical band.
