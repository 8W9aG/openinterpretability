## Hypothesis

> This Layer-0 negative pole is a token-identity (unigram) detector that saturates at its GELU floor (~0.17) whenever the current token belongs to a large fixed vocabulary subset (e.g. ' world', ' strong', ' John', ' advocates', ' transmitting', the continuation piece 'lying', '.', ';', ' when'), falls near zero on high-frequency function words and light punctuation (' the', ' and', ' in', ' ,', '('), and switches off entirely (the neuron goes positive) on tokens such as ' which' and ' who'.
