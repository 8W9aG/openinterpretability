## Hypothesis

> A context-independent token detector for small-to-medium cardinal numbers written as a word-initial token: space-prefixed digit tokens fire strongest for two-digit values (' 19', ' 58', ' 65', ' 23' near max; ' 10'-' 99' about 0.6-1.0 x max), slightly less for three-digit values (' 302', ' 500' about 0.6-0.8 x max), less for single digits (' 7' about 0.8, ' 1' about 0.5, ' 0' about 0.3 x max), and spelled number words (' seven', ' twelve', ' twenty', ' ten') fire at about 0.6-0.8 x max. It is near silent on year-like four-digit numbers (' 1985', ' 1990', ' 2010'), on digit tokens without a leading space (the '122' in '19122', '41'), on Roman numerals and on non-numeric words.
