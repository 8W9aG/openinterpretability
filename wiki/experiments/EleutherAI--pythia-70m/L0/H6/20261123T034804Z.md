## Hypothesis

> Broad early-position head: predict almost any text active-weak (about 0.25-0.5 of max), because its first ~7 tokens after the opening one (ordinary prose, code, HTML, a short sentence) cross the line, while tokens deeper than ~10 positions are weak. It is strong (about 0.45-1.0 of max) on the line break(s) that end a short opening line of 1-5 tokens, such as a title, name or header ('Carl Nielsen\n\n', 'package config\n\n', 'x = 1\n'). Texts that are only a comma-separated list of plain numbers ('3, 7, 12, 45') stay inactive (below 0.2 of max), as does replacing the title's line break with a comma, period or semicolon (which drops it back to the weak band).
