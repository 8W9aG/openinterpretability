## Hypothesis

> This pole fires when the current token is a lexical content token (open-class words such as nouns, verbs, adjectives and adverbs, numbers, proper names, and non-initial word pieces like -ise/-ats), and stays near-silent on punctuation, whitespace, and closed-class function tokens (determiners, prepositions, the copula, conjunctions).
