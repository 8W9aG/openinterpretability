## Hypothesis

> A context-free token detector for written forms of the verb "be": it fires on the token itself whenever that token is ' is' or ' was' (near max, ~3.78, even when ' is' is only the first piece of a word such as 'isometric'), a little less on ' were'/' are' (~0.87 of max), and about half to two-thirds of max on ' been', ' be', ' am', ' being', the stems ' isn'/' wasn'/' aren'/' weren' of negated contractions, sentence-initial 'Is'/'Was', and the clitic "'re"/"'m".
