## Hypothesis

> A layer-0, context-independent token detector for plural nouns: it fires on any single token that is a regular -s/-es/-ies plural noun (strongest, about 0.8-1.0 of max, on space-prefixed lowercase count-noun plurals such as projects, venues, apps, outlets, centres, schools, tasks, buildings; about 0.7-0.9 on most other regular plurals like dogs, cars, files, rooms).
