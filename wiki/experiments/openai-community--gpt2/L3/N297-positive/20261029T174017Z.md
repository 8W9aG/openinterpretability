## Hypothesis

> The pole fires where a text pins down what something is - its name, its kind or class, the content of a claim, or a measured value - and peaks on the linker that carries that content: ' of' after a quantifier or a kind/representation/measure noun ('one of', 'a couple of', 'the name/type/kind/sort/form/example/instance/case/pattern/image/description/definition/concept/idea/sense/sound/species/category/incidence/prevalence/frequency/proportion/number/presence/evidence of X') and ' that' after an assertion or evidence noun or verb ('the news/evidence/idea that', 'found/indicate that'), with the same job on the separators inside a written value ('3 x 3', 'n=123', '2,900'); it also fires, more weakly, on the verbs that perform that move (identified, named, known as, classified as, attributed, diagnosed, detected, found, seen, recorded, listed). It stays at baseline when the same linker follows a first-order noun - an object, part, possessor or event ('the leg/roof/head/owner/cost/price/value/result/purpose/rule/loss/weight of X') - and on ordinary narrative prose, so it tracks the specification construction, not the bare token ' of' and not the trigger-token list.
