## Hypothesis

> A diffuse head whose output grows the more a token's attention leaves the text's first token. That happens most strongly on ' All' (0.6-1.0 of max) after an earlier ' Copyright'/' ©', and more mildly (0.25-0.55) on the rest of that notice after the Copyright word (company name, ',', ' Inc', '.', ' Rights'/' rights'). Elsewhere strength is 0.1-0.4 and is set by position and token type. No token in a text's first ~6 tokens reaches 0.25. Tokens that do reach it are later clause words of running English prose or licence comments (pronouns, auxiliaries, verbs, conjunctions such as ' you', ' can', ' was', ' but', ' without', ' modify', especially a second clause after '.'/';'), sentence-final '.'/'?', and in code a closing '}' or ' {', ' return' and line-ending newlines. Word pieces without a leading space (inside identifiers, paths, numbers), indentation spaces, '_', '=', nouns and ' of'/' for'/' to' stay at 0.1-0.2, so windows of plain prose or licence text are usually active, and windows made of indented code, identifiers, data or non-Latin text usually are not.
