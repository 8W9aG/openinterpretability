## Hypothesis

> The pole marks discourse-level clause openings: it fires on the sentence adverbs and connective adverbs that frame a proposition instead of naming anything (certainly, obviously, probably, maybe, indeed, actually, never, always, usually, now, again, although, though, while, whereas, however, moreover, furthermore, therefore, instead), on the deictic/wh-words that open a clause (there, here, what, why, when, where, who), and on the head of a fixed sentence adverbial after its preposition (of course, in fact, in truth, in reality, for sure, no doubt, on the contrary, on the other hand, in addition, not only).
