## Hypothesis

> Retrospective detector for spatial meaning: the pole peaks on the content word that places something in a spatial frame of reference, i.e. an axis/direction word or morpheme ('forward', 'backward', 'downward', 'upward', 'outwards', 'westward', 'top-down', 'north', 'east', 'northeast') or a distance/dimension/spatial-motion term ('radius', 'coordinates', 'distance', 'dimensions', 'width', 'height', 'gravity', 'travels', 'descended', 'accelerated'), including conventional spatial metaphors such as 'top-down approach' and 'fast forward a few years'.
