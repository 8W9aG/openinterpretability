## Hypothesis

> Fires on the modifier or keyword that opens a declaration or clause in source code. It is strongest (~0.9-1.0 of max) on ' private', ' protected' and ' final' in a 'private/public static final' chain at the start of a class-member line, and weaker on ' public' (~0.5-0.6), ' static', 'const' in a parameter list, and VB ' then'. Any code with braces or statements also sets a floor of ~0.5-0.7 of max on ' {' and ';' tokens, but plain English prose stays near 0 even with 'private' or 'public' in it (e.g. 'the private sector'). The leftovers are a few prose tokens that reach ~0.3-0.5: ' than', ' Jersey', ' Pennsylvania'.
