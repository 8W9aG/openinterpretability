## Hypothesis

> This pole is a retrospective detector for the distal demonstrative 'that'/'those' used to pick out a specific thing, set of things or proposition that the context has made available — as a pronoun ('I want that', 'with that', 'and that mattered', 'what is that?') or as a determiner ('that study', 'those findings', "That's why", sentence-initial 'That …' after a full stop or a newline) — and it is silent on the proximal 'this'/'these' and on 'that' where the preceding word already selects a complementizer or relative clause ('the fact that', 'suggests that', 'it is clear that', 'the one that I wanted'); firing is graded by how well the prefix supports that referring reading (strongest after a full stop or newline, a conjunction, a preposition or a transitive verb, weakest after nouns and clause-selecting verbs, and never at a text's very first token). It also fires more weakly and only in some contexts (roughly a tenth to a third of the demonstrative peak) on scattered specific, technical or quantitative content words — 'revenue' after 'quarterly', 'charges' after 'faced', and 'test', 'system', 'computer', 'monitor', 'processor', 'measurement', 'metric', 'unit', 'model', 'number', 'input', 'output', 'component' after a determiner — which I read as an offshoot of the same 'specific, already-identifiable referent' direction rather than a second job.
