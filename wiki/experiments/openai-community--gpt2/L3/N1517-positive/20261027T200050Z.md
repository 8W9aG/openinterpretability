## Hypothesis

> A sparse, largely token-keyed detector (zero on whitespace, almost always zero on punctuation and function words, and zero at position 0, so plain text built from common words usually stays below 0.25 x max) that fires on the content token that completes a short unit. That token is either the second piece of a word whose first piece is a short prefix-like fragment (under|weight, unc|orrect, r|ags, Un|ions, un|ify, U|pps, super|visor, C|OS) or the head noun, participle or superlative right after a possessive, determiner or intensifier (your premiums, our minds, Your best, the greatest, highly decorated, newly diagnosed, more experienced). The token itself sets most of the strength, and a tight modifier or prefix just before it boosts it (highly > very > and before 'decorated'); the neuron is silent on the modifier or prefix itself and on the next token.
