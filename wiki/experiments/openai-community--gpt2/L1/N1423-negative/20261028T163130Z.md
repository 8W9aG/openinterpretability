## Hypothesis

> This pole is the saturated-inhibition readout (GeLU floor ~0.17) of an L1 neuron: it peaks on non-sequence-initial, word-initial tokens whose embedding plus local bigram context do not match the neuron's positive key (e.g. senator, pleaded, boils, at, summit, the cat/dog). It is silent on the sequence-initial token (which drives the positive pole instead), on continuation pieces and veto words with opposing unigram keys (iked, announced, degrees, rose, world, me, defendant), with the same token's strength varying strongly by predecessor (to: 0.17 after According vs 0.03 after hiked).
