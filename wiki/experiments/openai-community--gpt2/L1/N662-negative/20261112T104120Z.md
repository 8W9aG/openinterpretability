## Hypothesis

> The negative pole is this layer-1 neuron's shut state (GELU's trough, whose floor value 0.170 is the unit's maximum), and it is read out almost entirely from the current token's identity: it saturates on nearly every token of ordinary English prose and code - content words (hand, world, city, day, part, fact, thing, good, return), word-internal pieces (ology, ments, per, rib) and symbols ({, }, &, $, +) - and it steps off the trough toward 0 only on a stable per-token class: the most frequent grammatical single-token English words (the, is, are, can, has, they, some, most, other, which, only, may, two, three, first, through, we, I, people, table, book, time, way, place, here, years) and non-ASCII byte pieces and bare symbols (backslash, /, ?, %, parentheses, Cyrillic/Greek/Hebrew/Arabic bytes).
