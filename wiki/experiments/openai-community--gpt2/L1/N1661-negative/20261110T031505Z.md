## Hypothesis

> The negative pole is GELU's dip used as a token-rarity detector: it saturates at ≈0.17 on unfamiliar token types — any token carrying a non-ASCII byte (the byte pieces GPT-2 uses to spell Cyrillic, Greek, CJK, Arabic, Devanagari and accented Latin, which saturate in 90-95% of their occurrences), and among plain ASCII text the uncommon words, names, abbreviations and rare numbers — while the ~1000 most frequent English word tokens (the, of, and, a, in, to, it, on, can, do, day, table, bus, people, year) leave it at 0. Position adds a fixed term on top of the token rule: a text's first token is never active and its second token is boosted (active on 91% of scanned windows), decaying back to about half the tokens by mid-text.
