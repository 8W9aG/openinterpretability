## Hypothesis

> L1/N682- is a retrospective lexical-register detector: it fires on the token that carries the lexical content of a word from informational written prose (news, legal, academic, documentation), both when that word is one token (' source', ' evidence', ' decision', ' penalty', ' research', ' analysis', ' testimony', ' policy', ' trial') and, when the word is split across pieces, on its completing final piece ('ury' in All-Century, 'alty' in Penalty, 'icular' in vehicular, ' Degree' in First Degree).
