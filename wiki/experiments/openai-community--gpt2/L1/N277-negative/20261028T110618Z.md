## Hypothesis

> GPT-2 L1/N277's pre-activation is pinned in the flat basin around the GELU minimum (x = -0.753, where the activation bottoms out at -0.17004, exactly this pole's scan maximum, 0.17004072666168213), because the neuron reads the block-0 attention output - the mixed left context - rather than the current token's identity. The negative pole is therefore a near-constant GELU-floor readout, not a content detector: predicted active (peak 0.65-1.00 x scale, 0.87-1.00 for ordinary text) for any prompt of two or more tokens whatever the topic, language or token identity, and predicted inactive (peak <= 0.16 x scale) only for a one-token prompt; within a sequence its strength decays smoothly with absolute position (pre-activation crosses zero near position 90 and turns positive beyond, where the same neuron's positive pole fires), so a long sequence loses the pole after roughly 80 tokens even though the peak over a prompt is always set by its early tokens.
