## Hypothesis

> L1/N321 tracks whose voice the text is in: its negative pole is the neuron's off state (the GELU floor at ~0.17), and it fires where the current token refers to someone other than the writer - 2nd/3rd-person personal pronouns and possessives such as his, her, their, he, she, they, you, your, him, them - and across clauses that are narrated in the third person rather than voiced by the writer. The same position drops to ~0 when it carries first-person reference (I, we, my, our) or a non-person anaphor (it, that, those), and changing only a clause's subject from 'they/he' to 'we/I' lowers the pole's peak by 0.11-0.14 while every other token stays identical.
