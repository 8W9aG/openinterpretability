## Hypothesis

> This pole is a sparse surface-form detector, not a concept neuron: it fires on a fixed ~1-2% of GPT-2 token types whose forms are marked, namely uppercase chunk tokens ('AS', 'AT', 'IE', 'IS', 'IF', 'ME'), capitalized function words ('In', 'To', 'By', 'Of') and word-internal (no-leading-space) fragments ('ase', 'ages', 'ie', 'ures'), which reach 0.6-1.6, while the same strings' canonical space-prefixed lowercase tokens stay near the floor ('Ġas' 0.10, 'Ġto' 0.05, 'Ġie' 0.13, 'Ġthe' -0.16). 'AS' is not special and neither is the acronym/technical register: in matched frames 'AT' in 'MCAT' fires as hard as 'AS' in 'MCAS' (1.55 vs 1.82), '-ase' in 'helicase' fires as hard as 'AS' in 'GWAS' (1.16), yet genuine acronyms ('NBC', 'CBS', 'ASCII') and technical words split into other fragments ('ization', 'ence', 'ography') stay at the GELU floor; a residual 0.35-0.9 response to a few ordinary concrete nouns in canonical form ('Ġice', 'Ġshake', 'Ġblood', 'Ġlife') is not explained by this rule.
