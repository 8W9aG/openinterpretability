## Hypothesis

> N1762+ is a line-start layout detector: it fires on the token that opens a new line of a non-prose, formatted document when that token carries no word content - a symbol token directly after a newline (table pipe, bracket, list dash, heading hash, comment slashes, bullet, rule characters, closing bracket) or the whitespace of an indentation run - and it fires most strongly when the line follows a blank line, so the newline that opens a blank line itself fires (~0.3 of max) as well. It is silent on words, including words at the start of a line, and on prose punctuation mid-sentence, and it is only weakly active (mostly under 0.25 of max) on the same symbol tokens when they appear mid-line.

- **Mechanism claimed:** retrospective
