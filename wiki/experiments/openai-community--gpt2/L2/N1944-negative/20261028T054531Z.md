Third GPT-2 cap-artifact audit (random draw, seed 1989718940; 200k fineweb-edu tokens as
128-token windows). The scan maximum is **0.1700408**, the `gelu_new` floor, and the
proposer traced it exactly as with L7/N1883− and L11/N2566−: the neuron is alive — positively
active on 58% of tokens with peaks to 1.30 — but the negative pole is
`-min(gelu_new(pre), 0)`, capped at 0.1700408 for any pre-activation in roughly
[−0.92, −0.60]. The cap is a property of the layer (3,045 of 3,072 L2 neurons reach the same
floor on this corpus), 46% of scan windows land within 1e-4 of it, and all 20 top examples
are 2-ULP float32 ties.

This record is the cleanest of the three because the claim specifies the boundary and the
probes test it directly. It came out **VERIFIED 1.00**: 13/13 hard negatives, 7/7 holdouts,
6/6 counterfactuals, and it beats the position baseline (0.69; token identity R² 0.68).
Bare single tokens sit at the floor (` transport` −0, ` table` −0, ` nitrogen` 0.0002,
`]` 0.007) and so do 2–4 token all-flat spans (` the information` −0, ` it was not` −0,
` many of their own` −0, ` on the river` −0, ` the problem of` −0, ` his own number` −0,
