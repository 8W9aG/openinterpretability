## Hypothesis

> The pole fires where a quantity or measure expression is attached to what it quantifies: its strongest activations (up to ~2.2 against a 0.18 corpus mean) are on the partitive ' of' after a numeral or measure word - percentages ('90% of', '90 percent of'), counts and durations ('20 years of', '10 minutes of'), and magnitude words ('thousands of', 'millions of') - and it also fires on a noun directly quantified by a preceding numeral ('20,000 people', '216 tree populations').
