## Hypothesis

> The pole marks an indefinite quantity/extent phrase: it fires on 'a'/'an' where the model expects such a phrase's degree or amount word next ('a lot of', 'a few', 'a number of', 'a variety of', 'a wide range', 'a high standard', 'a good deal'), and on the degree modifier itself when it follows (wide, vast, broad, high, long, good, great, very, significant); its output direction promotes exactly those quantity heads (variety, array, range, multitude, slew, plethora, host, lot), and zero-ablating it changes the next-token distribution only weakly (KL ~1e-5), so the claim is retrospective. It is near zero on 'the'/'some'/'his', on 'a' in copular predicate nominatives ('she is a doctor'), on quantifier heads (few, number, lot, variety), and on degree words outside an indefinite phrase ('the range is very wide' is silent, 'the wide range' about half as strong).
