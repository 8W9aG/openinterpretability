## Hypothesis

> The pole fires on discourse-anaphoric and scope-marking expressions whose content is fixed by earlier discourse: prototypically 'so' retrieving a prior proposition, list, or name (said/think so, and so on/forth, so-called, so far, even so), plus functional relatives in the same role (to the extent/degree, except, as, such). It stays near floor when the same tokens are used non-anaphorically, especially degree-intensifier 'so' (was/felt so + gradable adjective), approximator 'or so', or unrelated content words (very, etc., creams, offers). The same onset-matching extends to memorized routine continuations where the peak lands on a word-initial fragment (the 'so' of 'soaps', the 'g' of 'which gives' after a line break, surname-initial 'G' after a newline or handle boundary), which drive it while same-frame non-routine substitutions do not.
