## Hypothesis

> Fires on code tokens inside a still-open bracket, mostly a round call or argument bracket ('(' ... not yet closed). It is zero outside brackets, on the closing token and after it. It is strongest at depth 2-3 of nested calls, and it peaks on the token that completes the innermost argument just before the closers, typically a standalone closing quote ("'" or '"' left as its own token before '))'), a final identifier/number/keyword argument, or ']'/'}' ending a nested list; it is weaker (about a third) at depth 1 and inside square brackets alone, and weak in prose parentheticals.
