## Hypothesis

> Fires on the preposition that introduces the penalty right after a criminal-sentencing verb: near max on ' to' in 'sentenced/resentenced (him) to' (also with an adverbial in between, e.g. 'sentenced yesterday to'), about 0.6x on 'condemned to', about 0.4-0.5x on 'ordered to' and 'a sentence of'; it stays below 0.25x on ' to'/'for'/'with' after other passive verbs (sent, exiled, deported, confined, promoted, assigned, doomed, forced, jailed for, punished with, sentenced for). It is zero on all other tokens, including the sentencing verb itself and any non-sentencing ' to', so there is no floor on ordinary text.
