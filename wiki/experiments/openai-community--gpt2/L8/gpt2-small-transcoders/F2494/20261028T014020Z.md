## Hypothesis

> Fires on the determiner that opens a noun phrase which is the object of a preposition or verb in mid-sentence, mainly 'the' and also possessives (my/his/their/'s), demonstratives and all/a/any, often with a weaker echo on a following adjective such as 'last' or 'same'. It is strongest after relational or compound prepositions and phrases ('on the back of the', 'outside of the', 'next to/beside the', 'at the edge/heart of the', 'produced for the', 'take a look at my', 'keeps making the'). It is weak or zero after plain 'on/in/to the', on sentence-initial subject determiners, on bare-noun or numeral objects ('of dense forest', 'two lamps') and on content words; there is no floor: the first token and determiner-free text read 0.
