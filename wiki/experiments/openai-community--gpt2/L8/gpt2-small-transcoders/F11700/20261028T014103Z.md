## Hypothesis

> Fires on the token of an 'affect'-class verb (affect/affects/affected/affecting, impact, effect used as a verb, and more weakly influence, impair, hurt, harm, alter, damage) when it is read as an active transitive verb right after its subject or a modal/auxiliary (e.g. 'X may affect', 'which affected', 'will impact', 'drought affected areas'); strongest for affect/impact (~0.8-1.1 x max), ~0.4-0.55 for influence/impair/hurt/harm, ~0.3 for alter/damage. It is near zero for noun uses after a determiner ('an impact on', 'the impact of', 'an effect'), passive or adjectival participles ('were affected by', 'the affected patients', 'deeply affected'), neutral or positive verbs (help, cover, boost, protect, change, determine), and every other token, so there is no floor on prompts lacking such a verb.
