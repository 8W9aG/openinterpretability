## Hypothesis

> GPT-2 L4/N420 is an abstract-relation detector: its positive pole fires on abstract/relational nouns and nominalizations ('construction', 'reduction', 'importance', 'nature', 'outcome', 'role', 'relationship') and then across the complement phrase such a head governs ('of/between' + 'the' + complement noun, e.g. 'reduction of the cost' = +0.95, +1.55, +0.71, +0.92), while staying off on the matching verb/adjective ('constructed', 'reduced', 'important', 'related') and on physical or concrete heads ('building', 'length', 'width', 'position', 'battle', 'cat'); its input row's top vocabulary projections are the nominalizing suffixes 'eness', 'itiveness', 'itivity', 'fulness', 'ation', 'ology', 'fficiency'.
