## Hypothesis

> Fires only on a compound-joining hyphen (more weakly '/' or '_') placed right after a standalone word that is the first part of a noun-first compound. It is strongest (~0.8-1.0 x max) when that word is lowercase ' time'/' times' ('time-consuming', 'time-dependent', 'time-shift', 'time-saving', 'time-out'). It is moderate (~0.2-0.5 x max) after other quantity/resource nouns such as life, cost, age, date, dose, price, money ('life-time', 'cost-effective', 'age-dependent'), and weaker for capitalized sentence-initial 'Time-' or for 'time/' and 'microtime_'. It is zero everywhere else: on the following word, on hyphens after adjectives/adverbs or other first elements (well-, high-, part-, full-, real-, long-, labor-, data-, user-, very-), on hyphens after compounds where 'time' is the second element ('real-time', 'part-time'), on spaced dashes, and on every other token, so ordinary prose without such a compound has no activity floor.
