## Discussion

**NEEDS_REVISION 0.75** — specificity 0.923 (12/13), sensitivity 0.571 (4/7), counterfactual reproduction **1.00 (8/8)**, lookahead consistent, never-active baseline 0.676 below the hypothesis's 0.747. Only the sensitivity gate fails. This is a strong result for a first pass on a very sparse pole (max 3.13, 98.7% exact zeros, 340/4000 held-out windows active): the core idea — the pole fires on the token that closes a fused two-token unit, not on the same words as free constituents — reproduced everywhere except on three held-out unit types.

**The counterfactuals are exact.** Predicted vs observed Δ: 0.60/0.62 (`in place` vs `a place`), 0.65/0.89 (`into play` vs `into drama`), 0.60/0.73 (`cut short` vs `cut brief`), 0.50/0.43 (fused `in-house` vs spaced `in house`), 0.35/0.41 (`end of` vs `end off`), 0.25/0.28 (`onsite` vs `on site`), and 0.00/0.02, 0.00/0.00 for the two controls. Breaking the second member, de-fusing a compound, or swapping the head inside the frame all move the pole by the predicted fraction of scale.

**Specificity is near-perfect**: 12/13 free-constituent negatives read 0.000–0.737 (single `house` 0.000, `take place` 0.000, `changed her mind` 0.000, `into the room` 0.000, `on site` 0.207, `farmhouse` 0.000, `of the` 0.105), and `in house` spaced at 0.737 stays just under the 0.781 line against fused `in-house` at 1.414. The one false positive is `The proposed zoning change is in line with county guidance` at **0.939**, which the claim lists as baseline — and the argmax there is ` with`, not ` line`, so the exclusion of `in line` itself may hold; what fires is somewhere else in that phrase.
