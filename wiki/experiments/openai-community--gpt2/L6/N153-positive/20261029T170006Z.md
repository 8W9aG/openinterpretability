## Hypothesis

> Fires on a noun (or pronoun) that is the head of a verb's object or a preposition's object - a noun phrase in complement rather than subject position ('opened the [[door]]', 'on the [[mat]]', 'read the [[book]]', 'told [[her]]') - and drops about 5-10x when the same noun heads a subject phrase ('The [[door]] was open' 0.09, 'I think the [[cat]] left' 0.29, 'The [[cat]] saw me' 0.22).
