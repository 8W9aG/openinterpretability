## Hypothesis

> Fires on the word right after a definite article 'the' (the head noun, or a pre-nominal evaluative/degree adjective such as good/high/great/low) when that 'the'-phrase is the object of a verb or preposition and names an abstract, non-referential notion that waits for a defining postmodifier: manner, situation, stage, role, kind, range, point, mistake, effects, approach, fortune, or the 'high/low/good' of an attained quantity (earn the high, achieve the low, have the good).
