## Hypothesis

> Fires on the word right after a definite article 'the' (the head noun, or a pre-nominal evaluative/degree adjective such as good/high/great/low) when that 'the'-phrase is the object of a verb or preposition and names an abstract, non-referential notion that waits for a defining postmodifier: manner, situation, stage, role, kind, range, point, mistake, effects, approach, fortune, or the 'high/low/good' of an attained quantity (earn the high, achieve the low, have the good). It is near silent when the article is 'a', 'this' or 'his' instead of 'the', when the noun is concrete (box, kitchen, table, piano, hammer, car, bus), and on sentence-initial subject phrases such as 'The way ...'; a weaker offshoot fires on 'that' or 'it' starting a relative clause right after an abstract noun (toughness that, attitude that).
