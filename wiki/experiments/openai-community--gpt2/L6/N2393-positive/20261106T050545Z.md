## Hypothesis

> The pole fires at the point where a general, characterizing claim - what something is, or is generally like, rather than a report of a one-off event - is grounded or defined, peaking on the connector that carries the relation: 'since', 'as' or 'because' introducing its reason or evidence ('Plants need water since they cannot store it'; 'as' also fires in 'as well as' and evidential 'as shown by'), 'refers to' in a definition, and, more weakly, the period that ends such a claim.
