## Hypothesis

> Fires on the determiner (mostly ' the'; weaker on ' a', ' my', ' every', ' this') that directly follows a directional preposition/particle (to, on, off, in, into, out of, up to) governed by a light motion or reaching verb, i.e. the slot naming the destination in 'get/got to the', 'come to the', 'go/went to the', 'got on/off the', 'taken to the', 'up to the task', 'look in the', 'get out of the'; 'get/got' gives the strongest activation (often above the scan max) and come/go/take/run next. It is zero on every other token (including the first token, so there is no floor), zero when the preposition follows a non-motion verb (talked to, listened to, sat on, belongs to, arrived at, welcome to, according to, close to) or a non-directional preposition (from, with, over), and weak (<0.45 of max) for manner-of-motion verbs like walked/drove.
