## Hypothesis

> This pole fires at the seam between two discourse units: its strongest trigger is the newline that ends a syntactically complete line of structured text (a completed clause, a colon-introduced list, or an HTML/code line), and in running prose it fires on the function word that opens the next clause or noun phrase instead of continuing the current one ('since', 'because', 'whenever', 'each', 'every', 'only', 'more', 'less', 'right'). A newline that splits a phrase fires at about half strength and coordinators ('and', 'but', 'or'), commas, semicolons and periods do not fire, so the peak is a retrospective read of the unit just completed rather than preparation of a specific next token (Jacobian-lens kurtosis 3.2, promoted-token list incoherent).
