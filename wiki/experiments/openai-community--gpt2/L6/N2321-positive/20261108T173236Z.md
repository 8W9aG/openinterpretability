## Hypothesis

> The neuron fires retrospectively on the token that refers to a story character or animate being: a personal name or the final subword of a multi-token name (Sam, Sue, Lily, John, and the fragments ue in Sue, ie in Rosie, my in Timmy), a pronoun or animate common noun referring to an established character (it, people, bird, ant, owl, someone), most strongly when that referent has already been introduced earlier in the story. It also fires, more weakly, on the indefinite article a/an when it introduces a new sound or percept after a perception verb (heard a voice, heard an angry sound, heard a loud noise). It does not fire on verbs, adjectives, or determiners in other roles, and only weakly on inanimate nouns such as tomatoes or cars.
