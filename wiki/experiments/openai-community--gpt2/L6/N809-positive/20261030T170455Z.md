## Hypothesis

> N809+ fires on predications of an ongoing or currently-obtaining situation rather than a completed event or a generic property: it peaks on the present participle of a be ... -ing progressive or a participial modifier (is walking, was cooking, you are doing, sat reading, stalking through), on while/as when one event is framed as running on while another happens, and on a locative or definite time complement of be (is outside, are upstairs, is here, is winter, is Monday); the same clause in simple past/perfect (fished, the children all laughed, has already finished), a passive past participle (was broken, was written), a nominal -ing (building, feelings) or a generic property predicate (is pleasant, is red, is a doctor, are animals) stays near zero. This is not chunk-completion or local predictability: the trigger is imperfective aspect plus referential anchorage, so highly predictable simple-tense collocations stay dead ('the kettle boiled' 0.00, 'central bank raised rates' 0.55 on a non-imperfective token) while low-probability progressives fire maximally ('is rumbling' 2.01, 'were laughing' 1.17), and a single aspectual edit flips the same lexical frame ('she is walking' 1.83 vs 'she is late' 0.62; 'was breaking' 0.97 vs 'was broken' 0.21). The pole sometimes lights on the auxiliary one token before the participle (are at 2.92 before doing), but it peaks on the participle already read, and zero-ablating it mostly removes probability from -ing and path words (umbling, playing, being, through), so the mechanism is retrospective.
