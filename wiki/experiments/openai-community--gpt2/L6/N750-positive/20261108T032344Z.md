## Hypothesis

> The pole fires on the word that joins a phrase to the complement or clause it introduces - the connector itself, never the content that follows: a wh-word after a preposition ('in which', 'by which', 'with which'), 'where' after a noun ('the place where', 'the laboratory where'), purposive 'so (that)', a question word plus inverted auxiliary ('how do', 'what is'), a purpose 'for' that complements a preceding noun or 'in order' ('a charity for', 'in order for'), the dot or slash that opens a new label inside a URL or file path ('github.com', 'http://', 'libcds/'), and the high-frequency complement-taking verbs bring, help, provide, supply, give and link when they introduce an object.
