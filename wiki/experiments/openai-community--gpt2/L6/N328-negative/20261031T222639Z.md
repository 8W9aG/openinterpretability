## Hypothesis

> The negative pole fires on a token that opens a new unit of text rather than continuing the current one, and its strongest and most reliable trigger is the coordinating conjunction: ' and' and ' or' carry a negative pre-activation in 95% and 100% of their corpus occurrences and land in GELU's well (strength ~0.17) in 57% and 80% of them, deepest as the sentence-initial 'And'/'Or'/'But'/'So', and equally in non-coordinating uses of the same word ('one hundred and five', 'try and see', 'on and on'), while the first word of a new sentence after '.' or a line break (capitalized 'The', 'We', 'It', 'Then') and list or markup openers such as ':' or '(' push it the same way but more weakly. It is at the floor on the tokens that close or fill a unit - ')', ']', '?', '!', proper names, numbers and the content word of the second conjunct ('...and the dog were fed' leaves ' and' at pre-activation -1.01, ' the' at -0.67 and ' dog' at +0.76) - so plain declarative prose with no conjunction and no sentence-initial capital usually peaks well below the maximum (mean peak 0.117 over twelve plain sentences versus 0.159 over the same twelve rewritten with 'and'/'or'), and because the ceiling is only GELU's well, any token whose pre-activation happens to land near -0.75 registers as a saturated top example.
