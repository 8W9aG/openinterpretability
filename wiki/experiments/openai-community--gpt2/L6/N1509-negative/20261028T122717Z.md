## Hypothesis

> This pole is the neuron's default/background state: it sits at a low graded baseline on ordinary tokens and is driven fully to zero exactly on the sparse trigger tokens where the positive pole fires (auxiliaries like 'did'/'does', 'all' in 'to all', the verb after 'never', 'to' after 'no reason', 'not' after 'would'). Whether a token is suppressed depends on its context rather than its identity ('not' is at ceiling after 'was' but zero after 'would'; 'to' is at ceiling after 'guilty' but zero after 'no reason'), and the pole promotes no coherent token set (Jacobian kurtosis 3.1, incoherent lens lists), so it reacts retrospectively to the current token in context.
