## Hypothesis

> The negative pole is GELU's dip read out of neuron 225's 'core good' unit - its Jacobian lens is that unit's output direction with the sign flipped (it suppresses confidence, trust, credibility and functioning and promotes errors, defeats and Problems) - so its strength is a band-pass of the unit's pre-activation rather than a token detector: it sits at the 0.170 ceiling (GELU's minimum, reached at a pre-activation of about -0.75) on ordinary running-text tokens, and falls toward 0 in two regimes, where the unit fires (inside a clause predicating the state of something fundamental: 'lost/kept/regained' + his/her/its + health, strength, integrity, dignity, credibility, livelihood, functioning, homes, and 'cannot', 'missing', 'lack', 'everything she had') and where the pre-activation overshoots below about -2 (16% of scanned tokens: page, markup, URL, digit and name pieces). Predict the 0.15-0.17 ceiling for any prompt containing at least one ordinary running-text token, and only the 0.075-0.13 floor for a prompt whose every token, sentence-final punctuation included, lies inside one of those two regimes; because layer 9's first-token state is token-independent (0.075-0.082 over 409 sampled tokens), no non-empty prompt can read below 0.44 of max, which is already above the protocol's 0.043 activity line, so the discriminative split for this pole is 0.17 versus 0.08, not active versus inactive.
