## Hypothesis

> N2697+ is a retrospective detector for closed-class wording that carries extent or quantity in generic, habitual or directive English, not for those words as such: it peaks on both 'as' tokens of an 'as ... as' comparative of extent ('as quickly as possible', 'as much as you can', 'as soon as possible') whatever word fills the middle or follows, on 'every' in a habitual sentence ('We eat out every Friday'), and on the indefinite article or quantifier in an imperative or light-verb frame ('Take a few seconds', 'Please take a ...'). It stays silent on bare prepositional 'as' ('as a rule', 'as the manager asked', 'as the author of this work'), on the definite article in the same slot ('Take the photo'), and on the same closed-class words inside specific third-person narration ('He took a seat', 'I saw a photo').
