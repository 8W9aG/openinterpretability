First audit of this pole (random draw, audit seed 975213270; 200k fineweb-edu tokens as
128-token windows; max 2.75). Token identity explains 44% of scan variance. The
**singular-quantifier determiner** claim — fires on ` a` (24% of its scan occurrences) and
` every`/` each`/` another`/` any`/` one`/` no`/` first`/` last`/` next` opening a
non-referential NP, essentially silent on definite, demonstrative, possessive and plural
quantifiers and on predicate-nominal ` a` (the capitalised confound tokens being BPE onsets
whose response is token-bound) — came out **NEEDS_REVISION at 0.75**: 12/13 hard negatives
but 4/7 holdouts, 9/9 counterfactuals, never-active baseline 0.68 behind the hypothesis.

The exclusions are strong: predicate-nominal `a` scores 0.00 (`She is a very good doctor`),
`the`/`this`/`those`/`my`/`some`/`all`/`two` all 0.00–0.22, and `At first` 0.00,
`Every so often` 0.54 and `Did she find any?` 0.03 stay low. The positives that pass are
`No ticket is valid` 0.76, `First frost arrived` 0.83, `One passenger fainted` 1.24 and the
tent-setup passage 1.29.

But the quantifier inventory is unreliable: all three fresh-genre quantifier holdouts fail —
`Every applicant must bring a passport` 0.39, `Another witness described` 0.37, `Each child
received a booklet` 0.47 — under the 0.687 threshold. And the letter-name exclusion fails:
`The options were a, b, and c` fires at 0.92. So the "a as non-referential determiner" core
holds, while `every`/`each`/`another` do not reliably fire and letter-name `a` does.

Held-out explanation scores: detection 0.70 (TPR 0.88, FPR 0.48), fuzzing 0.83, embedding
AUROC 0.54. Self-reviewed (proposer and reviewer both `external/deepseek-flash[1m]`, via the
sonnet alias); the record is signed, so CI skips reproduction. A revision should enumerate
which quantifiers actually fire with matched probes and explain the letter-name case.
