## Hypothesis

> Retrospective detector for a mid-clause determiner or preposition (a/the/in/to/on/at) that opens or continues a post-verbal object, locative/goal/temporal adjunct, or enumerated noun phrase (e.g. 'into the hallway', 'in a kingdom', 'to the pan', 'and a pinch', 'from 9 a.m.'). Strength is graded by governor and context: maximal after a rich descriptive clause with a placement/transfer/motion verb, moderate in short clauses, weak in fixed collocations ('times a day', 'on the mat'), copular/light-verb frames ('carry a negative', 'sits on'), and zero on sentence-initial determiners. Ablating either the peak token or its preceding governor removes ~all activation, and the output lens promotes no coherent token set (Jacobian kurtosis ~3.0), so it reacts to input already seen rather than preparing a specific upcoming token.
