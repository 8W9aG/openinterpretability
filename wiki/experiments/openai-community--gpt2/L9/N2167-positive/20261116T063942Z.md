## Hypothesis

> This pole fires on short (1-3 character) tokens that cannot stand alone as content words: subword fragments, especially vowel-initial or k/h-onset open syllables (ke, Ka, e/E, o, ha, au, os, al, es), that compose longer words across languages (keer, een, Eesteren, ke- -an nouns, Kasifleri, e-Cigarettes, AS IS), plus short grammatical particles (has, are, de) when licensed by collocational left context (it-has, You-are, van-de/een).
