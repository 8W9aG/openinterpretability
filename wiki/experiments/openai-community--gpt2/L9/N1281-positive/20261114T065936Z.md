The driver is the word's unresolved identity, not the token or its capitalisation: the same token sits at the floor when it is a common word ('As' 0.15 in 'As if he knew', 'me' -0.07 as a pronoun, 'An' 0.15 as the article, 'a' 0.08 in 'It was a problem'), and frequent multi-piece words do not fire (penicillin -0.01, testosterone -0.12, Thomas 0.00).
