## Hypothesis

> The pole fires on the token that carries the spelling of an uncommon, not-yet-resolved word - the opening fragment of a proper name, a genus/species name, or a rare technical or foreign term that BPE splits across several pieces ('An' in Anastasia 2.11, 'As' in Asio 2.73, 'a' in aldosterone 1.40, 'me' in meandering 1.99) - and stays partly raised over that word's later pieces. The driver is the word's unresolved identity, not the token or its capitalisation: the same token sits at the floor when it is a common word ('As' 0.15 in 'As if he knew', 'me' -0.07 as a pronoun, 'An' 0.15 as the article, 'a' 0.08 in 'It was a problem'), and frequent multi-piece words do not fire (penicillin -0.01, testosterone -0.12, Thomas 0.00).
