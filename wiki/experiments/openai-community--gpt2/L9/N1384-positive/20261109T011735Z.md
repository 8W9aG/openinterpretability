## Hypothesis

> This pole fires when the clause under way predicates something about a person: activation climbs through the first few tokens of the predicate (the copula or auxiliary - is, was, are, am, have, can - a predicate adverb such as 'also', 'not', 'mainly', or a possessive/relative pronoun that refers back to the person: his, who, or its for an organisation) whenever the clause's subject denotes a human being (a name, he/she/I/you, a human noun phrase) or a body of people (an organisation, committee, institution, family).
