## Hypothesis

> The pole fires retrospectively on tokens that carry no referential content for the model - material that frames or manages the discourse rather than naming something. Concretely that is English clause-level adverbial conjuncts and disjuncts (however, therefore, thus, hence, moreover, furthermore, nevertheless, consequently, meanwhile, instead, alternatively, also, otherwise, namely, respectively, the -ly sentence adverbs), English discourse particles and interjections (well, yes, no, thanks, please, sorry, okay, hey), and the Latin abbreviations i.e., e.g. and vs. - and, far more broadly, most whole function words and word-final pieces anywhere in a passage in a language the model does not read as English (Spanish, Italian, French, Portuguese, Polish, Russian, Ukrainian, Turkish, Greek, Chinese, Japanese, Korean, Hindi, Arabic, even romanised Russian): it stays near zero on ordinary English prose and on English content words, determiners and the plain closed class (the, and, but, because, although, while, unless, if, whether), on rare or invented English words however they are tokenised (glorp, zymurgy, uncharacteristically), on German and Dutch, and on source code.
