## Hypothesis

> L11/N468+ is a retrospective specialist-content detector: it fires on scientific, medical and technical vocabulary - the specialist word itself, one of its sub-word pieces, or the compound term it heads (immune, genetic, molecular, cognitive, synaptic, enzyme, protein, cell, DNA, Pleistocene, gas giant, chemical energy) - at 0.7-2.2 (0.3-1.0 of the scan maximum), and it also fires at 0.9-2.1, with the peak on the auxiliary, on reported-statement clauses that have a person-noun subject ('the senator announced she was late', 'the mayor said she would attend', 'my sister announced she was engaged').
