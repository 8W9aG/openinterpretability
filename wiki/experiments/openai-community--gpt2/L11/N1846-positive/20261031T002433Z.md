## Hypothesis

> The pole fires on the content word that completes a clause reporting a bounded event: an eventive verb of motion, transfer or change ('wrote', 'closed', 'moved', 'broke', 'became', 'arrived', 'sent', 'opened', 'sold', 'rose', 'scored') or the object/adjunct word that closes that predicate's phrase ('wrote a letter', 'closed the shop', 'moved to Boston', 'broke the window', 'became a teacher'), peaking at 1.0-3.6 there. It stays low on copular and stative predicates whatever the tense ('is a teacher' 0.23, 'is big' 0.04, 'kept a letter' 0.20, 'own the shop' 0.26, 'lives in Boston' 0.12, 'sat on the mat' 0.00, 'waited at the door' 0.00; never above 0.7), on function words and on a text's first token, and the same noun flips with the verb it follows ('letter' 2.0 after 'wrote' vs 0.2 after 'kept'); its negative pole never exceeds 0.17 and carries no contrastive class.
