## Hypothesis

> The pole fires on the standalone space token ' ' (GPT-2 token id 220) wherever it occurs as its own token: as a trailing space after sentence-final punctuation before a paragraph break, in a double space between words, or alone at the end of a text. It fires at about 2.8 to 3.2 there, driven by the token itself: input attribution puts essentially all of the peak on the space token, and token identity explains 99.5% of activation variance. Ordinary text with only single spaces never contains the token and stays quiet. A few content tokens also fire moderately on their own, most strongly ' ball' at about 1.2, but they are not part of the claimed pattern.
