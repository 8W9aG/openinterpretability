## Hypothesis

> Fires retrospectively on the opening double-quote token that begins a quoted utterance of direct speech, the token ' "' (a space followed by a double quote) as in Mom said, "Time for bed." It fires at about 5.4 on that token and much more weakly, about 2.4, on the bare '"' token, which in this corpus is usually a closing quote or a quote fused with preceding punctuation. It does not fire on the words inside the quotation or on the speech verb that precedes it.

- **Mechanism claimed:** retrospective
