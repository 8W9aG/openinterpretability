## Hypothesis

> This Layer-0 pole is a retrospective unigram detector for span-opening tokens: it fires when the current token conventionally opens or initiates a new addressed or named span — markup/bracket openers ('<', '{', '[', '@' with or without a leading space; strongest), utterance openers (greetings Hello/hey/Hi and letter-opener Dear), and first elements of multiword names and titles (New, Los, San, El, La, Mr, Ms). It stays near zero on ordinary content words, on closers ('>', '}', ')', '('), and on mid-span name parts (John, York, Angeles, London), with idiosyncratic edges (e.g., Dr does not fire) consistent with a learned token family rather than a clean semantic rule.
