## Hypothesis

> This L0 pole is a retrospective detector that fires on the current token when it dates or reports a completed event: strongest on contemporary calendar-year tokens (graded, peaking around 2000-2016 and fading to zero for years before ~1960), and moderately on occurrence/completion/reporting tokens such as ' ended', ' ending', ' posted', ' shared', ' post'/' shares', and weakly ' ago'; it is silent on other numbers, older years, and neutral verbs/nouns. Activity is driven by the current token's embedding (input-key projection ranks years highest; ~95% of attribution sits on the peak token), not by upcoming-token prediction (Jacobian-lens kurtosis is ~3 with no coherent promoted set).
