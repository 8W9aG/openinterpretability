## Hypothesis

> L0/N1506+ fires on the bare single-character divider glyphs that split or join two items in structured text — '/' (ratio, path, per), ':' (key: value, hh:mm), '-' (range, compound, list dash) and more weakly '+' — and only at roughly half that level on ordinary words or sentence punctuation ('.', ',', ';', '?', '!', quotes, brackets) placed in the same slot.
