## Hypothesis

> L0/N1109+ is a token-level detector for the word SUPPORT: at any position whose token is a common surface form of it (' support', ' supports', ' supported', ' Support', 'supporting', 'supportive') the pole fires near its maximum, whatever the sense (financial, emotional, structural, military, software), part of speech, case or surrounding text - a nonsense sentence around the token fires it as hard as a real one (3.25), while stem-free synonyms of support (help, aid, assistance, backing, fund, endorse, sponsor) give nothing.
