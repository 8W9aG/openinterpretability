## Hypothesis

That unspaced form is what GPT-2's tokeniser produces when a word is glued to preceding punctuation (an opening parenthesis, quote, em dash, hyphen, slash, underscore or bracket, or a line start) rather than following a space, so its peaks are the first word inside a parenthetical or quotation — 'files (the "Software")', '(with a count)', '(and you)', 'models (which differ)' — and it fires on the token wherever it occurs, including as the first piece of 'theoretical' ('the') or 'however' ('how').
