## Hypothesis

> Fires on the function and connective tokens (a, -, can, be, its, more, and, or, is, you) inside formal institutional, legal or corporate prose, strongest in software-license and copyright boilerplate ("governed by a BSD-style license that can be", "Inc. or its affiliates", "one or more contributor", "permissions and limitations") and in corporate or industry subjects ("the software industry is", "board of directors that can be"); it also gives moderate responses on degree qualifiers after a negation or article ("not entirely", "equally", "vastly", "very"). It is near zero on casual, domestic or narrative text with the same syntax ("my mom or her friends", "the puppy is chased by a"), and the first token of a prompt is always ~0, so there is no floor.
