## Hypothesis

> N698+ fires on an agent's own place — the locale where a person or entity lives, stays or is lodged — peaking on the possessive determiner or pre-nominal modifier immediately before an abode noun ('their/your/his/her/its/'s' + home, residence, apartment, attic, basement, office, lair, place, directory) inside a locative predicate (lived/slept/stayed/ate/waited in, moved into, returned to, entered, fled, hid in, spent the night at), and on the abode noun itself, strongest for 'home'. It stays quiet when that same possessive is governed by a property or action predicate rather than a locative one ('painted/insured/sold/rebuilt/described/photographed their home'), when an abode noun appears with no locative relation ('the home was sold at auction'), and in agentless locations ('the keys are on the table', 'put the plates in the cupboard'); its output writes the same concept back, promoting home/homes/place/residence/house/apartment/location and 'directory' (as in 'home directory', 'parent directory' — the same relation for files), spread over that whole abode set rather than one token, hence the retrospective reading.
