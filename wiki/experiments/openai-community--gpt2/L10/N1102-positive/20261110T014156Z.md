## Hypothesis

> Fires on the determiner (a/the/an) or head adjective of a predicative nominal complement of a be-verb: at 'is/are/was/were/be/been + a/the/an + (adjective) + noun' where the phrase ascribes a role, property, or evaluation to the subject (e.g. 'was a significant contributor', 'are the norm', 'may be a good thing', 'has been a cause'). The copular construction, not the specific adjective or noun, drives it: replacing the be-verb with a lexical verb collapses activation while swapping the adjective leaves it unchanged. Retrospective: activation peaks on the determiner/adjective itself and falls on the following noun, integrates distant subject context (mean attribution distance 12.4 tokens), and the Jacobian lens shows only a diffuse role-noun direction (kurtosis 3.3), so it reacts to the already-read ascriptive construction rather than preparing a specific upcoming token.

- **Activating tokens:** `a`, `the`, `significant`, `strong`, `an`, `other`
- **Mechanism claimed:** retrospective
