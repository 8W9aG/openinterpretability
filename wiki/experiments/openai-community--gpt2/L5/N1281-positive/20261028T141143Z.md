## Hypothesis

> The pole fires on the word that classifies a referent by its general type rather than by its own properties: typicality/kind adjectives (typical, ordinary, everyday, usual, common, normal, standard, average, classic, quintessential, conventional, traditional) and essence/rank adjectives (primary, main, fundamental, basic, essential, key, real, ultimate, best, top), plus the indefinite/quantified determiner that makes the phrase non-specific (a, an, another, some, every, all, most, one, two). It peaks in predicative position after a copula ('it is typical' 1.80, 'this mistake is classic' 1.86) or between an indefinite article and its noun ('a typical example' 1.26, 'a primary source' 1.88).
