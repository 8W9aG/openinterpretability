## Hypothesis

> Induction head with a short, exact token-ID match window: it fires on a token whose last 2-3 GPT-2 tokens (the current token plus the one or two before it, same IDs, so case and leading-space variants do not match) also occurred earlier in the context. It then attends to the token that followed that earlier copy. Strength grows with match length, from about 0.3-0.5 of max for a 2-token match of content tokens to about 0.6 for 3 or more tokens, and reaches about 0.7-1.0 when the earlier copy was followed by a line break ('\n'). Differences more than about 3 tokens back are ignored, so template and partial repeats such as 'x = alpha + beta;' ... 'z = alpha + beta;' or 'The dog ran...' ... 'the dog ran...' are active from the point where the local tokens match. A single repeated token is usually inactive (common words and punctuation such as ' the', ' of', ',', '.' are always inactive; rare names and numbers reach at most about 0.4), and common-word bigrams like ' of the' or '. The' are weak.
