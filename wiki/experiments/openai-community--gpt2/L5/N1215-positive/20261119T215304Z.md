## Hypothesis

> N1215+ is a retrospective chunk-completion detector: it fires on the token that completes a multi-token unit already introduced in the text — the final piece of a split word or closed compound (beeswax, newsletter, FileInputStream, whoo), the second half of a fixed collocation, coordination or parallel pair (ionized gas, extend and expand, go boom, 95 and 83, thin layer, folded newspaper), or a licensed complement such as an infinitive or object pronoun (to hide, to dissolve, phrase her, lose their, where he). Plain attributive epithets (heavy oak door, musical phrase) and bare material PPs (of aluminium) alone do not drive it, and the peak sits on the completing token rather than the introducing word.
