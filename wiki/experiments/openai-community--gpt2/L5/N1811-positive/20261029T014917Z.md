## Hypothesis

> This pole is retrospective: it peaks on the token that completes a state, position, or time predicate's argument frame -- the object determiner after 'opened', the locative 'on' after posture verbs (sat/sits/stood/lay), the participle in a 'Where are you going' question, or a day/time noun continuing an opening-hours expression ('opens Tuesday', 'Tuesday morning') -- and stays at floor on the same tokens outside those frames ('the' after closed/ate/saw, 'on' after slept/fell, 'going' in declaratives, 'Tuesday' after every/you). The Jacobian lens is incoherent (kurtosis 3.1) and integrated-gradients attribution sits on the 1-3 preceding tokens, so it reacts to the predicate already read rather than preparing an upcoming token.
