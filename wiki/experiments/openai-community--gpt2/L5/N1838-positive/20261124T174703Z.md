It does not fire on 'to' after desire, intention, or motion verbs ('wanted to', 'hoped to', 'decided to', 'needed to', 'tried to', 'went to', 'came to'); on 'was a' with a dummy subject ('There was a little girl', 'It was a big house', 'It was a real dog'); on 'that' after animate nouns with non-motion verbs ('the dog that slept', 'the girl that sang') or in a relative clause reporting a one-off event ('a book that fell', 'a ball that rolled away'); on 'for' after a verb ('cooked food for her family', 'looked for', 'waited for', 'paid for') or 'for' marking purpose, occasion, thanks, or exchange ('went to the shop for milk', 'a gift for her birthday', 'Thank you for', 'for the book'); on dative 'to' ('gave the ball to her friend', 'sent a letter to his mom'); on 'a' or 'the' after ordinary transitive verbs ('saw a scale', 'bought a red ball'); or in verb-less lists.
