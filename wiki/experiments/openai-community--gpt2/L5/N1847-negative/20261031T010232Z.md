## Hypothesis

> This negative pole reports neuron N1847's default/off state: it sits near its ceiling (~0.17, the GELU minimum) on mid-sequence tokens that are strongly constrained by fluent preceding context, typically frequent function words ('the', 'and', 'all', 'not') but also collocationally predicted content words ('market' in 'stock market', 'level'/'floor' in 'sea level'/'sea floor'), and it dips toward zero on contextually unpredictable, information-bearing tokens ('oak', 'rug', 'receipt', 'north') and at sequence start. It is retrospective, not lookahead: input attribution concentrates on the peak token and its immediate predecessor (mean distance ~1 token), its Jacobian lens is incoherent with kurtosis ~3.1 (no specific upcoming-token direction), and its activity is uncorrelated with unigram frequency (r ~ -0.05), so the driver is contextual predictability rather than token identity or frequency alone.
