## Hypothesis

> Fires on the preposition ' about' (much more weakly on ' on'/' regarding'/' of' in the same slot) when it attaches to a preceding quantity or information noun to introduce a topic of knowledge, as in 'find out more about', 'learn/know/see more about', 'more information/details about', 'something/nothing/a lot about' (peak ~0.8-1.1 of max); it stays near zero for approximate 'about' ('costs about ten'), for verb + about ('talked/cares/thinking about'), and for 'What/How about', and is only moderate for bare 'know about' (~0.4). A weaker secondary job (~0.5-0.9 of max) is a mid-word subword piece inside long biomedical/chemical compounds ('ul' in isomaltulose/cardiopulmonary, 'em' in sociodemographics, 'ologically' in histopathologically); ordinary text including the first token stays near 0, so there is no floor.
