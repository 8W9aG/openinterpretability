## Hypothesis

> Fires on the second word of a noun phrase (the pre-head slot right after 'the'/'some'/'all') when that phrase is the object of a verb of examining, presenting or selecting (look at, list, describe, examine, review, discuss, identify, rank, determine, calculate, study, compare, highlight, show you) or of a presentational question/frame ('What is the', 'Here is the'), and the word picks items out of a set: superlative or ranking modifiers (best, greatest, highest, leading, main, top, key, latest, first, favorite, most) are strongest, and generic 'new' or a second determiner ('all the', 'some of the') are moderate. It stays near zero on the bare determiner and the head noun, on descriptive adjectives (young, red), and when the same phrase follows a physical-action verb (eat, buy, kick) or a plain copula ('He was the best', 'It is the best').
