First audit of this pole (random draw, audit seed 1220254970; 200k fineweb-edu tokens as
128-token windows; max 3.95). Token identity explains 65% of scan variance, and the
**lemma-DO** claim — fires on the stem token of every full form of *do* in any syntactic
role (auxiliary of negation, emphatic, inverted, imperative, main verb) regardless of
negation or what follows, with be/have/can/will etc. flat, attenuation to 0.2–0.3 when
clause-initial/inverted, ~half on contractions, ~0.3 on adjectival *done*, a fringe on
*may*, and ` Am` explained as a name fragment — came out **NEEDS_REVISION at 0.74**:
10/13 hard negatives, 5/7 holdouts, 9/10 counterfactuals. Specificity failed, and the win
over the token-identity baseline is narrow (0.70 vs 0.74).

The core fires: `The board did approve` 3.01, `had already done the setup` 3.24, `was doing
stretches` 3.41, `doesn't cover water damage` 1.73, the reservoir passage with in-situ
` does` 3.53; and non-DO auxiliaries mostly sit at 0 (`can`, `was`, `am`). But the
role-invariance claim fails in both directions. Adjectival `a done deal` fires 1.23 (claim:
~0.3), nominal deer `does` fires **3.18** (claim: floor), and the fronted `Only after the
third attempt did the auditors find` fires 2.23 (claim: 0.2–0.3 attenuation). Meanwhile the
imperative `Do not sign the lease` scores **0.00** and passive `was done by a professional
service` 0.26, both predicted active.

So this is not a role-blind lemma detector: the same surface forms fire or fail with
syntactic role and position (nominal `does` and adjectival `done` fire; clause-initial
imperative `Do` and passive `was done` do not), and the inverted case contradicts the
position story. Held-out explanation scores: detection 0.64 (TPR 0.32, FPR 0.04), fuzzing
0.65, embedding AUROC 0.51. Self-reviewed (proposer and reviewer both
`external/deepseek-flash[1m]`, via the sonnet alias); the record is signed, so CI skips
reproduction.
