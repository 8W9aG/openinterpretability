## Hypothesis

> Fires on the token itself, mainly on the desire verb " wish" (strongest when it follows a subject in a relative or conditional clause: "products that they wish to", "if you wish to", "I wish I") and on the royal titles " Queen"/" King" used as a capitalised title or the start of a name ("Queen Mary", "King's Cross", "King's College"), each about 0.65-0.9 of max. Near-synonyms (want, like, hope, plan), "Kings"/"Kingston" and ship-style names stay at or below about 0.35. The relative/interrogative " who" (0.35-0.55), "Prince", "Thank you"/"Thanks in advance" and "Copyright (" in long license headers fire more weakly. Ordinary prose stays near 0.1, but sentence-final '.' or ',' after a clause often reaches 0.2-0.35 of max, so even short prompts without a trigger can sit near the 0.25 threshold.
