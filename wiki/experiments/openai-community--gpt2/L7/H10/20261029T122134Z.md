## Hypothesis

> Induction head: at a token X that occurred earlier in the text, it attends to the token Y that followed the earlier X and writes it. It is far above the line (about 0.5-0.9 of max) when Y is a piece of a word that GPT-2 splits into 2+ tokens (rare names, technical terms, identifiers), so it peaks on the first pieces of the second and later occurrences of such a word, e.g. ' Car' of ' Car|lu|cci', ' Z|b|ign' of Zbigniew, or on '.' of a repeated 'Mr.' right before ' Bol|kon|sky'. It is also above the line, but only at about 0.25-0.5 of max, when the current token continues a repeated run of 2+ ordinary tokens whose earlier copy went on with a non-pronoun word ('Erdogan is a' after 'is a given', 'dog sat on the' after 'cat sat on the', repeated code lines). It stays below the line (under about 0.25 of max, usually at the 0.06 resting level) on the first occurrence of anything, on single repeated words whose neighbours differ (' rental', ' said' repeated many times), and on repeated reporting clauses such as ' said that' followed by a pronoun.
