## Hypothesis

> Induction head with a fuzzy match on the preceding token: it fires on a GPT-2 token X (same token ID as an earlier X, or a case variant of the same word piece split the same way, e.g. 'ering' vs 'ER'+'ING') when the token just before the current X is the same as, or similar to, the token before the earlier X, and it attends to the token that followed the earlier X. Similar means a near-synonym or member of the same narrow set (bought/purchased/sold, red/blue/purple, beta/eta), at about 0.3-0.65 of max; exact repeats of 3 or more tokens give about 0.6-0.9, rising to 0.85-1.0 when the earlier copy was followed by '\n'. It is inactive (under 0.25) when X recurs after an unrelated preceding token: a different part of speech, or determiners and quantifiers swapped (a/the, all/every). It is also inactive when X was tokenized differently the first time ('Ber'+'lin' vs ' Berlin') or when the match is only function words or punctuation (' of the').
