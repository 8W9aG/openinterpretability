First audit of this pole (random draw, audit seed 3312812099; 200k fineweb-edu tokens as
128-token windows; max 2.03). The **naming/restating linker** claim — fires at the `(` that
opens a parenthetical annotation on a referent (strongest after a proper name), and at the
` by`/` with` after a verb of beginning, ending or opening that restates the event, at floor
on square brackets, formula/reference parentheses, locative `by`, `began to read`, plain
narrative and copulas — came out **NEEDS_REVISION at 0.77**: 7/13 hard negatives but 7/7
holdouts and 8/8 counterfactuals. Specificity is the failed gate; the hypothesis (0.77)
narrowly beats the token-identity baseline (0.66; R² 0.34).

The positive side is solid: `The International Tribunal for the Law of the Sea (ITLOS)`
1.72, `the regional water authority (RWA)` 1.39, `the memorial service ended by reading`
1.21, `The documentary opens with a slow pan` 1.26, `Banksia integrifolia (coast banksia)`
1.01, `By the autumn of 1929` 1.74, and even the comma apposition `Dr. Amara Osei, author of
the landmark study` 1.02.

The failures show the trigger is much broader than naming/restating. Parentheticals after
non-referents fire (`Sales climbed … (see fig. 3)` 1.62, `The board will decide (probably by
Friday)` 1.20), as do locative `by` (`the road ends by the lake` 1.14), instrumental `with`
(`He opened the box with a pocketknife` 0.77), the infinitive `began to read` 1.29, and the
temporal clause `As the sun set` 0.63. So the pole looks closer to a general
**adjunct/prepositional-phrase attachment** feature — a phrase appended after a head —
than to a naming-specific one, and its exclusion list should be replaced accordingly.
