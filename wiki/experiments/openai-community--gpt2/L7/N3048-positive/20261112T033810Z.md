## Hypothesis

> N3048+ fires on the closed-class tokens that build a clause's predication - copulas and auxiliaries ('was', 'is', 'are', 'be', "'re"), infinitival and complement 'to/that/as', prepositions that head a complement ('with', 'by', 'of', 'in') and the subject pronouns and conjunctions that open a clause - at roughly a fifth of their occurrences (~20% of ' was', ~17% of ' are'/' be', ~12% of ' is'/' to'), versus ~3% of ' the' and ~2% of tokens sitting inside a bare noun phrase or a list. The peak scales with how well the clause continues the preceding discourse: the same sentence peaks ~1.44 after a topic-matching sentence, ~0.7 after an unrelated one and ~0.54 at a text's start, it drops when the same content is written as a list rather than as clauses, and it does not change with what the clause goes on to say - so it reads clausal and discourse structure retrospectively rather than preparing a specific next token.
