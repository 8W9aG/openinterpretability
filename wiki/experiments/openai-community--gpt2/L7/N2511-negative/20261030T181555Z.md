## Hypothesis

> A tonic (default-on) pole: it sits near its 0.17 ceiling on most non-initial tokens (nouns, adjectives like ' old'/' new', determiners ' the', object pronouns, infinitival ' to', word pieces, numbers, punctuation), i.e. wherever the pre-activation is mildly negative (about -0.4 to -1.3). It is silent at the first position and on the neuron's positive-pole tokens, which are copulas/auxiliaries right after a subject (' is', ' are', ' am') and stative/locative relation words (' lives', ' in', ' on', ' at', ' by' after 'is'/'live'). It is also weak (<0.06) where the pre-activation is driven strongly negative, mainly on nationality or ethnic adjectives (' American', ' German', ' Chinese', ' Jewish') and '+' operators, and somewhat lower on possessives ' his'/' its'.
