## Hypothesis

> Fires on a coordinating token (' and', ' or', ' &', ' /', weaker on ',') that immediately follows a word from the law/legal-justice field (law, laws, Law, legal, legally, statutory, statute, legislation, legislative, constitutional, criminal, civil, court, judicial, justice, rights, lawyers, Securities, Competition, tax), i.e. coordinating that legal term with a following conjunct (e.g. 'law or agreed', 'law and economics', 'statutory and constitutional', 'Securities and Exchange'); a few non-legal first conjuncts also trigger it (' Bill' as in 'Bill & Melinda', ' death', 'men and women').
