## Hypothesis

> This pole fires on the concept of ALTERATION: a word saying that something is (or is being) made different from what it was. Its core members are change, alter, modify, amend, adjust, switch, swap, flip, transform, reshape, distort, adapt and redistribute, plus the 'go against / not conform' sense violate, infringe, contradict, conflict and incompatible, in verb, -ed, -ing and -ation forms (changed, altering, modification, alteration, transition, amended, distorted); it does not fire on verbs of substitution, joining, removal or repair (replace, convert, merge, join, delete, remove, fix) or on generic content words. Its single strongest trigger is the capitalised word ' United', which is a token-form effect rather than the meaning 'unite': it fires after any determiner and even in bare frames ('the United Kingdom', 'the United States', 'United Airlines', 'I said United'), but the lowercase token ' united' in the same sentence does not fire and 'Manchester United' does not fire.
