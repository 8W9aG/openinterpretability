## Hypothesis

> L7/N2069+ detects an expressed amount rather than the preposition: it peaks on a measure or unit noun that follows a number ('25 meters', '500 gigabytes', '300 feet', 'two hours', '10 minutes') and on the partitive ' of' that ties a measure, portion or collection noun to the substance it measures ('a bottle of water', 'a kilogram of sugar', 'a piece of cake', 'two cups of flour', 'thirty minutes of debate', 'a group of tourists'), and drops to its floor when the same ' of' marks a genitive relation ('the price of sugar', 'the colour of the sky', 'the mayor of the city', 'the handle of the door') or when a plural noun after a number is a plain counted object ('5 apples', '3 books'). It also fires on a few concrete body-substance nouns (muscle, tissue, cell, blood) with no quantity context - an offshoot this account leaves unexplained.
