## Hypothesis

> Fires on a negator token (' not', ' no', or the "'t" of a contraction) that negates the phrase that follows it rather than a whole finite clause. It is strongest (about 0.7-1.0 of max) in 'asked/begged/told him not to ...', 'accountable to no one', 'was not so well known', and in contractions like "isn't handled" or "wasn't sure" late in a sentence. Plain clause negation ('I do not know', 'does not want', 'It is not a car') and other negative words ('never', 'nobody', 'cannot') stay weak, below about 0.4. Two token-specific offshoots also fire strongly: the 'im' piece of 'Narasimha' (about 0.9) and ' of' in 'Section <number> of the ... Act' (about 0.75). Nothing sets a floor: the first token and text without these cues stay near 0.
