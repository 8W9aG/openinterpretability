## Hypothesis

> Fires on a negator token when the negation bears on an evaluative adjective, a gerund or obligation, or a past-tense contraction: ' not' before an adjective or gerund ('not nice/mean/shiny', 'not listening', 'asked not to tell'), "'t" in 'couldn't/didn't/wasn't' + verb, ' no' in 'no one', and ' hardly'/' barely'. It is strongest (about 0.7-1.0 of max) on ' not' before an evaluative adjective or gerund and on ' no' in 'no one'.
