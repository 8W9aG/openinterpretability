## Hypothesis

> The negative pole fires, retrospectively, on the token that completes a quantity pinned to an explicit scale: a non-first digit-piece of a multi-token numeral ('aged 73' peaks at '3', 'Excel 2003' mid-numeral, '90 percent' at '0', '20-30' at the final '0') or the %/magnitude word closing it ('90%', '7 billion', 'thirty years'). It requires the numeral to sit inside a scale construction: an age/demographic bracket ('aged N', 'ages A-B', 'her 30s', 'six months and older'), a version/model/rating ('iOS 6', 'Excel 2003', 'gcc 4.4', 'SPF 30'), a hyphenated range or every-N rate ('25-54', 'every 20-30 minutes'), or an N-or-more/threshold/duration frame ('50 or more', 'past thirty years'). Its gain is set by quantity-dense surrounding discourse (mean attribution distance ~84 tokens): the same construction fires weakly isolated and strongly amid other numerals. It stays near zero on opening digit-pieces, on sentence-initial bare numerals, and on numerals in non-scale uses: bare counts/scores ('73 guests', 'men scored 73', 'Thirty guests'), identifiers and calendar dates ('Room 73', 'year 2003', 'TC-3-22.20'), instruction temperatures and single times ('180 degrees', '45 minutes', 'every 45 minutes' at 2.2), and isolated bare percentages ('73% of households', '90 percent of us' sentence-initial). Residual leftovers it does not explain: bare scale nouns firing inside very numeral-dense passages ('decade' at 8.6) and large round magnitudes ('60,000', '100,000'). Its output direction promotes '+'-family continuation tokens, which together with steering (clamping emits enumerations like '100 dollars', '3 books on science, 2 books on history') indicates the pole hands downstream a to-be-continued quantity signal rather than predicting a specific next token.
