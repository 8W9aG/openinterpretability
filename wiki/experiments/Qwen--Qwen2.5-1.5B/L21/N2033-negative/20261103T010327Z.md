## Hypothesis

> Fires on the word 'single' itself (any case, including the subword 'ingle' in camelCase 'singleLine') when it is used in its plain counting/category sense of 'exactly one unit' or 'one-unit kind' (single-photon, single-center, a single game, single room/ticket, single mother, 'I am single'), at roughly 0.6-1.0 of max; other multiplicity words fire much more weakly (double ~0.35-0.45, multi/dual/sole/mon(o)/one ~0.1-0.3, return/twin/great ~0). The pole is strongly suppressed (<~0.25 of max) when 'single' is the emphatic polarity intensifier after 'every', 'not a', 'not one', 'without a' or 'just a', or the music-release noun after 'released', 'new', 'latest', 'first' or 'lead'; plain predicate negation ('I am not single') does not suppress it, and tokens after 'single' are inactive.
