## Hypothesis

> This pole is the model's FEED-word detector: it fires while the word 'feed' is being read, on any word-initial token of the lemma (' feed'/' Feed', ' feeds', ' feeding', ' feeder', ' fed'), including when ' feed' is only the first subword of a longer word (feedlot, feedstock, feedstuff, feed-forward); it does not fire when 'feed' is a non-initial subword (breastfeed, bottle-feed, overfeed, force-feed: 0.3-2.3) or on unrelated fe-/fee- words (feeble, fever, feel, federal, female, fellow, fence: 0.1-1.9), and about a third as strongly it also fires on word-initial ' bed'/' Bed' (and on 'bed' as the first subword of bedrock) and ' food'/' Food', with weak bleed onto ' nour', ' meal', ' bedding', ' fodder', ' seed', ' feet' (2-4). The firing is retrospective and graded: the peak sits on the word itself and changing the next token changes nothing (10 continuations of 'We should feed' all gave exactly 18.29), the strength varies several-fold with the surrounding sentence (3.6-36.5 across 28 contexts) without tracking the word's surprisal (r = -0.07), and the pole's output direction promotes the tokens that usually complete 'feed' (forward/-forward, back, store, lot, stock, mill).
