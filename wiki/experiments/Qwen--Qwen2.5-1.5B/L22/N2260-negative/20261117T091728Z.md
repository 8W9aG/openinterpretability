## Hypothesis

> N2511- is a foreign-script detector: it fires on the current token when the text around it is written in a script the model has almost no training data for, and the response builds over the run, so a single character of a very low-resource script is enough even inside an English sentence (Kannada 'ರ' 40, Bengali 'ক' 48, Devanagari 'न' 39, Khmer 'ក' 37, Tamil 'அ' 31, Burmese 25-35, Georgian 'მ' 26, Armenian 'ա' 21, Amharic 12), a real Greek word of 5+ letters reaches 12-31 ('Ελλάδα' 26, 'Παρίσι' 23, 'και' 20) while isolated Greek letters and nonsense Greek letter strings stay at 0.5-6, and well-covered scripts never fire even in normal runs (Russian/Ukrainian/Serbian Cyrillic <=3, Arabic/Persian <=2.4, Hebrew 0.6, Chinese/Japanese/Korean/Thai/Vietnamese <=1.5); it also ignores emoji, rare CJK or Hangul glyphs, accented Latin (<=1.3), IPA and maths symbols, and English rare words, typos and nonce strings (<1.6), and only rare archaic letters inside well-covered scripts reach the 5-10 tail (archaic Cyrillic 'ѨѩѪѫ' 10).
