## Hypothesis

> The pole fires on the concept of a public announcement or news item: information being announced, disclosed, posted or passed on to an audience, peaking on the words that name the item (announcement, notice, press release, statement, news, updates) and rising equally on the verb frames that do the same job when no such noun is present ('to inform', 'let you know', 'keep you posted', 'stay up to date', 'made public', 'share the news with everyone'). It stays low when the same content is framed as a directive or a non-disclosing act or medium (a warning or advisory, a speech, a memo, a report or raw data, academic 'published'), so it tracks disclosure-to-an-audience rather than the literal tokens.
