## Hypothesis

> The pole fires on a detached separator glyph: a single token that begins with a space and is made only of punctuation, detached from the word before it. Its strength is graded by glyph, strongest (0.5-1.0 of max) for the dash/slash/joiner family ' -', ' –', ' —', ' /', ' ·', ' |', ' >', ' --', ' +', ' •', ' :', ' ||', ' //' (and rarer forms such as ' ›', ' ―', ' ..', ' ---'), moderate (about 0.25-0.5 of max, sometimes just under the active threshold) for a detached ampersand, question or exclamation mark, semicolon, asterisk, equals sign, ellipsis or period, and at or below the active threshold for a detached comma, close paren, open bracket, quote, dollar sign, hash or backtick; the same glyph without its leading space (bare '-', '–', '/', as in 'well-known', '1925–2013', 'http://', 'Key:') is not detected at all.
