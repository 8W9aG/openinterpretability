## Hypothesis

> Retrospective detector of a person's given name drawn from the contemporary (late-20th-century) American name stock: it peaks on the given-name token itself (' Megan' 10.0, ' Kayla' 9.6, ' Brittany' 9.6, ' Megan' 8.7, ' Dustin' 8.7, ' Cody', ' Tyler', ' Colton', ' Courtney', ' Chad', ' Zach', ' Heather', ' Todd', ' Jason', ' Amanda') or its final subword ('enn' in Jenn, 'ack' in Eric, 'achie' in Lachie), in a person-naming frame (after a role noun such as expert/singer/son, inside a name list, after 'written by'), and falls to the pole's floor (~0.15 absolute, ~0.01 of max) in that same slot for name tokens outside that stock: traditional/biblical names (John, David, Michael, Robert, James, Thomas, William, Charles, George, Henry, Edward, Peter, Mark, Isaac, Jane), older-generation names (Walter, Harold, Arthur, Frank, Linda, Barbara, Susan, Margaret, Karen, Nancy), British/Welsh/archaic names (Gareth, Penelope, Harriet, Bartholomew, Owen, Delia, Rupert, Mildred) and recent top-20 coinages (Liam, Mason, Ava, Mia, Ella, Owen).
