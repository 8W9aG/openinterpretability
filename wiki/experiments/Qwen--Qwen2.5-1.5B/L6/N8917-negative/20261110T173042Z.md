> The pole fires on the token carrying a word-final -s inflection on a lexical word: most often the last token of a plural count noun, either a whole word (' tactics', ' guidelines', ' complications', ' raids') or a word-final subword piece ('endants' in 'Defendants', 'ections' in 'affections', 'Ps' in 'Ag-NPs'), and likewise the plural-form -s of place/team names such as ' Mills' and ' Seahawks'.
