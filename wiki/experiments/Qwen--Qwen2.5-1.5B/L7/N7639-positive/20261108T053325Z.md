## Hypothesis

> N7639+ is a sparse retrospective detector of a lexical unit under construction: it peaks at a token when the model is part-way through assembling a multi-token word or compound term, i.e. on the opening or interior piece of a long, usually technical or foreign word that the tokenizer splits (e.g. 'pale-' in paleontology, 'archae-' in archaeology, 'neuro-' in neurosurgery/neurology, 'alb-' in albendazole, 'oped' in orthopedics, 'mult-' in multicolored, 'astr-' in astronomical, 'sub-' in subvalvular, 'uff' in Muffins, 'at' in ratatouille, 'ob-' in obelisk), and on an attributive modifier that heads a compound, product or name term (e.g. 'high' in 'high heels', 'threaded' in 'threaded holes', 'soft' in 'soft drinks', 'Blue' in 'Blue Duiker', 'white' in 'a white patio dining set').
