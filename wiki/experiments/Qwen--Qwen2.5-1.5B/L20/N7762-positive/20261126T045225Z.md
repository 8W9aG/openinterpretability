## Hypothesis

> The pole marks the last token of a typographic unit: it fires on the word immediately before the bare '\n' that ends a hard-wrapped line in running text (code comments, licence headers, Gutenberg-style and other word-wrapped documents) and on the word immediately before a closing emphasis marker '_' or '*' (as in _hetairae_ or *could*), once several consistent line ends have established the wrap, so a 30-token passage usually will not fire. It stays near zero when the next token is ordinary text or ordinary punctuation (unwrapped prose, a sentence-final '.\n', a paragraph '\n\n', a comma, a period, a closing parenthesis), and its peaks sit where the model's own next-token mass is already mostly on that delimiter; zero-ablating the pole shifts the next-token distribution only slightly (KL about 2e-4), so the harness measures the mechanism as retrospective.
