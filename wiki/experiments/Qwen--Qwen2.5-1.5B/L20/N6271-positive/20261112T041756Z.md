## Hypothesis

> Fires retrospectively on a report's citing span: it peaks on the pivot that introduces a named outside source ('was obtained/reported/confirmed ... by/to X', 'on <site>', 'a review on X', 'an interview with X', 'appeared previously on X', 'source: X', and 'told/tells X' where X is a named outlet and the speaker is a designated source such as a spokesperson or named official) and on the source's own name tokens (a publication, website or outlet name, URL/domain pieces such as '://', or a byline bracket such as '【'). The pivot's firing is set by the preceding text alone, so the object it introduces does not change it; the pole stays inactive on non-citing passives ('was reviewed/written by'), on a bare publication name with no citing role, on an outlet used only as the subject of a reporting verb ('Al Jazeera reported ...'), and on ordinary locative phrases ('on the kitchen table').
