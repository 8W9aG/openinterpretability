## Hypothesis

> This pole is a retrospective detector for the aspirated-b consonant pair 'bh' (a 'b'/'B' immediately followed by 'h') in rare, non-native words. It peaks at the sub-word token carrying the 'h': the pair may straddle a token boundary ('Ab'|'hay' 15.2, 'V'|'ib'|'hu' 8.9, 'b'|'hang' 8.9, 'B'|'hop' 7.6, Hindi भारत 'bhārat' 9.9) or sit inside one ('ĠBh' fires at the identical 3.56 in Bhutan, Bhishma, Bhakti and Bhavan); it also fires one step weaker (1-8) on the h-bearing word-internal fragment of other rare or foreign words (Bischoffsgemälde 'cho' 7.9, Brihadaranyaka 'had' 4.8, Hsiang 'ang' 4.2, Verhängen 'äng' 3.4), while every other consonant+h pair is an order of magnitude weaker (gh 1.3, vh 1.7, kh 0.5, ch/dh/sh/th/ph/wh/zh < 0.1), a fragment without 'h' stays near zero, and the strength is fixed by the left context (changing the words after the peak leaves the value bit-identical).
