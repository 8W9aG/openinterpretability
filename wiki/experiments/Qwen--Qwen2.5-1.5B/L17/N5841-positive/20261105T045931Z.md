## Hypothesis

> The negative pole is a retrospective detector of the typographic line boundary of an informational article: it peaks on the newline token that closes a standalone label line - a heading, sub-headline, caption, pull-quote, case-citation line, date header or one-word section label ('Background', 'Personal history', 'Alternatives') - and, as a graded secondary family, on the temporal adjunct that anchors a paragraph or clause ('On Thursday,', 'That afternoon,', 'on Saturday'), the spike landing on the day name or adverb and its closing comma.
