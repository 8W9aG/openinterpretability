## Hypothesis

> N3782+ is a template/boilerplate detector rather than a word detector: it peaks on the slot token where a fixed, conventional construction commits to its continuation - the argument separator inside an SDK call idiom (`tracing.EndSpan(ctx, sc, err)`), the linker before a bug-tracker status label (`mark the bug as ___`), the copula of an administrative verdict (`For the reasons stated above, ... is ___`), the `multiple of` slot of a repeated MATH question - and it strengthens with each instance of that same formula in the preceding context. Strength is set by the formula, not the filler: swapping the filler leaves the peak untouched (UNCONFIRMED -> RESOLVED, denied -> granted -> purple all give the identical value) while breaking the formula removes 0.21-0.73 of max (fmt.Println for tracing.EndSpan, `as` -> `is`, `multiple of` -> `divisor of`, dropping the reason clause); it is retrospective, since zero-ablating the pole moves next-token logits by KL <= 0.006 and the promoted `return`/`result`/`setResult`/`Success` tokens never follow the peaks.

- **Activating tokens:** ` as`, ` is`, `,`, `->`, `EndSpan`, ` diagnostic`, `multiple of`, ` us`, `satisfaction`
