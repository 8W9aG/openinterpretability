## Hypothesis

> L19/N4304-negative is a retrospective lexical detector: it fires on individual word tokens that carry register-specific, non-core vocabulary, i.e. the fixed vocabulary of a specialised domain or marked usage (sexual/adult terms such as kiss, sexual, erotic, libido, intimate, pleasure; technical, chemical and medical terms such as methyl, ethyl, olefin, hydroxyl, vascular, somatic, receptor, estrogen, lipid, and legal-administrative terms such as licensed, authorization, concession, trespass, indict, exempt), and it sits at zero on register-general English, i.e. function words, basic nouns and verbs (water, dog, table, food, walk), basic emotion words (anger, fear, shame, pain) and academic words used across every register (analysis, theory, method, science, cognitive, search, logic, array).
