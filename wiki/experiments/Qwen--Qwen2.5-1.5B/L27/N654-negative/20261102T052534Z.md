## Hypothesis

> This pole fires retrospectively when the current token sits inside a highly constrained multi-token span: either a non-final fragment of a split word or name (e.g. ' ex' in exotico/exosomes, 'Av' in McAvoy, ' Tor' in Toru), or a function word inside a frozen legal/boilerplate paragraph with a fixed completion (e.g. ' either'/' the' before 'express' in license/copyright disclaimers).
