## Hypothesis

> The negative pole of L17/N650 fires on tokens that are pieces of non-prose formal notation rather than words: the '0' inside a 4-digit year in the 2000s ('20xx'), digits and dots inside version/decimal literals ('1.3.1', '2.6.32'), and fragments of URLs, file paths, dotted module names and hyphenated code/class identifiers ('/python', '.views', ' navbar', 'col-sm-3'); prose words and bare quantities (e.g. '2009 items', '4321') do not fire it.
