## Hypothesis

> This pole fires retrospectively when the current token completes a conventional, stereotyped multi-token construction: an idiom or collocation (pinch/bag of salt, at sea), a memorized proper name or fact (Neil after Moon-and, Seine, sea level in the boiling-point sentence), or a boilerplate measure, legal, or technical slot (greater-than five, times-a-day, from-9-a.m.-to, raised interest, negative charge, pleaded not guilty), with strength scaling with canonicality (salt 7.7-13.6 after of-phrases vs pepper 0.3-0.8; runs 0.29 vs eats 0.06). The identical surface token in a novel or compositional frame stays near silent (guilty 0.50 vs 0.00, charge 0.64 vs 0.01, Neil 1.20 with Apollo context vs 0.04 without), so the claim beats the token-identity confound (R2 82%). The effect is detection of the just-completed frame, not preparation of the next token: the Jacobian lens is Gaussian noise (kurtosis 3.3) whose promoted tokens never follow the peaks, and swapping the word after the peak (Neil Armstrong/ate, sea level/food, hall fell silent/apart) leaves the peak unchanged.
