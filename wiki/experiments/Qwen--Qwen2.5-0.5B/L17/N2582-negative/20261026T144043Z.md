## Discussion

<!-- Free-form notes. This page is never regenerated, so edits here are kept. -->

First experiment on this pole (random draw, audit seed 1423032342; 200k fineweb-edu
tokens read as 512-token windows; max activation 3.68). The proposer read the peaks as a
set of five fixed "link-token" frames — gendered ` or`, `del/of` in fixed names,
numeral-range separators, `high school`, change-of-state light verbs — and the review
rejects that at 0.53: 10/13 hard negatives but only 2/7 holdouts.

The failures point somewhere simpler. New instances of the claimed frames barely move the
pole (`The Ministry of Finance…` 0.118, `Valle del Cocora` 0.128, a `30-60` range 0.155,
against a 0.921 threshold), while the negatives that fired are all numeral-dense: `47
people in the audience` (1.057), `scored 12, 17 and 19` (1.342), and a long construction
report full of counts and quantities (2.056). The scan's own top peaks are consistent with
that reading (`Grades 11 and 12`, `ages 12-17`, `holds 90%`), and the "link-token" framing
looks like an overfit to a handful of coincidental examples rather than the pole's job.

So the next hypothesis should test a numeral/quantity response (digits, spelled-out
numbers, counts, percentages) rather than fixed phrase frames; the positive pole's
build/building reading does not explain the negative side. Counterfactuals reproduce
10/10 and the mechanism is retrospective; steering is a null. Proposer and reviewer were
the same model, so the record is self-reviewed.
