## Hypothesis

> The positive pole fires when text fixes a quantity to a scale: it peaks on the token that introduces the value (the space or delimiter before the number in 'p < 0.001', 'BMI > 30', '0.4 to 0.9', '% 74,1 2.927', '$50 $40', or a JSON/XML field) and on the bound or degree word that sets such a value ('below', 'above', 'within', 'exceeding', 'ranging from ... to', 'the point of'), and it is strongest in documents that enumerate many such values (election-result tables 5.5-6.6 of the 6.57 scan max, price lists 5.7, statistics-heavy abstracts 4.2-4.5, dimension-laden layout code 3.3-3.5), whereas a quantity merely mentioned in narrative prose does not fire it ('There were 12 apples' 0.32, 'born in 1987' 0.16) and neither does ordinary descriptive or inceptive prose ('the beginning of the story' 0.06, 'She began to read the whole book' 0.02).
