## Hypothesis

> Fires on the non-final digits of a large numeral written with a thousands separator in running prose (prices, populations, square footage, viewer counts), peaking on the 1st and 2nd digit after the ',' (any digit value, not just '0'; weaker with '.'), and staying near zero on the last digit of a group, on spelled-out numbers ('seven thousand', '$25 million'), and on years and other short numbers.
