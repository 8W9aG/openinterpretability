## Hypothesis

> Fires on the low-content glue tokens of citation- and notice-formatted records, at 1.2-2.9 (0.3-0.7 of max): the leading digit of a number in a citation field when that field is already instantiated ('50-32785 (1975) and 51-11876 (1976)' puts the peak on the '1' of 1976, a reference list with 'Nature 421' on the '4'), the line-initial '//' of '$Keyword$' comment headers, a comma inside a legal list run in a long copyright/licence page, and the possessive determiner of the corporate formula '..., Inc. or its ...'. The same words in ordinary narrative prose (a possessive after a verb, a lone year in running text, '//' before plain words) and the cardinality words that drive the negative pole (single, double, multi, one, individual) stay inactive, while ordinal/positional words (first, second, mid, early, late, top, high, low, main, primary) give only a weak 0.3-0.8 response that stays below the 0.25xmax active threshold.

- **Activating tokens:** ` its`, `1`, `9`, `//`, `,`, ` by`, ` Mode`, ` his`, ` your`, ` The`
- **Mechanism claimed:** retrospective
- **Max activation (scan):** 4.2035
