> Retrospective detector for the onset of reported/quoted material in a reportive frame: activation climbs through the attribution clause ('she said,', 'greeted her with', 'I thought:'), peaks on the opening quotation mark that follows it (' "', ' “', ' ‘'), stays elevated across the quoted words, and collapses at the closing quote; the frame carries most of the response (deleting the two quote glyphs costs only ~0.12 of max, and bare quoted dialogue lines still give ~0.5), while the same string in a machine-log frame, config/identifier string literals (JSON keys, #include headers), line-initial quotes after a newline, and impersonal news-register scare quotes sit near zero, and a moderate secondary trigger (~0.25-0.4 of max) fires on numerals ('4 years ago', '2019', '100%') and a few technical-term onsets (' mult'ithreading, ' Bio'hazard) in running prose.
