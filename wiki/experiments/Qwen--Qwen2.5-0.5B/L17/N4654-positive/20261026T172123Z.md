## Hypothesis

> This pole marks the backbone of a clause's main assertion: it peaks on the subject/topic head (The stock/cat/river, protons, one pill), on the head of the main predicate (lexical verbs like sat/fell/sits/print, copulas is/are, and measure or frequency complements like three, 12 percent, 100 degrees, all), and on tokens that launch the next clause or enumerated item (which, and/while, first-conjunct commas, sentence-final periods). It stays near zero on determiners, on backgrounded definite objects, and on highly predictable continuations (times after three, open after is, flooded after river), so the same noun fires strongly as subject but weakly as object.
