## Hypothesis

> Fires on a transitive verb (or the perfect auxiliary 'have/has' right before it) whose direct object has already been supplied to its left, so no object is expected after it: the verb of an object-gap relative clause with or without 'that' ('the table I found', 'the house Jack built', 'the option people have had', 'everything you need'), or a participle whose subject is its patient ('a method has been developed', 'has been known').
