## Hypothesis

> The pole fires on constituent-internal hinge tokens: the current token both satisfies an expectation set by the preceding 1-3 tokens (the verb an inanimate subject selects, the complement a noun/verb/query frame requires, the closer a call header opens) and itself tightly constrains the specific continuation (calls->for, of->France, golden->brown, FROM->users). Same-slot incongruent fillers go low or negative, the same surface token outside a hinge stays inactive, and zero-ablating the pole leaves next-token probabilities unchanged (KL ~1e-5), so the mechanism is retrospective detection, not next-token prediction.
