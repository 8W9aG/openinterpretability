## Discussion

<!-- Free-form notes. This page is never regenerated, so edits here are kept. -->

First audit of this pole (random draw, audit seed 999836069; 200k fineweb-edu tokens as
512-token windows; max 6.05). The **evaluative/normative modifier** claim came out
**NEEDS_REVISION at 0.64**, and the two halves are unusually clean: **13/13 hard negatives
pass** (specificity 1.00 — every non-evaluative use of the trigger words, every ordinal and
physical modifier, the inventory list and the predicative `the smoky one was best` all stay
low) but only **2/7 holdouts** fire. The never-active baseline sits at 0.86.

The passing holdouts point at a narrower feature than "judgement": `the ideal knife…` (2.36)
and the long Riverford Trust passage (2.35), whose trigger is `the recommended sampling
depth`. The failures are evaluative or normative but not *recommendations*: `the essential
item on the checklist` 1.23 (just under the 1.51 threshold), `the soundest choice` 0.94,
`the usual mistake` 0.46, `the worst outcome` 0.37, `the standard practice` 0.30. So the
pole is not reading evaluation or normativity as such; it looks like an *optimality or
recommendation* detector — "the best/ideal/recommended X", the member of a class you should
pick — with negative or merely conventional judgements excluded. That also fits the scan
max: `the best type to chew` (6.05).

Counterfactuals are 8/8 with the proposer's own measured deltas, and the lookahead check is
retrospective. Explanation scores on the held-out sample are strong but the sample is tiny:
detection 0.80 (TPR 0.80, FPR 0.20, 10 windows) and fuzzing 1.00 on a single window, with
embedding AUROC 0.60; only 6 of 4000 windows are active at this threshold, so the held-out
numbers do not carry much information and the probe result is what matters.

Self-reviewed (proposer and reviewer both `external/deepseek-flash[1m]`). A revision should
test the recommendation reading directly — `the best/ideal/recommended/recommended X` as
positives and `the worst/essential/standard/usual X` as negatives with the head noun held
fixed — and should check whether `soundest` fails because of the modifier or because of the
head noun `choice`.
