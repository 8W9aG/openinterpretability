> Retrospective detector of a stated age of a particular person: it peaks on the token that completes the age phrase - 'old' in 'N-year-old' or 'N years old', the numeral of 'aged N' / 'at age N', and the digits of a bare appositive age ('Name, 60,'). It stays near zero when the same forms describe a non-person or are generic ('the tree was 56 years old', 'men aged 44', '56-page', 'at mile 80'); a weaker (0.5-2) response also occurs on the 'of/from <place or institution>' appositive that identifies a named person ('First Last, of Springfield').
