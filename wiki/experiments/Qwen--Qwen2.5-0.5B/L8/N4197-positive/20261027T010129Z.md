First experiment on this pole (random draw, audit seed 2645956705; 200k fineweb-edu
tokens read as 512-token windows; max activation 3.99). The claim is a
**distributive-possession** detector: plural nouns naming what each member of a set has or
exhibits individually — belongings, personal attributes, per-item properties — boosted by
plural possessors and `own`/`themselves`, and flat on singulars, generic unpossessed
plurals, plural person nouns, irregular plurals used as plain subjects and singular nouns
ending in -s. The review verifies it at 0.89 (12/13 hard negatives, 6/7 holdouts, 8/8
counterfactuals, sufficiency and necessity 0.97), above the never-active baseline (0.53).
The lenses agree: both promote `their`, `themselves`, `各自`, `他们的` — the vocabulary of the
feature.

Both misses are informative. A generic unpossessed plural still fires when it is the scan's
peak noun (`The homes along the ridge…`, 1.799), so token identity contributes alongside the
distributive reading (R² 0.32). And a technical per-item property plural misses narrowly
(`the absorbances of the samples differed…`, 0.794 against the 0.998 threshold), so the
lab-frame version of the construction is weaker than the everyday-possession version.

Held-out explanation scores are respectable (detection 0.66, fuzzing 0.69, AUROC 0.71).
Proposer and reviewer were the same model, so the record is self-reviewed.
