## Discussion

<!-- Free-form notes. This page is never regenerated, so edits here are kept. -->

First audit of this pole (random draw, audit seed 3091482359; 200k fineweb-edu tokens as
512-token windows; max 3.70). The **privation-of-a-valued-thing** claim — fires where a
clause says a valued, needed thing is absent, denied, insufficient or cut down, peaking on
the valued word or the operator's complement; quiet when the same operator applies to
something unwanted, under neutral/supportive verbs, or in bare intransitive decline — came
out **NEEDS_REVISION at 0.79**: **13/13 hard negatives** (perfect specificity) but 4/7
holdouts, 9/9 counterfactuals, and it clears the never-active baseline (0.71).

The negative side is excellent: `reduce crime and cut costs` 0.18, a report that
`describes the quality of the water supply` 0.24, `no problems with the engine, nothing
dangerous` 0.23, `plenty of water` 0.17, `Nothing … affects any statutory rights` 0.16,
`high motivation and adequate training` 0.46, `not unsafe` 0.48, a blocked pipe flooding the
basement 0.57, `Not a single safety incident` 0.24, `measure prosperity` 0.15, `absorb
iron` 0.33 and `overcame … setbacks` 0.40 — all under the 0.926 threshold.

The failures separate the negator from the reduction verb. Reduction/removal verbs fire:
`The new budget slashes funding for rural schools and trims the pensions` **3.05**,
`the guard rails were removed` **3.26**, `Censorship laws have narrowed … and eroded` 1.34.
Plain negation with a valued noun does not: `no access to basic sanitation` 0.23, `the login
service still lacks the capacity` 0.24, `Nothing in the contract guarantees workers a living
wage` 0.30. So the pole looks more like a **reduction-of-valued-resource** detector driven by
the verb than a general privation detector that includes plain negation.

Held-out explanation scores: detection 0.62 (TPR 0.28, FPR 0.04), fuzzing 0.73, embedding
AUROC 0.73. Self-reviewed (proposer and reviewer both `external/deepseek-flash[1m]`, via the
sonnet alias); the record is signed, so CI skips reproduction. A revision should test
`slash/cut/erode/remove/trim` against `no/lack/nothing` with the same valued nouns.
