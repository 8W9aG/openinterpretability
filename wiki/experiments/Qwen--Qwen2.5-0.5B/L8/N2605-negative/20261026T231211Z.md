First experiment on this pole (random draw, audit seed 2562864993; 200k fineweb-edu
tokens read as 512-token windows; max activation 4.81). The claim is a sparse non-core
(oblique) relation-marker detector — agent ` by`, source ` from`, comparison `than`/`as`,
time/scope `during`/`within`, locative `in`/`at`, abstract-alternative `or` — strongest
when the relation phrase postmodifies a noun, adjective or participle, and flat on
`of`/`to`/`that`, plain `and`, and routine verb phrases. The review confirms the negative
half perfectly (13/13 hard negatives, specificity 1.00) but only 2/7 holdouts fire, so the
verdict is NEEDS_REVISION at 0.64.

The proposer had already flagged this as the likely failure mode, and the five misses bear
it out: fresh natural relation phrases sit far below the 1.202 threshold — `notice served
by the landlord` (0.206), `tissue samples from a colony of bats` (0.079), `considerably
lighter than the engineers had predicted` (0.123), `nearly as expensive as the first`
(0.288), `inertia or simple incompetence` (0.027). The calibrated examples reached 2.6–3.1,
so the effect is real but sparse and frame-sensitive; it does not transfer to ordinary
prose. With five of seven holdouts inactive, the never-active baseline scores 0.86 and
beats the hypothesis.

Causal sufficiency is 0.91 and necessity 0.94; held-out explanation scores are mixed
(detection 0.43, fuzzing 0.75, AUROC 0.68). Proposer and reviewer were the same model, so
the record is self-reviewed.
