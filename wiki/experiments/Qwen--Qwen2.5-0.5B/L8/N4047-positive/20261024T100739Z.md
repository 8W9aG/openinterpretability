## Hypothesis

> This pole is a subject-verb number-agreement detector: it fires on the token carrying a third-person-singular form - a 3sg finite verb (is, was, has, does, or -s forms such as works, explains, drops) or the 3sg possessive 'its' - when that form's grammatical subject/antecedent is plural, including across intervening material or a relative pronoun ('The keys to the cabinet is' peaks at 'is' while 'The key to the cabinets is' does not; irregular plurals such as children/mice/women and invariant nouns the model represents as plural, such as fish/sheep/police, also fire). It stays inactive when the singular form agrees with a singular subject, when the verb is plural/past/bare after a plural subject, when the singular form is inverted before its subject, and when a plural noun is only a nearby non-subject; a plural verb with a singular subject ('The cat are outside') does not fire either, because the 3sg-marked form is required.
