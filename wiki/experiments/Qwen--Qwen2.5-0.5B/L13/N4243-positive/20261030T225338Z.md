## Hypothesis

> This pole fires retrospectively at a token that continues a coherent clause or fixed phrase, with strength graded by the length and fit of its left context: strong firing (about 0.15-0.32) requires at least four preceding tokens forming an intact clause whose final one or two tokens directly license the peak (for example stared at leading to dog, by 12 leading to percent, would not leading to seek). The same content word fires only weakly (about 0.00-0.12) when its licensing bigram is replaced (zebra for at sends dog from 0.32 to 0.09; late for 12 sends percent from 0.22 to 0.09), when word order is scrambled (rates falls to about 0.12), when the context is random words (peak about 0.12 or less), or when three or fewer tokens precede it (At the dog gives 0.00; isolated Dog gives 0.00); isolated, sentence-initial, and clause-truncating function-word tokens sit near zero. Because activation ramps up over the intact prefix, corrupting a late token leaves earlier tokens still firing (stared stays near 0.25; friend in an intact prefix reaches 0.21 while a trailing truncating the is 0.00), so this claim applies per token at the manipulated position, not to the whole-text maximum.
