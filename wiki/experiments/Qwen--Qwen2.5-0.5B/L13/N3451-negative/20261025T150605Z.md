## Hypothesis

> N3451- is a retrospective 'frame-led word' detector: it puts a single sharp spike (up to 1.14, against a 1.18 scan maximum and a median of 0.002) on one word token when the words before it form a well-formed, conventional clause leading into that word, and the size of the spike is set by that left context rather than by the token itself - holding the peak token and its entire right context identical, scrambling the same preceding words ('The reason we continue to read/watch...' -> 'continue we reason The to read/watch...') drops ' read' from 1.14 to 0.03 (prompt peak 1.14 -> 0.61), replacing one frame word ('reason' -> 'point') drops the peak to 0.34, and the same word inside a plain progressive clause ('She was wearing headbands in the yard today.') peaks at only 0.14. Predict inactive when the same word's left context is scrambled, is degenerate filler ('ok ok ok...', 'the and of to in a...'), or is a bare list/label fragment, and predict active for a content word that closes a conventional clause - the pole is not a token detector (token identity explains 20% of variance, and ' wearing'/'reading' 1.11/1.09 both fire while 'carrying' 0.33 does not in the identical slot) and its Jacobian-lens direction names no target token (kurtosis 3.4, 0% logit/Jacobian overlap, zero-ablating the peaks shifts next-token KL by ~1e-5), so it reacts to what has been read rather than preparing a specific upcoming token.
