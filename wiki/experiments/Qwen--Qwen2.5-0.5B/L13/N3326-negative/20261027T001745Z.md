First experiment on this pole (random draw, audit seed 1412029742; 200k fineweb-edu
tokens read as 512-token windows; max activation 4.17). The claim is a historical-period
reference detector: the pole fires while a year, decade or century expression is read —
strongest on 19xx forms, peaking on the second or third digit — and moderately on
modern-past era terms, while the same digits as quantities, codes, ordinals or addresses,
antiquity, vague past talk and present-day prose stay at floor. The review verifies it at
0.86 (13/13 hard negatives, 5/7 holdouts, 7/7 counterfactuals, sufficiency 0.97 and
necessity 0.91), well above the never-active baseline (0.64).

Both misses are marginal and sit at the edge of the predicted strength bands. A screenplay
slugline year, `INT. COURTROOM - 1954` (1.017), falls 2.4% short of the 1.042 threshold, and
a new era term, `The Gilded Age drawing rooms` (0.758), lands just under the hypothesis's
own 1.0–1.5 band for era terms. Everything else in the set is clean, including all thirteen
matched number-sense controls.

Two side notes. The Jacobian lens's TV/television/Sony/broadcast promotion is an output
direction, not lookahead (next-token KL ~8e-4), and the positive pole (max 2.32 on the same
scan) does not carry this feature. Held-out explanation scores are strong (detection 0.90,
fuzzing 0.90, AUROC 0.58). Proposer and reviewer were the same model, so the record is
self-reviewed.
