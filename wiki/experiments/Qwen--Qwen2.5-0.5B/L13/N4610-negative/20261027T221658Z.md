## Hypothesis

> N4610- is a retrospective detector for the read-out of a low-frequency, domain-specific word: it fires on the token at which a rare content word becomes identified - usually the second BPE piece of a multi-token rare word (K|iv|u, Stone|h|enge, Hir|anyak|ashyapu, super|abs|orbent, Mach|-Z|ehnder, cata|le|psy, io.gitlab.art|urb|osch) and otherwise the distinctive word inside a specialised fixed phrase (coronal 'mass' ejections, institutional 'review' board, burden 'of' proof, ineffective assistance 'of' counsel).
