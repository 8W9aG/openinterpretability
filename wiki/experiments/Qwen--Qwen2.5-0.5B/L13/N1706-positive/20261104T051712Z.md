## Hypothesis

> The positive pole fires on a third-person personal pronoun or possessive determiner (she/her/he/him/his, more weakly it/its/they/them/their) that refers back to a person or thing already introduced earlier in the passage, and it fires hardest when that referent is not the nearest named person in its clause - a female pronoun after a male name ('Marco praised her work'), or a pronoun sitting after an inanimate noun. It is near zero on first- and second-person pronouns used generically or in instructions ('if you need...', 'I think...'), on expletive 'it', on a pronoun whose referent has not been introduced, and on non-pronoun words.
