## Hypothesis

> The negative pole fires on the concessive move in evaluative writing: it peaks on a clause-opening concessive marker ('although', 'though', 'even though', 'while', 'despite', 'in spite of', or a concessive opener such as 'sure', 'yes', 'granted', 'it's true') and stays elevated across the clause that marker introduces. The peak is gated by what the writer has just said: it is large only when the preceding sentence makes a strong positive point which the concession then qualifies ('The crew was excellent. Although the boat was old, ...' peaks ~2.4 of the 4.5 max; '...was good...' ~1.7; '...was terrible...' ~0.3; a bare 'Although the room was small, it was clean.' ~0.4), while coordinated contrast ('but', 'however', 'yet', 'nevertheless'), 'because'/'since', 'if', temporal 'while' and praise with no concession following it stay near baseline.
