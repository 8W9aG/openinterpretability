## Hypothesis

> The pole fires on the concessive move in discourse: it peaks at a clause-initial concessive subordinator ('though', 'although', 'even though', 'despite', 'granted', 'admittedly') and stays elevated over the clause that subordinator introduces, scaling with how strong the claim being conceded is ('AJ and Earl were excellent. Although ...' fires roughly four times harder than 'The boat left at eight. Although ...'). Outside a concessive frame it also fires on emphatic positive appraisal in predicate position ('The hotel was excellent/superb/outstanding', 'extremely good', 'the staff were knowledgeable'), while mild or adequate appraisal ('good', 'nice', 'decent', 'fine', 'okay' - which instead activate the opposite pole), negative appraisal ('terrible', 'awful') and the connectives 'but', 'however', 'yet', 'because', 'if' and 'since' leave it quiet.
