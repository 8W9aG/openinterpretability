## Hypothesis

> The negative pole of L13/N439 fires when the text sets a second, alternative referent against the one already in focus: it peaks on contrastive/substitutive phrases ('in favour of', 'instead of', 'in place of', 'rather than', 'as opposed to', 'compared to/with', 'in contrast to', 'unlike') and on the later members of an enumeration or alternative list ('X, Y and Z', 'X or Y', especially after 'like/such as/including'), the strength scaling with how explicitly the alternative is marked. It stays near the floor for proposition-level negation ('not', 'never'), for clause-linking contrast ('but', 'although', 'however', 'whereas', 'while'), for 'and' that merely sequences two clauses, and for 'between X and Y'; it is retrospective (zero-ablating the pole on its top examples leaves next-token probabilities essentially unchanged, KL 2e-5).
