## Hypothesis

> This (negative) pole fires on the token that completes a relational specifying frame set up by the words already read: a source/comparison/aboutness link such as FROM after a SELECT list, than after a comparative adjective, of after a relational noun, or the subject following an already-read contrastive connective (but/while), and it also fires on but itself; its output direction then promotes adversative but/but-family continuations while suppressing '=' identity tokens, the converse of the positive pole which fires on list and quantity items rather than specifying links.
