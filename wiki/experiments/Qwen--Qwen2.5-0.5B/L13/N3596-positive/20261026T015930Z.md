## Hypothesis

> The positive pole fires on the sub-word fragments of an unfamiliar, non-lexical letter string - an unseen acronym or ticker, a mixed letter-digit identifier, an e-mail local part, handle, hash or citation key - that the model cannot resolve to a dictionary word: activation is spread over the whole string ('RR' 0.7 then 'V' 2.5 inside 'RRV', 0.5-2.4 across 'X','K','Z') with the peak on one of its fragments, while the same letters inside ordinary words, familiar single-token acronyms (IBM, NASA, LLC, ABC), misspellings and non-English text leave it near zero. It is strongest when the unknown string is introduced as the label of a named entity ('The company Rentz (RRV)...' 3.8 vs 'Rentz (RRV)...' 1.7), so it is a retrospective detector of label/identifier strings being read out character by character rather than a next-token predictor.
