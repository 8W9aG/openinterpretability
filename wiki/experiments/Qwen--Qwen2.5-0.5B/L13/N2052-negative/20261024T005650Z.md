## Hypothesis

> The negative pole of L13/N2052 is a Wikipedia category-markup detector: it fires when the text contains a wiki category declaration ('Category:<term>', and even more strongly '[[Category:<term>]]'), ramping through 'Category' and ':' to a peak on the opening tokens of the category term, and it fires hardest when that term begins with a 4-digit year ('Category:1903 births/films', peak ~2.0 locally and 3.4 in full articles), less for word terms ('Category:Italian cyclists' ~1.4) and single words ('Category:Physics' ~0.9).
