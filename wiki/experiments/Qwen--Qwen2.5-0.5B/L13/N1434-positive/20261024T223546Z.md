## Hypothesis

> The pole retrospectively detects a locally licensed continuation: it fires on the current token when that token is the filler selected or obliged by the immediately preceding 1-3 tokens (the variable slot after 'If', the complement after a selecting verb such as 'believe' or 'calls', the unit after a quantity such as '500 mg' or 'billion light', the article continuing a parallel definite-NP chain such as 'and the', or the period closing a complete clause), with strength graded by how tightly the local frame constrains that filler. The same token in a non-licensing frame collapses (' x' 0.394 to 0.000; ' calls' 0.144 to 0.000) while distant edits leave firing intact; the Jacobian lens shows no coherent promoted set (kurtosis 3.1, 10% lens agreement) and attribution is 76% within one token, so it reacts to what was read rather than preparing a prediction.
