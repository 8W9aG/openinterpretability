## Hypothesis

> N4506+ is a retrospective detector of an anchored content noun phrase: it fires at the head noun (or at the possessive/determiner that introduces it) when a specific possessor - 's, her/his/their/its, those/these - attaches a noun naming the referent's contents, goods or makeup ('carried some of Emmi's products' 2.21, 'at its core' 1.88, 'his New York origins' 1.23, 'Emmi's goods/items/offerings/range/output/language/history/origins' 1.0-2.0), but not when the same noun is bare or carries a plain definite article ('the products' 0.49, 'products' 0.28) and not when the possessed noun is an ordinary object or scalar ('Emmi's debts/umbrellas/prices/catalogue/cheese' peak at only ~0.93 on the possessive itself). The local construction is necessary but not sufficient - the identical sentence scores ~0.15 alone and 0.3-0.6 in an unrelated short context versus >2 in a context that has been naming an entity's attributes (peak 2.33) - and the response is retrospective (zero-ablating the pole leaves next-token distributions essentially unchanged, KL ~5e-6, and the histogram is a detector: 84% of tokens below 0.08), while the negative pole is a separate job peaking on verbal predicates and their locative complements ('born and raised in', 'lives in', 'employs', 'competed').
