## Hypothesis

> This pole fires retrospectively on the slot-filler token that completes a short functional frame: the noun completing a determiner or prepositional phrase (The algorithm, a kingdom, the pan), the verb completing a subject or 'to' frame (She opened, to submit), the alias after 'as' (as np), or the variable after a lead-in (If x) — i.e. the word that names the entity or action the clause is about.
