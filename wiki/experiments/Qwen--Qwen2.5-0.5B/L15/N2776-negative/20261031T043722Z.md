## Hypothesis

> This pole fires on a concrete numeric quantity or measure: it peaks on the token that introduces or continues a numeral (in this tokenizer usually the standalone space token that precedes a digit sequence, then the digits themselves), on a spelled-out number, and on the unit/clock/date word that completes the measurement ('5 hours', '250 mg', '7.25 percent', '15 minutes', '1969', '9.30 a.m.'); the same input channel shows up in code on the identifier that completes a numeric binding ('def add(a, b)', 'self.value = value'), which is the same SwiGLU g+u- regime as the numerals. Filling that slot with a vague or non-numeric word ('several hours', 'a record high', 'red', 'unknown'), or using a numeral as a bare label ('The number 5 is prime', 'Chapter 3'), leaves it near zero; it is retrospective (zero-ablating the pole at its peaks moves the next-token distribution by KL ~1e-4, and its output direction promotes metalinguistic words like 'described', 'suggested', 'notes', 'answer', not digits), and its weaker peaks on month/weekday/'p.m.'/'days'/'years' words and state adjectives ('not guilty', 'a positive one', 'golden brown') are a weaker extension of the same 'specific value supplied' pattern rather than a claim that any value word fires.
