## Hypothesis

> The pole is a sparse detector of an explicit numeric quantity in running prose: it peaks on the token that carries the number - a digit of a numeral, a '%' sign, or a magnitude/scale word (hundred, hundreds, thousand, thousands, million, millions, billion, lakh, percent, decade, hour) - when the number states a value on an established scale, such as an age or age bracket ('men aged 25', 'from 15 to 99'), a version or model number ('iOS 6', 'IE8', 'Excel 2007', 'gcc 4.4'), a share ('90% of the media') or a duration ('20 years', '30 minutes').
