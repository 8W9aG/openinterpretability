## Hypothesis

> Retrospectively, the pole fires on the token that introduces a specific, narrow lexical value into a slot the preceding words have opened: the first piece of a rare or technical word or proper name (' sym'+'phony', ' am'+'oxic'+'illin', ' Ham'+'let', ' March', ' Celsius') or the bare leading token that Qwen's tokenizer splits off a numeral ('at 100', 'active = 1', 'Apollo 11'). It sits near baseline on high-frequency generic fillers in the same slot ('the concert', 'active = true'), on the words that set the slot up, and on ordinary prose; within the rare-word class the response is selective (penicillin, ibuprofen and xylophone stay at baseline), and a second, much larger response family on the copula after an expletive subject ('it is', 'it's', up to 7.8 in running text) is not explained by this claim.
