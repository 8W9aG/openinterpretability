## Hypothesis

> The pole is a retrospective detector for tokens that complete or establish a relational/structural link between two semantic units. It fires on prepositions (on, to, from), conjunctions (and), and terminating punctuation (., ,) when they connect a preceding element to a following one - e.g., 'sat [[on]] the mat', 'SELECT name [[FROM]] users', 'A and [[B]]', 'sentence [[.]]'. The input attribution (50% on peak token) and token identity confound (R²=0.63) are consistent with this: the neuron responds to the token's identity in a connecting structural position, not to any particular semantic content of the linked elements. The Jacobian lens promoting action verbs (draw, soak, kill) reflects what the pole writes to the residual stream downstream, not what it detects.

- **Activating tokens:** ` on`, ` add`, ` FROM`, ` to`, ` and`, `.`, `,`
- **Mechanism claimed:** retrospective
