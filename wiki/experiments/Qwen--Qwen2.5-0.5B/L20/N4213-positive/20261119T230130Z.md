## Hypothesis

> Fires on the finite verb, auxiliary or modal (and more weakly on the rest of its verb group, e.g. 'been', 'be', an infinitive) that directly follows a grammatically PLURAL subject noun phrase or plural pronoun (charges had, rhinos have, travaux doivent, they had, changes will take). Activity depends on the number of the subject's head noun, not on the nearest noun or the verb's own form: the same verb after a singular subject ('no charge had', 'the key to the cabinets had') stays near zero, and the output promotes singular-agreement forms (' has', ' is', ' does'), so it acts as a plural-subject agreement signal at the verb slot.
