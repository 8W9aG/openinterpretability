## Hypothesis

> The pole reads the onset of a Graeco-Latin word: it is strongest (about 17-21) on the bound word-piece that carries the Latin stem - ' mal'/' Mal', ' ben'/' bene'/' benef' - when the tokenizer splits it off the front of a longer word (malnutrition, maligned, malpractice, Benitez, benevolent), and it fires at 2-11 on whole word-initial tokens of the same learned stock (mathematical, medical, dental, linear, benign, malign, medicinal, molecular, neural). It is silent for ordinary, short or common words and for general academic vocabulary (normal, formal, hospital, benefit, biology, chemistry, analysis) and for the same letters buried inside one longer token (malaria, malfunction, Malta, mall), so it is neither an orthographic, a frequency nor a register detector.
