## Hypothesis

> N380 is a formal-schooling unit: its pre-activation is a signed 'is this context about school and study' score, so the negative pole is exactly zero on schooling vocabulary and through clauses that are about schooling (school, university, college, campus, student, teacher, professor, faculty, lecture, exam, degree, tuition, classes, dropped out of school), and it goes negative everywhere else. The negative pole therefore fires once the text is off the schooling topic and grows with the distance from it - at or near zero (<=0.08) in schooling clauses and abstract academic prose, 0.04-0.28 in short everyday sentences, ~0.1-0.44 in encyclopedic/technical prose, 0.27-0.50 in narrative fiction, literary, markup or code text, and up to 0.80 on a single non-school word that breaks a schooling clause ('hospital' in 'She attended classes every morning at the hospital' reaches the pole's maximum, while 'university' in the same slot scores 0.00).
