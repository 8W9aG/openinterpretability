## Hypothesis

> The pole fires when the text poses an open question that wants an answer - an interrogative clause, direct ('Is there a ...', 'How do I ...', 'Where is it?', 'What else should I ...') or embedded ('I would like to know where ...'), in English or another language ('Cuál es ...'), most strongly when the question is about a problem the writer is stuck on. It follows the question and not its surface tokens: it peaks over the question's opening auxiliaries or wh-word rather than at the '?', matter-of-fact questions with no problem behind them ('Is the sky blue?', 'Could you pass the salt?') and declarative paraphrases of the same content ('Is there a way to fix this?' 2.8 vs 'There is a way to fix this.' 0.8) stay low - except a 'way/solution to fix it' offered right after a described failure, which fires as hard as a question.
