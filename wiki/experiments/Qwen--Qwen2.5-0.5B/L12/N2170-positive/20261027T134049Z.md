Revision of [20261022T090411Z](20261022T090411Z.md) (NEEDS_REVISION 0.82: sensitivity 1.00
but specificity 0.64). The old claim said bare quotes after `the word` or `said` and
file-name pointers to README/AUTHORS stay near zero; its own hard negatives fired at 0.694,
0.634, 0.502 and 0.642. The revision therefore drops those carve-outs: it claims the pole
fires at the onset of *any* reproduced string — opening quote mark plus the first word of a
direct quotation regardless of introducer, the all-caps licence constants
LICENSE/COPYING/COPYRIGHT (and more weakly AUTHORS/CONTRIBUTORS/NOTICE) when the text
points at the file — and adds a person-identification branch (the period in an initial like
`W.C.`, a personal name in a kinship predicate). The rescan used the current corpus sample
(411 docs, 199,996 tokens; max 1.324), so the activations are not directly comparable with
the 2026-10-22 run.

Result: **VERIFIED 0.85** — 11/13 hard negatives, 6/7 holdouts, 10/10 counterfactuals,
specificity repaired from 0.64 to 0.85 while sensitivity stayed 0.86, never-active
baseline 0.49. The holdouts that carry it include a full attributive lead-in
(`told the assembled journalists "Yes, we…"`, 1.86), a cliché list introduced by
`saying things like` (1.11), a colon lead-in (1.07), a `COPYING` pointer (0.90), a kinship
predicate (0.66) and a quotation buried ~750 tokens deep in a formal inquiry (0.34).

The three failures mark real edges. `His parents, William and Agnes, drove him to the
airport` fires at 0.86, so the person-identification branch is broader than "kinship
predicate" — a plain appositive name list is enough. A text-initial quotation
(`"Yes, the shipment came in this morning."`) fires at 0.62, contradicting the claim's
exclusion of quotations with no preceding narration. And the mis-typed `”` opener falls
just short (0.26 against 0.331), so that trigger is weaker than the claim states.

As with the L4/N3659− audit, the probe verdict is much stronger than the held-out
explanation scores (detection 0.56 at TPR 0.24/FPR 0.12, fuzzing 0.62, AUROC 0.53), so a
judge reading only the written claim cannot find the windows. Both reviewer and proposer
were `external/deepseek-flash[1m]`, so this revision is self-reviewed and an independent
re-review is the natural follow-up; the text-initial and appositive-name failures are the
probes it should start from.
