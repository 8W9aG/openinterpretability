## Hypothesis

> The negative pole is a clause-completion detector for running expository prose: it fires on the token where a sentence that has just made a specific statement ends, either the sentence-final period or the comma that opens an exemplifying continuation of the noun phrase just read ('such as Gouda, Parmesan, or goat cheese', ', at 22 percent', ', including ...'). It stays near zero on punctuation internal to a phrase (list commas, decimal points, abbreviations), on the periods of one- and two-word fragments ('Table. Chair. Lamp.'), and on plain short sentences that state nothing specific ('The cat sat quietly on the old wooden mat', 'Bananas are yellow'); the material after the junction never changes it.
