## Hypothesis

> The negative pole fires on the token that closes an insertion spliced into a running structure: the ')' (or '!)', '])', '),') that ends a parenthetical aside, the second '—'/'--' of a dash-bracketed aside, or the newline that ends an in-line comment spliced into a line of code — i.e. exactly the point where the interrupted clause, sentence or code line resumes (the resumption word right after the closer often carries a smaller echo of the same activation). It stays at the floor when the same words are written flat, for comma-delimited asides (4 to 10 times weaker), for parentheses that belong to the clause itself such as notation ('f(x)', 'sin(x)') or labels ('Fig. 4', 'Table 2'), and for code lines with no comment, while inside a real aside the amplitude is graded — largest when the inserted material carries a qualifying fact or number, smallest for a bare single-word label or adverb.
