## Hypothesis

> Fires on the first piece of a word the tokenizer has split, a short fragment that is not a word by itself and must be continued by a suffix piece. It is strongest when that fragment is a lone r/R or w/W (also U), especially right after a hyphen or slash ('re-r|outed', 'most-w|atched', 'nerve-r|acking', ' R|OLLBACK', ' UR|GENCY', ' r|ica', 'men/w|omen'), and also fires on short non-word stems such as ' rep|umping', ' typ|ologically', ' sw|ivel', ' Str|asbourg'. It is near zero on complete single-token words (' top', ' rebooting', ' ENERGY'), on the continuation pieces themselves, on fragments starting with most other letters (' p|obre', '-h|ated', '-s|orted', ' H|ORIZONS'), and is weaker when the same word already appeared earlier in the text. Unlike the positive pole, which fires partway into broadcaster and other proper names, this pole tracks the opening fragment of an unfinished common word. A secondary case is ' Best' opening an award category ('nominated for Best Picture').
