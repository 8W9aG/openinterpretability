## Hypothesis

> The pole is a retrospective word-piece (spelling-fragment) detector: it fires when the current token is one of a small family of short subword fragments - the two-letter onsets ' ch', ' st', ' Ch', ' St', ' sh', ' ph' and mid-word pieces such as 'ph' in triphosphatase - i.e. a piece the tokenizer has split out of a longer word rather than a complete word. In a fixed carrier the fragment token alone sets the strength (' ch' 7.2 > ' st' 4.4 > ' sh' 2.0 > ' ph' 1.3 > ' pl' 0.8 > ' br' 0.6, identical across chisel/chive/chasm/chaff), and the pole stays at its floor on words held in one token (cheese, church, station, strong, photon, stanza, child), on longer fused pieces carrying the same letters (' perm' in permanganate, ' Neb' in Nebuchadnezzar, ' chlor', ' chem', ' Chunk' in getChunk), and on function words and punctuation.
