## Hypothesis

> Fires retrospectively when the current token is strongly licensed as the onset of a unit by its left-hand frame: a sentence-initial animate-pronoun subject or code/document keyword, a quantity or time slot after a reporting or measure phrase, or the first piece of a fixed term or formula. Peak strength scales with how constraining the frame is, so the same token fires weakly or not at all in a weak frame; it does not prepare any specific upcoming token (Jacobian/logit lenses agree, kurtosis ~3.4, promoted list is incoherent rare tokens) and its output direction instead suppresses frequent format tokens.
