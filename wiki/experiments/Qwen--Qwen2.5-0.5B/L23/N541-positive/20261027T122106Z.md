## Hypothesis

> At the first position of the input this pole is a graded opener detector: code openers (class/import/def) drive 7-9.5, connective or article openers (The/But/Because/Sh) drive 1-3, and pronouns, question words and most other openers drive ~0, while the same tokens placed mid-sentence in plain English prose evoke ~0. Within quoted dialogue and French spans it instead stays active across many tokens and spikes on straight-punctuation-adjacent fragments: the elided pronoun in j'habite (9.7, versus 3.3 for unelided il and 0 for the same elision in an English frame), the auxiliary did after she inside quotes (2.4), and mid-word pieces such as ochond in Mitochondria; its output direction promotes ASCII structural tokens and strongly suppresses curly-quote tokens, consistent with marking spans where straight-ASCII punctuation applies.
