## Hypothesis

> Fires on an opening or boundary token after which a fresh word will start with no leading space: strongest (10-12) when such a non-word token is the very first token of the text ('[', '\n', '\n\n', '---\n', '{\n', '"'), and moderately (3-10) mid-text on a line or paragraph break that ends a title, question or label (':\n\n', '?\n\n', '.\n') or on an opening ' (' / ' [' / ' "' that starts a parenthetical or quote. It is near zero on word tokens, including a first token that is a word ('The', 'So'), and on any token followed by a space-prefixed word (e.g. ',' or ' ' in place of the break). Its output promotes spaceless word-start fragments ('sc', 'sm', 'g', 'gl') as a broad class, not specific tokens.
