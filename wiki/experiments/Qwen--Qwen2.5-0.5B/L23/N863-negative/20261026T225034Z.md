## Hypothesis

> This negative pole fires retrospectively on social-media/chat-domain tokens (chat, tweet/tweets, Facebook, Twitter): a lone 'chat' already drives strength ~2.3 and stacks ('chat chat chat', 'Facebook Twitter tweets') drive it to 7-10, far above the scan max of 1.57, so the scan corpus, which contains no social tokens, caught only weak baseline flickers.
