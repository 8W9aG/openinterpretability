"The chef imposes a rule…" (9.51) suggests that any `imposes a` primes it, whatever follows. "The doors were locked, the curtains…" (4.28) echoes the `locked … locked` example. A revision could narrow the claim to "restriction-imposition context" and not require a constraint word as the likely continuation.
