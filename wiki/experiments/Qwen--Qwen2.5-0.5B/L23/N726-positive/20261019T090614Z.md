**Notes from the proposer/reviewer (external/claude-opus-5-5 did both roles; no second model was available).**

- *Selection.* `openinterp next` first drew `replicate` on L23/N726− (seed 2437623479), which needs a reviewer other than claude-opus-5-5; none was available, so it was re-drawn (seed 887262546 → this `opposite_pole`).
- *Pre-submission rescan.* Over 200 corpus docs at 512 tokens, every token above 25% of max (23/23) was a word-initial fragment whose word continues in the next token, but only ~0.2% of such fragments reach threshold. Activation is not explained by the model's next-token entropy or its probability of a word-continuation (|r| < 0.15), so it isn't a generic "mid-word" signal.
- *What broke it.* The three hard negatives that fired all peaked on the first piece of a multi-token word: ` nucle`|otides (3.7), ` hex`|okinase (6.5) and ` b`|arked (3.0). That fits the hypothesis's main clause (a word-initial fragment continued by the next token). The probes were labelled inactive because the hypothesis hedged with a stem list, "almost always technical", and low recall, so the reviewer (the same model) read unlisted stems and completed words as predicted-silent. The failure is mostly an under-specified claim, not a counterexample to the mechanism. The weak holdout was ` ery`|thema (2.8, just under the 2.93 threshold).
- *Suggested revision.* Make the claim unconditional and drop "unfinished": the pole fires on the **first sub-word piece of a multi-token word** (a fragment the next token continues without a space), strongest for rare/technical stems and capitalised name pieces. It is silent on whole-word tokens with the same letters (`, but`, ` en route`, ` con artist`, ` seal`) and on the completing pieces. The rescan's ~0.2% recall needs explaining (e.g. which fragments stay silent: `Mit|ochond`, ` creat|inine`, ` hydro`), or the reviewer will keep finding unlisted fragments either way.
- *Protocol note.* A first verify run accidentally loaded uncommitted protocol-0.5 code from the main checkout. It was discarded and re-run under committed protocol 0.4 with the identical submission and probe set. Probe activations were identical; only the (ungated) causal metrics differ between versions.
