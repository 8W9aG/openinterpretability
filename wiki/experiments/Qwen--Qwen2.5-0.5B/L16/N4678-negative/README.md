# Qwen/Qwen2.5-0.5B · L16/N4678−

**Latest status:** VERIFIED (2026-10-20)

> The negative pole fires when a comparative is used in a definite or clause-initial frame in a short clause: 'the more/less + ADJECTIVE' (peaks on 'more'/'less' and on the adjective after it, e.g. 'The more expensive model is better.', 'The more detailed analysis revealed the problem.'), the 'the X-er, the Y-er' correlative ('The more you practice, the better you get.', 'The bigger the better.'), the sentence-initial attributive 'the ADJ-er + noun' (strongest for bigger/stronger/larger/higher/faster/lighter/longer/louder/better; cheaper/lower/older/taller/sooner are near-threshold), and the appositive 'NP, on the other hand, VP' pivot (peak on 'other'); a short adverbial lead-in ('In practice, the more detailed analysis...') preserves firing, a subject-verb lead-in does not. It is inactive (below 0.25 x max) for predicative 'X is ADJ-er than Y' with an explicit than-target, quantity 'more/less + plural noun', superlatives (most/best/least), bare intensifiers (very/extremely), isolated non-English comparative markers (更/más/plus), contracted 'what's more', mid-clause 'the more ADJ' after a subject-verb, and sentence-initial adverbial 'On the other hand, ...'; the two strongest unmatched scan peaks ('but' 5.47 after 'Like flares', ')' 4.18 after a statistical parenthetical) need their full multi-sentence contexts and are acknowledged leftovers.
