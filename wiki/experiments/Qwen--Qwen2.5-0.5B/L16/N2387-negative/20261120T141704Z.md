## Hypothesis

> N2387- fires on a coordinating conjunction (`and`, `or`) and, more weakly, on the clause-linking punctuation (`,`, `—`, `?`) exactly where it continues an interrogative construction: a direct question, or an embedded complement clause opened by how/why/what/whether/if/when/where — i.e. the slot where a second sub-question or a second alternative of the same question is added.
