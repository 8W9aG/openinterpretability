## Hypothesis

> This pole is a retrospective, graded detector of conventionalized multi-token sequences: activation rises across runs of formulaic text (idioms like 'upon a', verb-selected prepositions/articles like 'boils at' and 'flooded the', collocations like 'interest rates', code/HTML idioms like 'add(a, b)', and subword completions like 'Photos+ynthesis'), peaking on the most locally constrained joint. The same function word in the same syntactic slot stays near zero when the surrounding frame is not formulaic (e.g. peak ' the' is 0.216 after 'flooded' but 0.000 after 'crossed'), which beats the token-identity confound (69% variance).
