## Hypothesis

> This pole is a retrospective detector for the determiner slot of a prepositional object: it fires on an overt determiner ('a'/'the'/'an' most strongly, and more weakly other determiners such as 'this', 'my', 'that', 'every', 'some' and non-English articles like 'het' or 'del') when that word heads the noun phrase that is the complement of a preposition ('on the ...', 'of the ...', 'at a ...', 'in the ...'). It stays inactive when the same determiner heads a subject NP or a verb's direct object, when it follows a subordinator or complementizer ('that the', 'because the', 'than the', 'like the'), and when the PP slot is filled by a pronoun ('on it') or a bare plural ('on tables'); the content noun after the determiner is irrelevant, it fires the same per token before 'table', 'floor', 'mat' or 'chair'.
