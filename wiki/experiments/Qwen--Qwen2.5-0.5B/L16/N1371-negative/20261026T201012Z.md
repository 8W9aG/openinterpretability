<!-- Free-form notes. This page is never regenerated, so edits here are kept. -->

First experiment on this pole (random draw, audit seed 2132115630; 200k fineweb-edu
tokens read as 512-token windows; max activation 4.53). The claim is a word-family
detector for equality/equivalence and quantitative comparison — `relationship` (the scan
top, 5.13), ` equal` in every sense, ` equality`, ` exponential`, ` unequal`, the `qu` piece
of *inequity*, and relation nouns like correlation/difference/comparison — explicitly
excluding other `equ` words, the fairness/justice lexicon, generic comparison words,
non-statistical `independence`, and social-identity compounds (`gender/racial equality`).
The review verifies it at 0.82 — 12/13 hard negatives, 5/7 holdouts, 10/10 counterfactuals,
sufficiency 0.88 and necessity 0.91 — and the held-out explanation scores are unusually
strong (detection 0.88, fuzzing 0.88, AUROC 0.71).

The misses both mark the edge of the exclusion list. `Solve the quadratic equation`
(1.173) fires although `equation` is explicitly excluded, so the quantitative-relation half
of the family is broader than the exclusion list allows; and two genuine family uses fall
under the 1.133 threshold — `equal weights of butter and flour` in a recipe (0.745) and a
long glassmaking passage whose `correlation`/`relationship` sit deep in context (1.073,
missing by 0.06). Cross-genre and long-context deployments of the family are therefore
weaker than the calibrated examples.

Token identity explains R² 0.47 here (the highest in this batch), but the hypothesis still
beats the never-active baseline (0.60 vs 0.82). Steering is a null. Proposer and reviewer
were the same model, so the record is self-reviewed.
