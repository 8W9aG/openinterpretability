**Latest status:** REJECTED (2026-10-20)

> The negative pole fires on the separator token that closes the numeric field of a bibliographic reference entry and opens its page/value field - the ':' in 'Magn Reson Med. 2019;81:2551', the comma-plus-space before the page number in 'Magn Reson Med, 81, 2551', or the space in 'Neuroimage. 2018;172: 456' - peaking there and staying elevated across the page digits that follow. The same separator-before-a-formatted-value pattern, weaker, covers inline statistical/technical records ('95% CI = 2.35-15.88', '3*k = 8019') and the '(' that opens a parenthesised result list in a Go-style signature, while the pole stays near zero on the same numbers as ordinary quantities in running prose ('The patient was 81 years old in 2019', 'The values were 2019, 81, 2551'), on values introduced by a copula or label ('The DOI is 10.1002/mrm.27594', 'The p-value was .034'), on author-year citations without a journal name ('Smith et al., 2019, 81, 2551') and on references described in words ('published in 2019, volume 81, pages 2551 to 2565').
