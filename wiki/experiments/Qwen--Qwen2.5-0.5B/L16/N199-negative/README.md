> Fires on a whole English word in running prose that breaks a strong local collocational expectation. The slot follows a modifier, determiner, possessive or preposition that strongly predicts one continuation (their hard -> work, their bedside -> manner, set of reference -> pictures, peer-reviewed -> journal, caused by a -> number), and a different real word fills it instead: a typo-word or malapropism (beside, reverence, world), a semantically clashing noun (banana, waste), or a construction that drops the article (by number of). The pole stays near zero on the expected word itself, on surprising words in unconstrained slots (e.g. 'her favorite mud/hat', 'planted sermons'), and on surprising subword fragments, names, punctuation and non-English text, so it is not a plain surprisal or token-identity detector.
