## Hypothesis

> This pole is a retrospective detector for length/composition specifications of an account or identity credential field: it ramps up through clauses like 'password/PIN/username/passphrase must/should/needs/has (contain | have | be) (at least | exactly) N ...' and peaks on the quantity or complement token (eight, four, least, characters, number). Identical requirement wording with a non-credential subject (recipe, story, meal, contract, committee) evokes only weak activity, and the specific quantity word does not matter. Its output direction strongly suppresses name-related tokens, consistent with firing in credential-field contexts while suppressing name prediction; weak responses on other specifying complements (guilty, light, WHERE) are acknowledged offshoots.
