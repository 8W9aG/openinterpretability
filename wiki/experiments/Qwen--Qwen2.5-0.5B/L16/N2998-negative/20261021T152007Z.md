A new project still begins with a block of text that nobody reads closely but everybody keeps, because removing it is the one mistake that no review will forgive. The words are copied from project to project with the names changed, which is exactly how a fixed template survives: not because anyone understands it, but because everyone copies it.

Printed books follow a similar logic. The front matter of a novel is not written by the author and is not read by most readers, yet it appears in the same order, with the same claims and the same reservation of rights, in hundreds of thousands of titles a year.
