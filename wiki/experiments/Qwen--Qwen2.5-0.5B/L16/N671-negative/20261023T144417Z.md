## Hypothesis

> The negative pole fires at the point where a clause ties something to a hazard: the 'from'/'against' that introduces the source a defence predicate holds off ('protects the rider from injury', 'hardening federal buildings against threats', 'guards the village against floods', 'shields the heart from damage'), the 'by'/'with' that introduces the agent of a harmful state ('endangered by hunting', 'threatened by habitat loss', 'damaged by frost', 'breached by attackers', 'tampered with'), 'to' after 'vulnerable', and the negating element of an elaborated permission/prohibition notice ('may be reproduced ... without the prior written permission', the non-exclusive copyright grant).
