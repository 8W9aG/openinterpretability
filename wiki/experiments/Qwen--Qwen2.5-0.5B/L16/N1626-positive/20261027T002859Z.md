## Hypothesis

> The positive pole is a retrospective, graded detector of clauses that fix how something is to be done, measured, provided or constrained: formal method, parameter, standard and paradigm specifications drive it hardest (peaks 2-3.5, e.g. post hoc Tukey, incubated at 37 C for 30 min, visual search paradigm with 12 distractors), legal and schedule rules moderately (~1), and everyday imperative procedures with measured parameters (recipes, dosages, build commands) plus specification sentences buried in long narrative only weakly (0.25-0.65) yet still above baseline. It stays at incidental-mention levels when the same words are merely reported, remembered or narrated without enacting a specification -- past-tense news, memoir and meta-mention such as Temperatures hit 37 C across the region, post hoc which he had learned in a statistics course, or version 1.0 had sold badly -- so everyday procedures count as weak in-scope positives while reported measurements and mentioned terms count as negatives.
