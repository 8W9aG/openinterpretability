## Hypothesis

> Fires retrospectively at the onset of a new line or sentence: activation peaks on the first token after a line break, an indentation run or sentence-final punctuation (indented markup attributes such as ' android' and ' app', sentence-initial 'I'/'We'/'In'/'Please' right after '.\n\n', and the token that completes a literal '\n' escape in code), while it stays near zero mid-line (18% of line-initial tokens exceed 0.2 versus 3% of all others). The same neuron's opposite pole instead fires on numerals and ordinals (' two', ' second', ' first'), so quantity tokens are what this pole suppresses; a residual, unexplained peak also occurs on the contraction 've after 'I'.

- **Activating tokens:** ` android`, ` app`, `I`, ` We`, `In`, `The`, `Please`, `\\n`, `.`
- **Mechanism claimed:** retrospective
