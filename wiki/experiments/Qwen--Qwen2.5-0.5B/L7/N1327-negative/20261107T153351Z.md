## Hypothesis

> The pole fires on an emphatic anaphoric demonstrative - 'that' used as pronoun or determiner, 'this', 'those', or the free relative 'what' - when it picks out a specific entity, action, kind, reason or degree already given in the preceding text and is focused by an immediately preceding focus particle ('just', 'exactly', 'precisely', 'only', 'simply', 'literally', 'even'), as in 'did exactly that', 'for precisely that reason', 'exactly what I need', 'just that kind of afternoon'; the peak sits on the demonstrative itself. It is a demonstrative-reference detector, not an exactness or 'that'-token detector: the same frame falls four- to ten-fold, to or below the activity line, when the demonstrative becomes the article or a plain pronoun ('exactly the reason', 'exactly the kind', 'precisely it'), when the focus particle is deleted ('did that', 'what she needed') or replaced by a non-focus adverb ('certainly that reason'), and when 'that' is a complementizer or cleft expletive ('said that he would', 'then that the train') or 'what' is interrogative rather than a free relative ('know exactly what she needed').
