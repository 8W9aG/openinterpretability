## Hypothesis

> The pole fires on a hand-held implement - a tool, weapon, musical instrument, eating or writing implement, or portable device - presented as an object in a person's hands: it peaks on the implement noun itself (chisel, hammer, rifle, camera, knife, guitar, wrench), is strongest with grip and manipulation verbs (grip, swing, pick up, carry, raise, clean, holster) and weakest when the same noun is only mentioned, owned or banned. It falls to baseline for non-implement graspables in identical frames (food, rope, stones, boxes, clothing), furniture and abstract nouns, stays sub-threshold for vehicles and machines even when operated, and fires at roughly half strength on the third-person possessive that introduces a handled object ('picked up his ...', 'raising his/her ...') even when that object is not an implement.
