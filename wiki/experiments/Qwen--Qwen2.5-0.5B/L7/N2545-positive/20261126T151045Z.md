## Hypothesis

> Fires on the word right before an expected body-part noun (mostly hand, finger, foot, arm or shoulder). This is usually a side or selector word (left, right, other, both, index, bare) or a possessive after 'with'/'across', in a context where a person or agent's own limb is the next thing named, e.g. 'with his [left] hand', 'with her [index] finger', 'with the [other]' after 'one hand'. It depends on that anticipation and not on the noun that actually follows ('his left pen' fires as much as 'his left hand'). The same words fire weakly or not at all when they introduce a non-body noun ('the left lane', 'the index page', 'the other team', 'on the other hand').
