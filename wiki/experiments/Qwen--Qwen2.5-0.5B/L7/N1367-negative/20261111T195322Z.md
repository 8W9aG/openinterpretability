## Hypothesis

> The pole fires at the closed-class word through which a noun phrase is given a property, quantity or kind rather than identified as an individual: at ' of' (and the determiner immediately after it) when the head noun is an abstract measurable or relational property (position, speed, displacement, relocation, height, weight, temperature, name), and at 'other', 'another', 'a' or 'an' when the noun phrase is an indefinite, kind-referring human noun in object or complement position ('other people', 'other teachers', 'a school', 'with other people').
