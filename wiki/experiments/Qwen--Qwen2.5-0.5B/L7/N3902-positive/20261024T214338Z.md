## Hypothesis

> Retrospective: the pole fires at the onset of a main clause that resumes after a fronted dependent element (a participial phrase, prepositional phrase, if/when-clause or appositive) that closes just before the peak, usually at a comma but often without one, and it peaks on the resumed clause's opening auxiliary/modal/wh-word or subject pronoun (' could', ' would', ' can', ' may', ' what', ' who', ' is', ' I', ' we', ' has', "'ve") or its first predicate word (' possible', ' suggest', ' critical'); the fronted element can be as short as two tokens and long prefaces neither help nor hurt (measured delta -0.02), so the pole is not context-length dependent. Firing is strongest when the resumed clause is a question, request, suggestion, or possibility/obligation/evaluation statement ('could you please', 'is it possible to', 'I suggest', 'it is critical that'); the same tokens continuing rather than resuming a clause (embedded 'what', mid-clause 'would', object 'I'), coordination and serial-list commas, sentence-starting clauses with no fronted element, and code/escape text all stay near zero, and the Jacobian lens is flat (kurtosis 3.5, agreement 0%) with a suppressed list of backslash/escape tokens that exactly mirrors the negative pole's promoted list, so this is a prose clause-resumption feature, not a lookahead or code-region unit.
