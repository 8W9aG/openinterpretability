## Hypothesis

> N1756+ is a retrospective list-boundary detector: it fires on the separator that closes one member of an enumeration - ',' ' and' ' or' and their cross-lingual counterparts '、' '・' 'や' 'と' ' und' ' и' ' و' '와' 'และ' - when the material before it ends an item being listed alongside comparable items (entities, names, parts, materials, products, parameters, numbers), and it is strongest when the set is announced earlier in the sentence ('the following:', 'both', 'two', 'such as', 'including', 'compared', or a series already in progress), while a bare two-item 'A and B' is only weak to moderate. It is predicted inactive on the same tokens when they are not item boundaries: conjunctions that add a second clause or predicate ('The rain stopped and the crowd went home'), subordinators (but, so, although, then, while), commas after an introductory phrase or after a subject NP ('In the morning, ...', 'The guide, Maria, ...'), clause splices, and digit-group commas ('1,500'); its negative pole takes those complementary cases (e.g. 'collapsed and died').
