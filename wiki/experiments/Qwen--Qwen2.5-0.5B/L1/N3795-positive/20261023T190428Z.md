## Hypothesis

> This pole is a retrospective detector whose strong responses are confined to a memorized token set: the mid-sentence FALL forms fell/fall/falls/falling (fallen and synonyms like rose, dropped, collapsed stay near zero) and the capitalized pronouns She/He/They (lowercase she, and I/It, stay near zero), firing on the token itself in any frame. A subject-gated component additionally fires otherwise-silent past verbs (got/saw/knew/took) only after a firing pronoun subject, scaling with that subject's strength (got: 0.68 after She, 0.47 after He, 0.33 after They, 0 after I or in neutral frames); weaker context-dependent responses on determiners, complementizers and participles (the/that/stored/around) fall outside this claim.
