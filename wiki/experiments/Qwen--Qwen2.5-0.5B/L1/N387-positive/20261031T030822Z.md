## Hypothesis

> This pole fires retrospectively on the current token when the token belongs to its preferred learned subset of familiar short content tokens, numerals/quantifiers, and fixed-phrase or clause-linking words (door, Paris, two, least, half, revenue, because, opened), with the same token gated by the preceding token (quickly is silent after 'is' at 0.017 but active after 'door' at 0.164); it stays near-silent on other same-slot tokens (fresh for two, elephant for because, quickly for Paris). It is the complement of the negative pole, which takes a different subset of content words in phrase-internal slots: this pole fires where that one is silent, covering both familiar completions and structural/function tokens (the, of, because), and its peak-token-dominated attribution (75% on the peak token, token identity 87% of variance) with an incoherent Jacobian lens (kurtosis 2.9) marks it as a detector, not a predictor.
