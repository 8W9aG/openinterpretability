## Hypothesis

> This pole fires retrospectively on the current token when it is a content word (concrete noun, lexical verb, or a word-piece of one) filling a phrase-internal content slot, e.g. the head noun of a noun phrase ('sea level', 'the dog', 'your report') or a lexical verb after its subject ('river flooded'), and stays near-silent on function words (determiners, prepositions, conjunctions) in any slot. Within a fixed slot it is selective to a learned token subset rather than to the part of speech (dog but not wolf/chair, flooded but not danced, Ham but not plays), and the same token is gated by the preceding context ('table' fires after 'sea' but not after 'wrote' or 'on the').
