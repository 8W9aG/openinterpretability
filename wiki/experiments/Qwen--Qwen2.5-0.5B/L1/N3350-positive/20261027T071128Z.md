First experiment on this pole (random draw, audit seed 3839284342; 200k fineweb-edu
tokens read as 512-token windows; max activation 2.25). This is a layer-1 pole, and the
claim is a place-context detector: national/institutional or civic content words (`Federal`,
`National`, `nation`, `America`, `district`, `campus`, `restaurant`, `church`…) fire when a
country, region, continent, city or nationality appears anywhere earlier in the passage,
against 0.0–0.6 for the same sentence with a person, brand or generic location in that
slot. The review reaches 0.70 — 5/6 holdouts fire, but only 8/14 hard negatives stay below
the 0.561 threshold — so the verdict is NEEDS_REVISION, and the never-active baseline is
0.37.

The problem is internal to the claim. The hypothesis itself lists place-free baselines of
0.6–0.9 for `National`, `nation`, `restaurant`, `state` and `university` — *above* the
active threshold — and the review's failures are exactly those words in their low frames:
`the Federal memorandum` (0.657), `the National register` (0.722), `the restaurant near the
office` (0.591), `the mobile clinic visits the county` (0.565) and `the state of the art`
after Turin (0.848). The relative place-context effect the proposer measured (1.0–2.2 vs
0.0–0.6) is real, but the binary low/high split cannot hold when the low band crosses the
active line.

One holdout also misses narrowly: `the restaurant near the Lisbon waterfront… not the
oldest in Portugal` (0.533, 0.03 short). Causal sufficiency is 0.97 and necessity 1.00;
held-out explanation scores are weak (detection 0.52, fuzzing 0.58, AUROC 0.51). Proposer
and reviewer were the same model, so the record is self-reviewed.
