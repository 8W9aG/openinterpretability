# Qwen/Qwen2.5-0.5B · L1/N12−

[← wiki home](../../../../README.md)

**Latest status:** VERIFIED (2026-10-20)

> A layer-1 bigram detector that fires on the second token of a few specific two-token pairs: most often the host token 'www' right after a URL scheme 'http://' or 'https://' (weak for a bare 'www', a missing scheme or most other subdomains such as 'docs' or 'mail'); also ' in'/' inside' right after a form of 'look' ('look in', 'looking in'; weak for ' at', ' into', or ' in' after other verbs); and 'L' right after 'OR' (as in 'ORL1'). The same tokens elsewhere, and the tokens before or after them, stay near zero.
