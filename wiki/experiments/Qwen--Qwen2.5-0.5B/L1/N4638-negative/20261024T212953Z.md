## Hypothesis

> The pole retrospectively detects a contextually licensed continuation: it fires when the current token is the filler that the preceding 1-3 tokens select or oblige (the 'to' in 'According to', the complement in 'two cups', the dialogue tag 'he' after a closing quote, a congruous noun like 'dog' after 'cat ... stared at the', or a subword completion like 'Ham'+'let'), with strength graded by how tightly the local frame constrains that filler. The same token in a non-selecting frame drops sharply ('to' 0.241 to 0.018-0.082) and an incongruent same-slot filler goes near zero, while distant or downstream edits leave firing intact; ablating or reading the weights shows no coherent upcoming-token set (Jacobian kurtosis 3.29, 0% lens agreement), so it reacts to what was read rather than preparing a prediction.
