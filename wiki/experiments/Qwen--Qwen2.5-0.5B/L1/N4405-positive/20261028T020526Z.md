First audit of this layer-1 pole (random draw, audit seed 1502003696; 200k fineweb-edu
tokens as 512-token windows; max 2.51). Token identity explains **66%** of scan variance,
so the claim is token-level by design: the pole fires on the word-final piece of a content
word the tokenizer split across two tokens (`str|aws`, `Con|dit`, `H|oles`, `hammer|head`,
`war|heads`, `sys|root`), with both pieces mattering, and stays low when the same word is
one whole token, two spaced words, or a productive suffix tail. It came out **VERIFIED
0.82**: 12/13 hard negatives, 5/7 holdouts, 10/10 counterfactuals, and it beats the
token-identity baseline (0.60) despite the 66% confound.

The split-word prediction holds on most probes: `seized warheads` 1.14, `hammerhead shark`
1.05, `/usr/include` in a build log 1.01, `the valley` passage 0.81, `wrench's flat head`
0.68, all against the 0.627 threshold; and the whole-token and spaced versions stay low
(`moles` 0.33, `adult` 0.29, `sys root` 0.46, `root cause` 0.12, `the string` 0.08,
`head of the department` 0.32, `proc filesystem` 0.35).

Three failures are worth recording. `He stirred the iced tea with two paper straws` fires
**2.31** although `straws` is a whole word there and the claim predicts floor — either the
tokenizer splits ` straws` differently than the scan's `str|aws`, or the piece follows a
different lexical pattern. And two claimed composites do not fire in fresh contexts:
`The aquatic plants…` 0.12 and `A single sunflower…` 0.06. The claim's inventory therefore
needs the actual tokenisation checked per host word, not just the split-word story.

Held-out explanation scores: detection 0.70 (TPR 0.40, FPR 0.00), fuzzing 0.83 (TPR 0.67),
embedding AUROC 0.64. Self-reviewed (proposer and reviewer both
`external/deepseek-flash[1m]`; the reviewer was re-spawned through the sonnet alias after
a provider 402 on the bare subagent alias). The proposer measured its own counterfactuals.
