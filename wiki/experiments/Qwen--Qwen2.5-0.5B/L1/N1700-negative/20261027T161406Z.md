## Hypothesis

> This is a retrospective layer-1 detector for the preposition/adverbial particle that directly follows a noun or verb naming a hostile or damaging event (fight/fought, war, battle, attack, strike, hit, assault, bombing, raid, siege, shooting, crash, blow, wound, damage, threat, struggle, and the setback words fall/fell, failure/failed, loss, drop, slump); one word of separation still fires at roughly a third of the strength, and the event word alone, at the end of the sentence, does not fire. It peaks on the particle itself and only for target/direction particles (on, to, at, for, against, upon, over, back, down, between, into), not for in/with/from or a non-preposition (the, a, and, was, punctuation), and it stays near zero after a benign head (walk, plan, profit, cost, key, access, decision, answer), so it tracks the adversative-collocation shape rather than the particle token alone (a weaker secondary mode, under 60% of max, also fires on the second token of rare capitalized/continuation pairs such as 'Dwar|ves', 'Bruce| N' and 'Die|-Namics', which this axis does not explain).
