## Hypothesis

> Fires on the token that closes a third-person SINGULAR subject of an upcoming finite verb: most strongly a subject pronoun 'it' (also 'she', 'he', 'everyone', a singular noun head, or 'who'/'that'/'and' whose implied subject is singular), i.e. the subject slot just before singular verb agreement ('is', 'was', 'has', 'doesn't'); it stays near zero when the subject is plural or first/second person (they, we, I, you, plural nouns), on object pronouns ('saw it', 'gave it'), and on the verb itself.
