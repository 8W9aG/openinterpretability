> The positive pole fires at the hinge of a value statement about a definite, measurable attribute of a concrete entity: it peaks on the last token of the attribute phrase, or of the reporting verb that introduces the value ('the stadium's capacity', 'the tower's height', 'the patient's age/gender', 'the male:female ratio', 'the company's beta', 'was recorded as', 'is expected to be'), one token before a form of 'be', 'as', '=', or the opening token of a literal value (a number, an ID or hex string), with strength scaling with how strongly that attribute term fixes the copula/value continuation. It is near zero for direct predication of an entity ('the car is red', 'the boy's mood is happy'), for non-measurable or unanchored attributes ('the arrangement is ready', 'the port number is 8080'), and at predictable non-equative continuations ('New York', 'in terms of'); the value's content never matters, and although the output direction promotes exactly that copula (' as', ' is'; Chinese 为/是) and suppresses relative pronouns ('which/who'), the measured direct ablation effect is small (lookahead KL ~3e-5), so the mechanism claim is retrospective.
