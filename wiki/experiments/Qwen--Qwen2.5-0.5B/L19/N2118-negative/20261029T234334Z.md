## Hypothesis

> Retrospective, current-token-triggered unit (100% of input attribution on the peak token, mean distance 0.0; all measured peaks sit in one SwiGLU g-u+ regime) whose output direction suppresses currency-unit tokens (Jacobian-suppressed: ¥, Rp, €, USD, 美元, Rs, \$, z ~ -7 to -8, kurtosis 4.2, while the promoted list is incoherent). It rides elevated across multi-token quantity/time phrases (e.g. 'percent compared with last year', 'from 9 a.m. to 5 p.m.') yet shuts off to exactly 0.0 on the amount and currency tokens inside an explicit copular price frame ('The price is 12 dollars/USD today': is/1/2/dollars/USD/today all 0.0; breaking the frame with 'saw'/'dog' reactivates them to 0.38-1.03). Acknowledged leftovers: its strongest single-token peaks are often non-quantitative (code ' as' 0.86, loop variable ' i', subword fragments 'yo'/'h'/'re', 'cold', 'because', 'capital'), consistent with token identity explaining 70% of variance — so this is a broadly token-tuned dial with a coherent currency-suppressing output and a genuine quantity-phrase-vs-price-frame dissociation, not a pure quantity detector.
