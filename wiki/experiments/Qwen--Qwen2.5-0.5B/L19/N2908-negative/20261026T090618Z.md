## Hypothesis

> This pole fires retrospectively when the current token is the highly predictable, syntactically selected continuation of the immediately preceding 1-3 tokens (a memorized collocation such as sat->on, fell->to, pleaded->not, announced she->would, theorem->follows, wind->rushed, Apollo->space+digit, galaxy->space+digit): replacing either the trigger or the completion collapses it, weakening the trigger grades it down, and a longer matching left context boosts it. It does not prepare a specific upcoming token (ablation next-token KL ~3.5e-06; swapping the upcoming digits 11->99 leaves the peak unchanged) despite a Jacobian lens pointing at closing quote/period tokens.
