## Hypothesis

> Retrospective: in its main (g+u+) regime the pole fires on a content token that fills a delimited structural slot — prototypically a table cell right after a ' | ' delimiter (strength 1-4, abolished to ~0 when the pipe is swapped for 'and'/',' at equal token length), and also tag-enclosed, parenthesized, or SQL BY-clause/statement-final content, with extra boost for column-header-like words (name/email/col). Its output direction specifically promotes ' | '-family pipe tokens (Jacobian kurtosis 7.7, z>10), pushing continuations toward pipe-delimited structure. A separate weak SwiGLU double-negative (g-u-) regime fires ~0.3 on common function words ('on','to','the'), explaining the scan's low prose tail and the 59% token-identity variance rather than the strong peaks.
