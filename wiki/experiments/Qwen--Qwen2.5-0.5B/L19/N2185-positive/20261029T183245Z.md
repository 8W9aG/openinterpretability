## Hypothesis

> L19/N2185+ is a lookahead adjective neuron: it peaks on the token at which the next word (or the next word after a determiner, e.g. 'described as " a dark velvet"') is a descriptive, gradable adjective, whichever syntactic slot announces it - copula or linking verb (is/are/was/be, sounded, tasted, looked), degree adverb (so, most, very, extremely, generally), determiner or possessive opening an attributively modified NP ('its expressive...', 'a web-friendly...'), or a coordinator/comma continuing an adjectival series ('ADJ and', 'ADJ,'). Its transported output direction promotes exactly this class ('crisp', 'soft', 'warm', 'sharp', 'bold'), and activation collapses to near zero when the same surface position instead predicts a noun, verb or manner adverb ('wood and', 'ran smoothly', 'Victorian and'), so it tracks the model's expectation of an upcoming adjective rather than the part of speech already read; the negative pole is not a mirror and stays an order of magnitude weaker.
