## Hypothesis

> L19/N1127+ is a familiarity detector for formulaic web text: it fires while the model reads a span it has stored verbatim from pretraining - site titles, taglines, footers, disclaimers, citation/attribution lines and famous quotations (NASA APOD's 'Discover the cosmos!', British History Online's 'A History of the County of <name>: Volume <n>', the Library of Congress 'Search America's historic newspaper pages from 1789-1924 ...' blurb, hypermail archive footers, 'Published on PsychCentral.com. All rights reserved.', 'First in war, first in peace, and first in the hearts of his countrymen') - and peaks on one token inside that span, usually the closing punctuation or a name/number token in it.
