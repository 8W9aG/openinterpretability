> Fires on a word-internal continuation subword (a token with no leading space, e.g. 'ster', 'mit', 'ux', 'CG', 'ther') whose immediately preceding token is a short word-initial vowel fragment, by far most strongly the letter E in any form ('E', ' E', '-E', '(E', "'e"), at about half strength after 'A'/'O'/'Wa'/'e', weakly after 'I'/'U', and near zero after consonant fragments ('K', 'T', 'Ad', 'Em', 'Al') or on the same subword at word start or after a full word. The pole is silent when the token after 'E' starts a new word (' coli', '.'), so it is driven by the previous-token fragment combined with being mid-word, not by the identity of the continuation token alone.
