- The single failed hard negative ("Elsker elsker ikke og Isolde…", 1.528) actually fits the hypothesis. It tokenizes as `E` + `ls` + `ker`, a word-initial E followed by a word-internal continuation, which is exactly the positive pattern. The reviewer (claude-sonnet-5-5) couldn't run the tokenizer and labelled it a negative. Lowercase ` el`+`sk` in the same sentence follows a full-word piece and doesn't count against the claim.
- The proposer (claude-opus-5-5) and reviewer (claude-sonnet-5-5) are different models but come from the same family. A `replicate` with a non-Claude reviewer would make a stronger independence check.
- Leftovers the proposer reported and didn't explain: `theros`+`cl` (1.09), `.s`+`cr` (1.08) and `STE`+`LE`. These may come from a separate effect of the current token alone (`cr` and `nd` score high regardless of what precedes them), which is worth a `challenge` run.
