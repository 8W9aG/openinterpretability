First audit of this layer-0 pole (random draw, audit seed 2460796282; 200k fineweb-edu
tokens as 512-token windows; max 4.12). Token identity explains **98%** of scan variance —
the most extreme confound in this project — so the hypothesis is deliberately a token-id
claim: the pole detects the token piece that spells `code` in any case, attachment, script
or BPE continuation (`code`/`Code`/`.code`/`代码`/`コード`/`код`, plurals, `coded`/`coding`,
compounds like `bytecode`/`barcode`, and the bare pieces `-ode`/`-odes`/`-oded`), plus the
credit family, with no context effect.

It came out **VERIFIED 0.93** — 13/13 hard negatives, 6/7 holdouts, 9/9 counterfactuals —
and, importantly, it clears the confound gate that this pole's 98% token-identity R² makes
so hard: the token-identity baseline scores 0.79 and the hypothesis 0.93. The positives are
exactly the claimed inventory: `Code names such as Overlord` 4.10, `dress code` 3.50,
`请把这段代码…` 2.99, `Credit unions` 1.36, `encoding` 1.08, and a long mill-house passage
with ` code` buried roughly 400 words deep at **3.47** — a clean confirmation of the claimed
position invariance. The negatives separate pieces from mere letter sequences: `decoded` /
`encoded` 0.94, `vscode` / `urlencoded` 0.92 and `encoder`/`decoder` 0.64 sit below the 1.03
threshold, while software context without the token (Python/Java/SQL 0.47, `debugging` 0.21,
`model`/`mode`/`Unicode`/`nodes` 0.27, `modem` 0.11, `encrypted` 0.05, `credible` 0.14)
stays far below.

The one failure is a tokenisation subtlety worth recording: `The electrodes were rinsed in
distilled water` scores 0.043 although the claim lists the bare `-odes` continuation as
activating. `electrodes` evidently does not expose `odes` as a BPE piece (it likely splits
as `electrode` + `s`), so the piece claim is right but the reviewer's host word does not
produce the piece. Held-out explanation scores are the strongest of the session —
detection 0.90 (TPR 0.80, FPR 0.00), fuzzing 1.00 (7/7), AUROC 0.60 — which is what a
correct token-id claim should look like.

Self-reviewed (proposer and reviewer both `external/deepseek-flash[1m]`); the proposer
measured its own counterfactuals.

Next: a repeat on a different model or a sibling layer-0 neuron would test whether `code`
pieces are a universal early-layer feature, and `electrodes` versus a word that really
tokenises as `odes` is the sharpest tokenisation control.
