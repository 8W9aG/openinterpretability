## Hypothesis

> Layer-0 grapheme detector for the letter h in the token currently being read: the pole fires on any token whose surface string starts with h/H, whether it is a bare or short piece or a hyphen/space/punctuation-prefixed one ('-H' 4.02, '-h' 3.91, 'HP' 3.63, 'H' 3.53, ' H' 3.48, 'h' 2.52, 'hl' 3.12, ' Harvard' 2.26) or the word-initial h that BPE leaves inside a longer word ('historic' 1.60 in 'prehistoric', 'hood' 0.93 in 'boyhood'), reaching 1.1-4.4 of the 4.4 scan max for all but its ~9% most frequent h-words (' have' 0.24, ' his' 0.19, ' here' 0.30, ' how' 0.12, ' however' 0.03), while tokens whose h is not leading stay at the floor (medians 0.11 for h-after-one-letter pieces like ' sh', 'ch', 0.00-0.01 for ' the', 'this', 'which', and 0.000 for tokens with no h at all), with only a weak residual band when h ends a short piece (' unh' 1.5, ' pH' 1.35, 'oh' 0.53).
