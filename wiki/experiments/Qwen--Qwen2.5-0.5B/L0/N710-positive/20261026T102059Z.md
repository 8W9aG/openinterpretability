Mining companies have begun to look at the nodules on those plains, and biologists argue that a community which takes decades to recover from a single scrape should be protected before any machinery is lowered. The team also wants to know how the microbes got there in the first place, whether they drifted down in the water column over millions of years or whether they evolved in place, cut off from the surface since the trench first opened. Answering that question will take more cores, more sequencing and a far longer record than a single cruise can provide.” (activation 0.435)
