## Hypothesis

> A layer-0 token detector for regular '-s' plural word forms (one token ending in plural -s/-es/-ies, whether noun or 3rd-person verb, in prose or code identifiers like .objects, .Arrays, Pages, Sites). It fires hardest on abstract category, collection and place plurals (types, kinds, ways, cities, countries, towns, objects, Arrays, pages, names, styles: above ~0.5 of max) and only weakly on concrete everyday plurals (dogs, cars, cats, items: under ~0.25 of max). It is near zero on the singular of the same word, on irregular plurals (people, children) and on non-plural words ending in s (bus, gas, is), whatever the surrounding context.
