## Hypothesis

> This pole is a retrospective unigram detector for grammatical glue/boundary tokens that hand off to the next constituent: it peaks on the token itself, strongest for the lexically specific verb form ' prescribed', strong for the infinitive/preposition ' to', the indefinite article ' a', and the standalone space token that separates a head from a following number, and moderate for ' at'/' in'/' of', comma, quote, '>' and ' and'. It is silent on content words and on matched function tokens ' the', ' on', ' for', ' with', sentence-final '.', and other past participles such as ' described' or ' given', with surrounding words contributing almost nothing (single-token probes replicate in-context strengths).
