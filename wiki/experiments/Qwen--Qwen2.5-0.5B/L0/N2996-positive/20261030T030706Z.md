## Hypothesis

> The positive pole is a retrospective unigram detector for a clustered family of relational/generic-vocabulary tokens: it fires on the current token when that token is a spatial or relational marker (around, through, within, behind, during), a temporal term (Friday, days, weeks, quarterly, today), a pronoun, number or light auxiliary (she, there, did, five), or a generic relational predicate (began, announced, gave, told, love, sits, runs, guide, support, range, interest, crowd), and stays near zero on semantically narrow concrete nouns (museum, pizza, river, senator, planet, banana) and specific action verbs (wrote, danced, scored, fell, raised). The same neuron's negative pole fires on exactly those concrete-specific tokens (princess, mat, fraud, Neil), confirming the generic-vs-specific distinction it encodes; the boundary is fuzzy (house/table drive it weakly, while time/always/get/every do not), reflecting an embedding-neighborhood detector rather than a crisp semantic class.
