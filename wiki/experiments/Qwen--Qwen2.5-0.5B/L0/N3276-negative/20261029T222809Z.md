## Hypothesis

> This layer-0 negative pole is a retrospective current-token (unigram) detector: its strength is set almost entirely by the identity of the token it sits on (token identity explains 98% of activation variance, 78% of input attribution is on the peak token itself, single-token replacement agrees with attribution at rho=0.97). It fires strongly on an idiosyncratic fixed embedding-defined unigram set including month names (March, June, October, January, February), the pronouns They/it, the copulas is/are, and scattered content tokens (Armstrong, Paris, market, here, import), while staying near zero on most function words (to, the, of, and, in, at, by) and many common nouns (table, dog, book, city). Acknowledged leftovers: no semantic or syntactic generalization covers the actives without counterexamples (proper nouns London/John and pronouns he/you/she are ~0; Monday is weak), both vocabulary lenses are incoherent (Jacobian kurtosis ~3.3, 0% lens agreement) so nothing specific is promoted downstream, and there is weak layer-0-attention context modulation (e.g. ' theorem' fires after 'The' but not after 'Cat').
