## Hypothesis

> The negative pole fires on the content word that a group term attributively qualifies: it peaks on the second element of a compound whose first word names a human group or social category (nationality, ethnicity, religion, race, age, gender, kinship, disability or role), strongest on collective/abstract heads (Muslim history 3.6, black history 9.1, gay community 2.2, refugee families 2.2, young person 2.3, trans woman, white power, Japanese Patent), and it generalises to unseen groups and heads (Kurdish, Roma, Sikh, deaf, immigrant, Catholic, migrant all fire at 1.4-2.5 on unseen heads).
