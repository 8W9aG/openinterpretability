## Hypothesis

> Detects the orthographic stem "deci-" in any language or casing, mainly the decide/decision family (decide, decided, deciding, decision(s), decisive, undecided, indecisive, decidir/decisión, décidé/décision, decisões) plus offshoots that share the letters (decimal, deciliter, decibel, deciduous). It fires on the token that completes the stem, and fires hardest when the stem is split across tokens (Dec+ided, Dec+ide, DEC+ISION, DEC+IMAL, inde+cis, decis+ões; roughly 5-9) rather than held in one whole-word token (' decided', ' decision', ' decimal'; roughly 1.5-4). It stays near zero on other "dec-" words (deceive, decorate, December, declare, decree only ~0.9), on look-alikes (divide, division, divisive, precise, concise) and on synonyms (choose, choice, resolved, agreed).
