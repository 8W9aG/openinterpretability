## Hypothesis

> The negative pole fires on closed-class 'linker' words of two kinds. About 23% of its activation mass is the single token ' to' (the infinitive/complement marker), which reaches strength about 0.4 in nearly any syntactic context, so almost any English sentence containing ' to' peaks just above the scan's active threshold (0.55); far stronger responses (strength 1-4, at or above the scan maximum of 2.2) go to the word that completes a 'for'/'of' name or term phrase - the noun 1-3 tokens after 'for' in a naming frame ('the Institute for Cancer' 3.4, 'for Teachers' 2.7, 'for Class 9' 1.4) or the connective that closes a name head ('Conservation of' 1.7, 'what to expect from' 2.4, 'Polk County Courthouse' 1.1) - while the same nouns outside such a frame stay silent ('the University of Michigan' 0.1, 'for students' 0.04, 'we met Cancer' 0.04). The two modes are separate SwiGLU sign regimes (gate<0,up>0 for ' to'; gate>0,up<0 for the frame completions), and the pole is retrospective: zero-ablating it on a mix of both modes gives KL 3.8e-4 against the next-token distribution, below the 1e-3 lookahead threshold.
