## Hypothesis

> The negative pole fires on closed-class 'linker' words of two kinds. About 23% of its activation mass is the single token ' to' (the infinitive/complement marker), which reaches strength about 0.4 in nearly any syntactic context, so almost any English sentence containing ' to' peaks just above the scan's active threshold (0.55); far stronger responses (strength 1-4, at or above the scan maximum of 2.2) go to the word that completes a 'for'/'of' name or term phrase - the noun 1-3 tokens after 'for' in a naming frame ('the Institute for Cancer' 3.4, 'for Teachers' 2.7, 'for Class 9' 1.4) or the connective that closes a name head ('Conservation of' 1.7, 'what to expect from' 2.4, 'Polk County Courthouse' 1.1) - while the same nouns outside such a frame stay silent ('the University of Michigan' 0.1, 'for students' 0.04, 'we met Cancer' 0.04). ## Discussion

<!-- Free-form notes. This page is never regenerated, so edits here are kept. -->

First audit of this pole (random draw, audit seed 1576174785; 200k fineweb-edu tokens as
512-token windows; max 2.20). Token identity explains R² 0.54, the highest since L0/N869+,
and the claim is a **two-mode linker** account: a weak bare ` to` mode (~0.4 in nearly any
sentence) plus a strong `for`/`of` name-frame mode that fires on the word completing a name
or term phrase (`the Institute for Cancer` 3.4 vs `the school for Cancer` 0.4). It came out
**NEEDS_REVISION at 0.71**: 11/13 hard negatives, 4/7 holdouts, 8/8 counterfactuals,
never-active baseline 0.64 behind the hypothesis.

The name-frame mode works only sometimes. Firing: `Here is what to expect from the new
coach` (2.25), `This Agreement for the Purchase of Goods` (0.79), `They decided to postpone
the meeting until Friday` (0.68) and the Riverton Library passage (0.64). Failing: `The
Center for Disease Control` (0.32), `The Department of Transportation` (0.13) and `The
Commission for Rural Housing` (0.29) — three textbook name frames that stay at floor, so the
`for`/`of` inventory is not reliable.

The ` to` baseline mode also does not behave as stated. The claim says ~0.4 for bare ` to`
and that this "pushes any prose containing to just past the 0.55 threshold", but 0.4 is
below 0.55 — the sentence contradicts itself — and in the probes ordinary sentences with
`to` mostly stay low (postponement 0.68 is the exception). Two negatives fire:
`For more than a decade` (1.14), which the claim's own examples predict *should* fire (a
probe mis-specification rather than a refutation), and `The interviewer asked a question
about the budget` (1.31), which neither mode explains.

Held-out explanation scores are the best for a token-level claim in a while: detection 0.76
(TPR 0.84, FPR 0.32), fuzzing 0.86 (TPR 0.77), though embedding AUROC is 0.43. Self-reviewed
(proposer and reviewer both `external/deepseek-flash[1m]`). A revision should fix the ` to`
wording, enumerate the name-frame inventory against the CDC/DoT/Commission failures, and
treat the `question about the budget` fire as a seed.
