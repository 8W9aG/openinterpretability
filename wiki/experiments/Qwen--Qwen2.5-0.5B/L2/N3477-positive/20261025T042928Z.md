## Hypothesis

> This positive pole is a retrospective, graded detector of two-token binding: it peaks on a token that completes a unit opened by the token immediately to its left, either the next piece of a word the tokenizer split ('As' after 'PD' in PDAs, 'sons' after 'Daw' in Dawsons, 'ius' after 'Maurit', 'us' after 'sa' in sausages, 'va' after 'Pur') or the second element of a multi-token name or fixed expression ('Britain' after 'Great', 'Alberta' after 'University of', 'Violence' after 'Family', 'China' after 'Greater', 'Lakes' after 'Great', 'recently' after 'until', 'out' after 'grew', 'well' after 'may', 'A' after 'David'). The same word after a determiner, preposition or unrelated modifier ('modern Britain', 'the college in Alberta', 'may still', 'came out', lowercase 'violence') sits at or below zero, so the pole tracks how strongly this model binds the pair rather than the token's identity, frequency or surprisal (correlation with the model's own next-token log-probability is -0.005 over 20k natural-text tokens, with entropy -0.03, with token frequency 0.06); it is selective rather than a general compound detector, since 'New York', 'White House' and 'University of Toronto' are inactive while 'Great Britain', 'Great Lakes' and 'University of Alberta' are active.
