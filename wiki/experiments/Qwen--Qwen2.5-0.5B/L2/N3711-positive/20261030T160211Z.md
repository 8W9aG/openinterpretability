## Hypothesis

> Retrospective negation-scope detector. Its strong, general response (about 0.5-2.7) is to the token that sits inside the scope of a negator earlier in the clause (not, n't, never, no, nobody, nothing, without, hardly, cannot): the pole peaks on the polarity-sensitive or light word the negation licenses (any, ever, anything, anyone, at all, all, way, just) and on the light word that continues the negated predicate (to, the, a, like, want, some), and it falls to the floor (<=0.15) when that negator is replaced by an affirmative word (also, often, one, all, with) or when the peak token is replaced by a non-polarity synonym. In affirmative text the pole only reaches a weaker 0.15-0.5 band, on degree/quantifier modifiers (much, more, far, very, too, even, both, lower) and on scattered single content words that complete a familiar phrase (central, rain, increased, captured, of), so text with no negator and none of those tokens is predicted inactive.
