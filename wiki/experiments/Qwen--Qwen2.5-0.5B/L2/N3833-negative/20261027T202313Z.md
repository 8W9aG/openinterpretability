First audit of this pole (random draw, audit seed 2898657548; 200k fineweb-edu tokens as
512-token windows; max 3.53). Token identity explains R² 0.42, and the claim is a
**two-token-chunk** detector: the pole peaks on the second token of a pair the model stores
as one lexical unit — a split-word continuation (` Concor`+`de`, ` disc`+`ursive`,
` dispos`+`als`, ` dis`+`joint`+`ed`, ` part`+`ing`) or a frozen collocation (`do`+`so`,
`do`+`best`, `dispose`+`of`, `differ`+`from`, `similar`+`to`, `Hudson`+`Bay`,
`political`+`will`) — and reads the pair, not the token. It came out **VERIFIED 0.93**:
13/13 hard negatives, 6/7 holdouts, 10/10 counterfactuals, and it beats the trivial baseline
(0.57) despite the 42% token-identity confound.

The pair-reading prediction holds cleanly. Positives: `Place de la Concorde` 2.34,
`do best` 1.83, `asset disposals` 1.58, `disjointed` 1.43, `do so` 1.32, `dispose of` 1.14
(threshold 0.883). Every partner-swapped negative stays low: `consist of` 0.14,
`think so` 0.07, `jointed` 0.17, `different from` 0.74, `part of` 0.11, `in spite of` 0.19,
`free will` 0.19, `Chesapeake Bay` 0.59. That is the sharpest partner-contrast result of
this session.

The single failure is a long-context one: the ~570-token Northwest Passage passage with
`Hudson Bay` and `political will` buried deep scores 0.74, below threshold. The frozen
collocations do not reach the active line at long range, so the claim's context-invariance
is established for short contexts only — unlike the L0/N869+ ` code` pole, where a trigger
buried 400 words deep still fired at 3.47.

Held-out explanation scores are near floor (detection 0.53, TPR 0.07; fuzzing 0.50;
embedding AUROC 0.28), the usual pattern for pair-level token claims, so the probe evidence
carries this result. Self-reviewed (proposer and reviewer both
`external/deepseek-flash[1m]`); the proposer measured its own counterfactuals.

Next: a repeat with the same pairs at several context distances would pin down where the
pair effect decays, and the long-passage failure is the seed.
