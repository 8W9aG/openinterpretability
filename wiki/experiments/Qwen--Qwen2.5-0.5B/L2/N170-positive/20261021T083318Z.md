## Hypothesis

> N170+ is a personal-pronoun anaphora detector: it fires on a personal pronoun (I/my, you/your, he/him/his, she/her, we/our, they/them/their, or a reflexive like myself/himself/herself/themselves) when that pronoun agrees in person, number and gender with a person already mentioned in the preceding context, and it stays near zero on the very same pronoun token when the antecedent is absent (sentence-initial), only later (cataphoric), or gender/number-mismatched. The match is evaluated retrospectively against the recent context: repeated names and nouns never fire it, words after the pronoun do not change it, and a competing feminine mention nearby silences masculine pronouns while a salient feminine antecedent licenses she/her even alongside male mentions.
