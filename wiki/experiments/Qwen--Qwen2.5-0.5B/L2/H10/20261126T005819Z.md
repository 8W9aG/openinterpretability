## Hypothesis

> This head copies a scope operator forward: at the first word or two of a negated, restricted or quantified phrase it attends sharply back (weight 0.7-0.95) to the operator that opened the phrase - a negator ('not', "n't", 'never', 'no', 'nothing', 'nobody', 'hardly', 'rarely', 'without'), a restrictive/focus particle ('only', 'just', 'merely', 'even', 'also', 'still') or a frequency/quantifier adverb ('always', 'often', 'usually', 'all') - and writes that operator's direction into the current token, making its write far from its mean.
