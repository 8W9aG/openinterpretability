## Hypothesis

> The negative pole detects the letter L at a word-piece boundary: it peaks (1.2-1.7 of its 1.76 max) on the token that continues a word whose immediately preceding piece is a short fragment ending in 'l'/'L' - above all the bare L piece (' l', ' L', '-l', '-L', '/l'), as in ' l'+'entic' (lenticular), ' lo'+'quit' (loquitur), '/l'+'ua' (lua), ' l'+'us' (lus) - and it fires more weakly (0.2-0.6) on word-initial tokens that carry a capital L, especially L-abbreviations (LTD, LED, LR, LSM, LGA, LTP) and proper nouns. It stays at the floor on the bare L token itself, on ordinary words that merely contain an l (also, world, album, clinic), and it is much weaker after longer l-final fragments ('al', 'pal', 'loader'), so it is a piece-boundary feature, not a 'contains l' or 'these tokens' feature.
