## Hypothesis

> The pole fires retrospectively when the current token completes a conventionalized local collocation ('defendant pleaded', 'O(n', dosage '500 mg', 'Once upon', 'cat sat', 'market fell'), with peak strength graded by how tightly the preceding token or tokens predict it: swapping either half of the bigram for a non-formulaic alternative collapses the peak, while swapping only the upcoming token leaves it identical.
