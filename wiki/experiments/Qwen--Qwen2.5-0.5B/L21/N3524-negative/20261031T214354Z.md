The peak lands on the relator or verb that links the patient to its medium (' in', ' with', ' into', ' through', ' of') rather than on a fixed word class, and the pole stays at the floor when those same relators and verbs carry an abstract or informational argument ('the claim/company was dissolved', 'the report was filled with errors', 'the data was filtered', 'depends on the support of the board'), for mere placement of inert objects ('put on the shelf', 'stood against the wall'), for weather or metaphorical temperature ('the weather was warm', 'a heated debate') and for ordinary narrative prose; as an offshoot it also fires on the internal pieces of long technical or transliterated names ('Methylglyoxal', 'fluorouracil', 'Hiranyakashipu'). This is the mirror of the positive pole, whose rejected rule sat on the first token of the complement the clause was building toward: the negative pole sits on the relator inside such material clauses, and it is retrospective (its write is wasted by the time a temperature word would come next).
