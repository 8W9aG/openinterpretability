## Hypothesis

> Retrospective detector for nominal and numeral material: the pole activates on tokens inside entity-, measure- or time-denoting phrases (modifiers and determiners in the run-up such as 'days', 'heavy', 'with', the completing noun such as 'rain', 'level', 'year', 'Moon', 'sunrise', 'cities', 'guilty', and clause-final content words such as 'began', 'fast') and on numeral-onset and numeral-interior tokens (the space before a digit, '(' before digits, digits inside '3.9', '500', 'range(10)'), with the largest strengths at phrase completion while the clause's relational skeleton ('by', 'at', 'from', 'to', 'increased', 'prescribed') instead drives the positive pole. Content-word peaks arise from a g+u- SwiGLU regime and numeral peaks from a g-u+ regime, but both share one digit-promoting output direction (Jacobian kurtosis 12.1, 100% lens agreement); zero-ablating the pole moves almost no next-token mass (KL about 1e-4, unconcentrated) and changing what follows a peak leaves the peak unchanged, so the digit write is a weak retrospective bias rather than next-digit prediction.
