## Hypothesis

> The negative pole is the active regime of a bilingual 'appraisal' concept neuron: it fires when the running text names an evaluation of something — a rating, judgment, verdict, review/reviewer, evaluation, appraisal, award or acclaim (Chinese 评价/评分/评级) — peaking on that word or on the function word one or two tokens after it (e.g. 'The [[rating]] was', 'a rating [[of]] four and a half stars', 'Two [[reviewers]] independently'), and stays near zero for process/reporting vocabulary (audit, inspection, measurement, analysis, report) and for neutral description even in identical frames. It is retrospective, not lookahead: its output direction demotes exactly the vocabulary it detects (rating/evaluation/criteria rank 4/8/15 in w_out·W_U and are suppressed with z≈-12, while its 'promoted' list is the bottom of the same unembedding ranking), the classic Voita suppression signature, so it does not prepare an upcoming token.
