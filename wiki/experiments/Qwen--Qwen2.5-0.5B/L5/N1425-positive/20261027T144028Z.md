First audit of this pole (random draw, audit seed 3896367426; 200k fineweb-edu tokens as
512-token windows; max 1.78). The **asserted-clause-boundary** claim — the pole fires at
sentence-final `.` and elaborating `:` when the passage asserts in its own voice, and is
passage-gated (~7× more likely to fire after a firing boundary), but collapses for reported
speech, quotes and list items — came out **REJECTED at 0.53**: 10/13 hard negatives, 2/7
holdouts, 6/6 counterfactuals, never-active baseline 0.74. Token identity is high here
(R² 0.35), the second-highest in this session.

The failures contradict the mechanism in both directions. Single-sentence hard negatives
that the gate should keep quiet fire: `Why did the museum close the east gallery…` (0.53),
`At the press conference the mayor insisted: "The bridge…"` (0.62) and the two-sentence
`Ms Okafor said… Her lawyer said…` (0.50) — reported speech and a quote, both predicted
near-zero. Multi-sentence holdouts that should fire stay low: the mantle-convection passage
(0.33), the seedling passage (0.27), the lease clause (0.17), the first-person coffee
passage (0.26) and the field-kit passage (0.28), all under the 0.445 threshold. Only the
colon apposition `Field ecologists face a recurring problem: …` (0.69) and the groundwater
passage (0.52) pass.

So the boundary story is not doing the work. The scan's highest mean-activation tokens are
content words — ` dioxide`, ` amino`, ` nitrogen`, ` neurological`, ` confidence`, ` Tolkien`
— and several of the failing negatives are ordinary prose with punctuation at the same
positions. A better hypothesis would treat punctuation as a confound and test the lexical
set (science/chemistry vocabulary, perhaps a "technical noun" feature) against matched
period positions, or rescan with punctuation-stratified evidence. Held-out explanation
scores reflect the same problem: detection 0.62 (TPR 0.84, FPR 0.60 — the claim over-fires),
fuzzing 0.85 (TPR 0.69), embedding AUROC 0.53.

Self-reviewed (proposer and reviewer both `external/deepseek-flash[1m]`); the proposer
measured its own counterfactuals, which reproduce 6/6 while the claim fails.
