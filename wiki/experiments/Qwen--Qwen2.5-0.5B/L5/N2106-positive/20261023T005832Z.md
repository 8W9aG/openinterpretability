## Hypothesis

> Fires on words that denote the authorities taking a person into restraint, i.e. arrest, detention, custody or jail ('arrested', 'under arrest', 'into custody', 'detention'/'détention', 'sent to jail', 'caught'/'apprehended' by police, 'imprisoned'), on the illegality marker 'Unlawful/unlawful', and on the preposition right after such a verb ('arrested for', 'jailed for', 'imprisoned for'). It is weak or silent when the same word has a non-criminal sense ('joint custody', 'the ball was caught', 'in this case', 'a case of beer') and on conviction or sentencing words ('convicted', 'sentenced to', 'prison' all stay below 0.1 of max). It is retrospective: the Jacobian lens suppresses criminal/sentencing tokens rather than promoting the next token.
