## Hypothesis

> L5/N245+ is a retrospective lexical detector with a sparse, concrete lexicon: it fires on the current token when that token is a scene-setting physical word (landform/weather/terrain and outdoor-activity words such as 'reef', 'ledge', 'cliff', 'cavern', 'cave', 'glacier', 'avalanche', 'powder', 'surf', 'moss', 'beach', 'trail', 'quarry', 'dell') or a bodily/health-experience word ('appetite', 'pills', 'medicine', 'hygiene', 'conscious', 'fasting'), reaching 1-7 on such prompts, while function words, abstract/business/legal prose, everyday basic-level vocabulary ('cat', 'dog', 'house', 'cup', 'door', 'money') and the generic terms of its own domains ('mountain', 'hill', 'island', 'canyon', 'crag', 'water', 'rock', 'grass') stay at or below the activation line; on plain narrative prose the pole still crosses the line weakly (0.1-0.3) in about a third of sentences, and it has scattered single-word hits (e.g. ' directly' in 'follows directly from', ' year', ' while'). It does not look ahead: the determiner one token before a strong noun is flat (0.03) whichever noun follows, activation groups more by the current token than by the next one, and ablating the pole moves the next-token distribution by KL about 1.4e-4.
