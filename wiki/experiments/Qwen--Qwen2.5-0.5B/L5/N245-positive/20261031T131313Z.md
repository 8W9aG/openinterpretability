## Hypothesis

> L5/N245+ fires retrospectively on the current token when it realizes a vivid outdoor-adventure scene cue, rugged terrain, snow/surf conditions, or energetic motion phrasing (steep, rocky, crag, ridge, slopes, powder, avalanche, reef, waves, surf, climbed, along, directly), with peak strength 0.5-7+ scaling with cue specificity, spreading to neighboring function words in such passages (e.g. ' the' reaches ~3 before ' steep'), and firing lexically even when the sense is non-outdoor ('surf the web' still fires); outside such passages it shows only a weak <=0.4 background on common temporal/contrastive continuations (year, morning, while, in/of/the, far, three) and stays near-flat (<0.1) on short generic, office/abstract, or calm-domestic sentences.
