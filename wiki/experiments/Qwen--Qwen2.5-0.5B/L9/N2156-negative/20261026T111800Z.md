## Hypothesis

> The pole detects the stock 'more information / ask us / get in touch' formulae of written prose and peaks on their cue words: 'more'/'further' inside a purpose phrase ('For more information/details, ...' or '... for more details'), 'any' in 'if you have/need any ...', 'to' in 'do not hesitate to ...', and 'about'/'concerning'/'regarding'/'on' linking 'questions' to its topic. It does not fire on the bare directive itself ('Contact us', 'Visit our website', 'Click here'), on the same words in argument position ('she asked for more information', 'he did not hesitate to contact me'), or on ordinary conditionals ('if it rains'); because the peak is at the cue word, a fronted 'For more ...' is already active even when the continuation turns out not to be informational ('For more than twenty years').
