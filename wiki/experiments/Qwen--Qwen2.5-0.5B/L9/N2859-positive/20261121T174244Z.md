## Hypothesis

> The pole fires on grammatical rather than lexical tokens at word boundaries: in Greek on the last character-token of an inflected word - the case/number/person ending - with genitive, plural and feminine nominal suffixes (-εων/-άσεων, -ίας, -ικών/-ικές, -μάτων, -ής, -ης, -ας) and the genitive-plural article των firing hardest, while letters inside a word, word-initial letters and nominative-singular masculine/neuter forms (-ος, -ον, -οι) mostly stay below threshold. In English it is about ten times sparser and fires only on closed-class structural tokens - a determiner directly after 'of', a complementizer ('that') after a noun phrase, an auxiliary or an opening quotation mark right after a comma, and closing punctuation - not on open-class content words.
