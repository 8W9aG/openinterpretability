> The pole fires on a relation word - a preposition or subordinator (at, as, for, by, on, under, when, where, in, to) - when that word continues a nominal/prepositional chain rather than the clause's verb: the word immediately before it is a noun or name (never a finite verb, auxiliary or verb-adjunct), and strength grows with the amount of nominal/PP material accumulated before it - about 0.1-0.3 of scale at <=6 tokens with a bare or determiner-only noun ('the town at age 80' 0.17, 'the clinic at the edge of town' 0.10, 'the report of the committee' 0.11), 0.5-1.5 at 8-12 tokens or with a proper/technical name ('He died in Louisiana at age 80' 0.72, 'in primary myelofibrosis as an appropriate tool' 1.36/' as an' 1.79, 'establishment of a radioactive waste disposal facility in Western Australia for low level waste' 1.51), and up to the scan maximum for multi-word names or appositive/PP chains ('his home town of Epps, Louisiana at age 80' 2.35, 'alcohol, drug and mental health problems at 149 W. 132nd St.' 2.08-2.43). It stays near zero (<0.2) when the same word follows a finite verb or auxiliary ('gathered at' 0.03, 'was demonstrated as' 0.19, 'looked at' 0.13, 'collected in' 0.11, 'used as an appropriate tool' 0.09) or opens the first PP of a short clause-level subject or object ('the study at the clinic' 0.06, 'the facility in Western Australia for ...' 0.28 against 1.51 once a long nominal precedes the chain).
