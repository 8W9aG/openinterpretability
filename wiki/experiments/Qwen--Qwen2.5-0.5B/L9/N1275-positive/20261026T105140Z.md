## Discussion

<!-- Free-form notes. This page is never regenerated, so edits here are kept. -->

First experiment on this pole (random draw, audit seed 1932056900; 200k fineweb-edu
tokens read as 512-token windows; max activation 2.35). The claim is a graded
place/people detector: place names and nationality/ethnicity/language adjectives active,
other specific concrete referents about half strength, everything else at background. The
review rejects it at 0.57 — 11/13 hard negatives but only 2/7 holdouts — and the
token-identity baseline scores 0.82, well above the hypothesis.

The failures are consistent with a token-level, sense-blind response. The two failed
negatives are the same surface tokens in non-place senses (`India, the new neighbour…`,
0.705; `The Dutch oven…`, 1.208), so the pole fires on the token regardless of whether it
denotes a place. Meanwhile fresh place names in new contexts fall short of the 0.588
threshold (`Antarctica` 0.559, `Swahili` 0.523, `Uruguay` at the end of a long passage
0.387, `Porto` 0.210). The proposer's own scan replay had already shown large within-token
variance (Europe 0.09 over 27 occurrences vs Dutch 1.00 over 5); this experiment turns
that into the main finding.

So the honest summary is: the pole responds to a set of specific nationality/place tokens
with unstable, context-dependent strength, not to the semantic category. Causal
reproduction is 7/7 and the mechanism is retrospective; steering is a null. Proposer and
reviewer were the same model, so the record is self-reviewed.
