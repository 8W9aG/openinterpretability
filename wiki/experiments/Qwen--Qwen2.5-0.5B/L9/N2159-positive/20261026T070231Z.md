## Discussion

<!-- Free-form notes. This page is never regenerated, so edits here are kept. -->

First experiment on this pole (random draw, audit seed 1269044382; 200k fineweb-edu
tokens read as 512-token windows; max activation 2.40). The hypothesis is a
graded-comparison detector, sharpest on the second element of a reduplicated comparative
and on the junction closing a degree phrase; the review scores 0.81 and fails only the
specificity gate (0.77).

The three counterexamples are informative. A coordinated comparative fires
(`more engineers and more funding`, 1.016) even though a single one is flat, so the
construction that matters is not reduplication as such but a *coordinated sequence of
graded quantities*. `rather than` fires at 0.796, just over threshold, which sits oddly
with the claim that `rather` alone is a weak marker. The third, a flat factual sentence
about rattlesnakes (`eat`, 0.606), is a marginal threshold crossing. The one missed
holdout, `recovered only slightly` (0.419), is the mirror image: a closing junction that
stays under threshold.

Read together, the claim is close but drawn too narrowly — the pole responds to graded
comparison more broadly than reduplication plus a closing focus word, and the 0.60
threshold (0.25 × 2.40) is tight enough that marginal cases decide the gate. Causal
sufficiency and necessity are 0.91/0.91; steering is weak (a ×2 drift toward percentages
and discounts). Proposer and reviewer were the same model, so the record is self-reviewed.
