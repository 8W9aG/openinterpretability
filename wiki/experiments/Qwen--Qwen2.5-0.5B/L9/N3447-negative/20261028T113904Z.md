## Discussion

**Independent replication of [20261024T234440Z](20261024T234440Z.md), and it does not reproduce the VERIFIED verdict.** That record scored 0.90 (specificity 1.00, sensitivity 0.80, counterfactuals 1.00) when the proposer `external/glm-5.3-flash[1m]` also wrote the probes. With a fresh 20-probe set from `external/deepseek-flash[1m]` the same claim scores **0.81 — NEEDS_REVISION**, failing specificity at 10/13 (0.769) while sensitivity is 6/7 (0.857) and all ten of the record's own counterfactuals reproduce exactly (10/10), so the core is intact and the boundary is what moved. (`supersedes` is inherited from the original submission and points at the earlier NEEDS_REVISION record; this page is a new review of 20261024T234440Z, not a replacement of it.)

**The animacy exclusion is wrong.** The claim says inanimate co-hyponym pairs stay near zero (`sushi/ramen`, `apples/oranges`). Two of the three false positives are inanimate same-kind pairs that fire hard:

- `The sister ship docked two hours after the flagship…` **1.124** — a same-kind functional pair of ships (the `sister` token in its attributive sense, which the claim explicitly excludes);
- `The 44-year-old statute and the 39-year-old regulation…` **1.013** — repeated `-old` numerals attached to inanimate legal instruments.

The feature is a *same-kind pair*, not an *animate* pair; animacy happens to be how the scan's examples were phrased.

**The ordering condition is looser than claimed.** `Taller than his brother, Marcus still felt small beside him…` fires at 0.568, just over the 0.566 line, although the second member precedes the first — so "retrospective completion" is not strict about order.

**The place-pair-in-event-frame sub-clause fails for the second time.** `The delegation flew from Lisbon to Warsaw for the climate summit` reads 0.197.
