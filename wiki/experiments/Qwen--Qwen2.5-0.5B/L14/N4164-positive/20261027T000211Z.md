## Hypothesis

> The pole fires retrospectively on the short run of grammatical scaffold words that carries a clause's copula/auxiliary, typically a relativizer or light subject followed by is/was/will/'s/to ('...a LEGO Dimensions announcement which is supposed to...', '...; your first post wasn't...', '...a file was...', '...is expected to...', '...astronomers expect it to...'), and drops to ~0 one token later on the clause's lexical verb or on an adjacent rare content word ('happen', 'modified', 'peak', ' LEGO', ' Dimensions'). The run is only scored when 20-50 tokens of preceding text have already fixed the clause's content: the same words alone give 0.06, after an unrelated sentence 0.5-0.6, inside their own passage 2.6-2.9, and the same clause with 'that' instead of 'which', or with a coordinated instead of a relativized clause, drops to 1.3-1.7 -- so it tracks how tightly the passage constrains the current grammatical run, not the identity of the token; the opposite pole is the numeric counterpart, peaking on digit continuations of numbers ('1[6].00', '7[0] months', '5[0]0th').
