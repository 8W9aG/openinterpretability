## Hypothesis

> Fires on website cookie/tracking-consent notice boilerplate, i.e. a site telling its visitor that it uses cookies (or analytics/tracking) to give them the best experience and that continuing to browse/use the site means they agree (English, French and other languages). Activity builds across the notice clause and peaks on the sentence-ending punctuation (or the following 'By'/'This') right after a 'we use cookies to ... experience/website' or 'this site uses cookies' clause; it is near zero when cookies are a topic rather than a notice (browser cookie how-tos, studies about cookie banners) or food (bake/eat cookies, jar of cookies), and weak (~0.15-0.45 of max) on adjacent legal boilerplate like reCAPTCHA notices, newsletter sign-up privacy lines and GPL licence headers.
