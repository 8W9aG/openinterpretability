## Hypothesis

> The negative pole is a retrospective detector for comparison against a second referent: it is driven by constructions that set the current entity against another named entity or set, and it peaks on the token that introduces that second referent - the comparative word itself ('than', 'relative to', 'compared with', 'similar to', 'same as', 'unlike') or the determiner/preposition opening the standard's noun phrase - and by the alternative determiner 'other' ('the other proposals'). It stays near zero for plain assertions about a single definite referent, for comparatives whose standard is never stated ('colder during the winter'), and for degree adverbs whatever their identity (swapping quite/very or almost/nearly changes nothing), so it is not a function-word, degree-adverb or token-frequency detector; the punctuation, numeral and code-fragment peaks in the scan are low-strength leftovers this claim does not cover.

- **Activating tokens:** `than`, ` other`, ` relative`, ` compared`, ` to`, ` the`
- **Mechanism claimed:** retrospective
