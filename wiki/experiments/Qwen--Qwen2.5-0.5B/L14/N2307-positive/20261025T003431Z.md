License', the AS-IS disclaimer), court-caption formulas ('PER CURIAM' under 'UNITED STATES COURT OF APPEALS', '... CIRCUIT'), tool-output and template spans (netstat rows, docker info lines, '(ABSTRACT TRUNCATED AT 250 WORDS)', docfx/javadoc markup like '<!--[if IE]><![endif]-->'), box-drawn comment headers, Windows GAC strong-name paths (v4.0_4.0.0.0__b03f5f7f11d50a3a), and other fixed runs such as hex digits, aligned whitespace columns and rare word fragments (' abs|ou|lt|ely'); activation rises along such a span and peaks where the upcoming token is most certain (measured Spearman +0.63 between pole strength and next-token certainty, -0.63 with next-token surprise), which is why the very same fragments (' subject', 'f', ' C', 'RED') are inert in ordinary prose.
