## Hypothesis

> The negative pole is a weak, graded dial for concrete physical content: it is most active on content words naming physical materials, substances, parts, body parts and small countable things (particles, cells, tissues, rocks, grains, blades, gears, valves, 'texture'; plural people and animals are intermediate) and much less active on large whole artefacts, abstract or social-institutional vocabulary (rules, costs, topics, claims, importance), proper names and function words.
