## Discussion

<!-- Free-form notes. This page is never regenerated, so edits here are kept. -->

First experiment on this pole (random draw, audit seed 1946718591; 200k fineweb-edu
tokens read as 512-token windows; max activation 2.48). The proposer deliberately claimed
a *graded dial* for concrete physical content rather than a detector, and the review bears
that out at 0.82: 12/13 hard negatives and 5/7 holdouts pass, and the hypothesis beats the
best trivial baseline (token identity, 0.67).

The misses are informative about the dial reading. The failed hard negative is a business
frame where "lines" is figurative ("lines of credit", "product lines", "lines of
reporting", 0.658, just over the 0.62 threshold). The two failed holdouts are genuinely
physical but sit at the bottom of the predicted 0.4–1.9 physical band: tiny watch parts
(0.458) and a masonry sentence (0.527). The ordering the hypothesis predicts still holds;
the binary active/inactive line at 0.62 cuts through the middle of a graded claim, so the
gate is blunt for a dial.

Causal metrics are correspondingly weak (sufficiency 0.56, necessity 0.63), and steering
is a null: ablation reproduces baseline on all five neutral prompts and the ×1–×2 clamps
show no concrete-content drift. That fits a weak, low-effect dial. Proposer and reviewer
were the same model, so the record is self-reviewed.
