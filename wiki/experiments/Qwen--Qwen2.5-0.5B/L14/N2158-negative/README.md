**Latest status:** VERIFIED (2026-10-19)

> Fires at the onset of a numeral that states a cardinal quantity (a count or amount of things: subjects, people, hours, fans, donations, dollars, points, votes), peaking on the space token, '$', 'n =' or first digit just before the digits, and strongest when the frame sets up a sizable quantity ('a combined total of', 'over', 'about', 'more than $', '(n =', 'needs', 'population of'). It stays near zero when the same digits are an identifier or date (years, 'in 1960', page/room/ward/form numbers, 'Fig.', postal codes, coordinates) and when no number follows, so it encodes 'a quantity count is starting here', not digit tokens per se.
