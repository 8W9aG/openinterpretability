## Hypothesis

> L14/N4392+ is a statement-closure unit for expository prose: it fires, retrospectively, on the single token that completes a long (roughly 15+ word) formal, technical or legal declarative - a sentence-final '.', '.\n' or '.\n\n' (or '?'), a paragraph/section break right after a completed sentence ('<p>', a heading underline), or the last token of a fixed formula such as 'incorporated herein by reference' - reaching about 0.4-0.9 of its 2.74 maximum on patent and academic clauses but only about 0.1-0.2 on short, terse or colloquial sentences. It stays near zero on clause-interior function words, on headings, list fragments and ad copy, and on the branch vocabulary that its output direction suppresses ('other', 'another', 'else', 'whereas', 'same', 'both', 'instead', and list-separating ';'), so it marks 'this statement is finished and the text is not branching to an alternative'.
