## Discussion

<!-- Free-form notes. This page is never regenerated, so edits here are kept. -->

First experiment on this pole (random draw, audit seed 2833295248; 200k fineweb-edu
tokens read as 512-token windows). At max activation 0.85 this is another floor-level
pole — the 8th percentile of layer-14 negative-pole maxima. The proposer measured real
relative contrasts with controlled batteries: punctuation that opens or separates a
further nominal designation scores above the same punctuation in non-nominal frames. But
the binary form of the claim fails the review: 7/7 holdouts fire while only 6/13 hard
negatives stay below threshold, so balanced accuracy is 0.73 and the specificity gate
fails.

The failure mode is the same one recorded on L13/N2147. With a max of 0.85 the threshold
is 0.212, *below* the pole's ordinary level: 2207 of 4000 held-out windows are active. So
parenthetical adverbs (0.426), relative-clause parentheses (0.221), quotes after verbs of
saying (0.268) and playing (0.347), scare quotes (0.307), clause-joining `and` (0.213) and
interrogative `or` (0.263) all "fire" even though the proposer's controlled comparisons put
them below the nominal frames. The relative claim survives; the absolute one does not.

For floor-level poles the protocol's relative threshold turns ordinary punctuation-rich
text active, so a graded relative claim is the right shape and a binary content detector is
not. Counterfactuals reproduce 9/9, the mechanism is retrospective, and steering is a null.
Proposer and reviewer were the same model, so the record is self-reviewed.
