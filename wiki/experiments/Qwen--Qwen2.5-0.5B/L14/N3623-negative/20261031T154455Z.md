## Hypothesis

> The negative pole fires on the token that supplies a time or quantity value in running text: the digits of a written year or date (1815, 1987, 1969, 1600), a calendar or clock expression that fixes when something happened (last night, next year, last October, this quarter, each month, in late August, 5 p.m.), and scale/unit words (degrees, Celsius, billion).
