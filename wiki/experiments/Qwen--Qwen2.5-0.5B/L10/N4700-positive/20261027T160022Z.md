## Hypothesis

> This pole fires retrospectively on the current token when it is a highly predictable local continuation of the preceding one or two tokens: it completes a common bigram or collocation (fry until, heavy rain, to all, are all), a fixed phrase (Once upon a time), a subword (pro+tons), or a high-frequency function-word transition (I can, The patient, Celsius at). Replacing the continuation with an unpredictable word, or changing the preceding context so the same token is no longer predictable, collapses the peak, while an equally predictable substitute leaves it unchanged.
