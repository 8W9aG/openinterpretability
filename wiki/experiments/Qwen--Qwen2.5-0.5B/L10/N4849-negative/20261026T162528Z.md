First experiment on this pole (random draw, audit seed 420182422; 200k fineweb-edu
tokens read as 512-token windows). At max activation 0.98 it is near the layer floor (12th
percentile of layer-10 negative-pole maxima). The proposed reading is a weak counter-pole
to a strong magnitude neuron: the negative side peaks only on a qualitative appraisal word
inside a comparison or improvement claim (`safer and better`, `cleaner and better`,
`more efficient than`), and stays flat when the same slot holds a measurable property
(`larger`, `longer`, `faster`, `cheaper`).

The review is close at 0.75 — 12/13 hard negatives, 4/7 holdouts — but fails sensitivity,
and the misses are informative: natural comparative sentences land far below the 0.244
threshold (`sturdier and more dependable` 0.124, `better and cheaper than the previous
design` 0.233, `clearer and more generous` 0.042), while the proposer's constructed
frames reached 0.43–0.98. So the claim describes the frames it was built on but does not
transfer to ordinary comparative prose. The one failed negative is the admitted leftover:
an archive header with a timestamp `17:20:40 EDT` (0.744).

Counterfactuals reproduce 7/7 and sufficiency/necessity are 0.94; the never-active
baseline is 0.68 against the hypothesis's 0.75, so the margin is thin. Steering is a null.
Proposer and reviewer were the same model, so the record is self-reviewed.
