## Hypothesis

> The positive pole is a retrospective detector for everyday interpersonal exchange: it fires on the ordinary verbs of people dealing with one another - talk, chat, help, share, thank, agree, offer, advise - and on the speaker or addressee words that frame them (I, me, my, us, your; the peak sits on ' your' in 'thank you for your help' and on ' my' in 'I helped my sister'), landing on whichever of those words closes the social phrase, and most strongly when the speaker or addressee is a participant. It stays at the floor for the same events rendered as a solitary action on an object (read, work, watch, keep, eat), in a formal or institutional register (assisted, greeted, 'the committee agreed to the plan'), and for formulaic arithmetic and code; the negative pole is the impersonal end of that axis, most suppressed on formulaic math such as 'greatest common divisor'.
