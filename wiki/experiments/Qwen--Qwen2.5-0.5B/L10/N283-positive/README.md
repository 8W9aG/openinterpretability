**Latest status:** VERIFIED (2026-10-19)

> Fires on the preposition or determiner that opens the interlocutor slot right after a word naming an act of interviewing, telling or reporting, questioning, surveying or recording: 'interview with', 'confirmed to', 'statement to', 'speaking to', 'told [the]', 'interviewed/questioned/photographed/surveyed by', 'phone call with', '(return) phone calls'. The peak is usually on 'with/to/by' (or 'the' / the outlet name just after it), whoever the interlocutor turns out to be, and activity is weak (<0.2 of max) when the same preposition follows a non-communicative head ('partnership with', 'returned to', 'funded by', 'walking to'). A weaker secondary activity (about 0.3-0.6 of max) appears on the digits of four-digit years that date a reported or studied event ('in 2004', 'June 2018').
