> This pole fires when a singular individual is picked out of a plural or group background: activation peaks on the finite third-person-singular predicate that follows the singular subject noun phrase ('the transfer arm is designed', 'Spider-Man has grossed', 'the song has sold') or on an appositive/comma attached to that NP, and it is strongest when a plural mention of the same kind sits in the preceding sentence ('several films ... The film has', 'two engines ... The engine was'). It is near zero for plural subjects with plural predicates ('the films have', 'the transfer arms are'), for bare non-referential pronouns ('it is', 'they are'), and for a lone singular clause with no plural or group context (single sentences such as 'The film has grossed $864 million' peak at 0.03-0.28, well below the 0.25 x max threshold); a 3sg form under a plural subject ('the films has') is intermediate, and the pole's output suppresses plural third-person pronouns ('they/their', 他们/她们/它们, 各自) while promoting singular possessives ('his', 'himself').
