## Hypothesis

> This negative pole is a retrospective detector of a word-internal token piece: it fires when the current token carries no leading space, i.e. a fragment the tokenizer glued inside a word that is being spelled out piece by piece (md in Filmdrama, ap in mahaparinirvana, rom in retromastoid, os in chemosignals, op in immunopositive, ':' in Category:X, the digits inside 10.1126/science.1227037, single-character Greek/Arabic and byte-fallback pieces), plus the rarer case of a glued punctuation or whitespace token; it never fires on a space-prefixed alphabetic word standing alone (0 of 90,498 such positions in the layer's scan reached a quarter of max, while 255 of 40,610 no-leading-space fragments did). Strength is largest for a short, common piece that cannot end its word inside a long, rare or technical string (chemical, biological and botanical terms, identifiers, URLs, DOIs, wikitext templates), and drops to baseline when the same letters are written as separate words ('retro mastoid', 'chemo signals') or when the rare word happens to be a single token ('quokka', 'syzygy', 'zugzwang'), so it is a spelling-out/fragment detector rather than a rarity or surprisal detector.
