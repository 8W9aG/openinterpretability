## Hypothesis

> The pole fires retrospectively on the token that completes a familiar 2-4-token cue inside a well-formed declarative sentence: an adjective/noun/verb plus noun collocation ('light energy', 'on the Moon', 'of the cell'), a quantity ('100', 'two cups', '3.9 percent'), or a fixed function frame ('at least', 'FROM users WHERE', 'x =', 'in range'). Scrambling the cue word order, swapping in an anomalous or nonce completion, or removing the sentence context collapses the peak, while paraphrases that preserve the frame or edits after the peak leave it unchanged. This beats the token-identity confound (72% variance): the same token fires after its familiar cue and goes quiet elsewhere (e.g. 'energy' peaks after 'light' but not after 'chemical' or 'banana').
