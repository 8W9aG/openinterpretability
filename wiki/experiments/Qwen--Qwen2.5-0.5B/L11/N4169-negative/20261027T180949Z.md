First audit of this pole (random draw, audit seed 1111528753; 200k fineweb-edu tokens as
512-token windows; max 4.81). The **ending** claim — fires on words marking something as
final, departing or taken away (`last`, `leave/left/leaving`, `goodbye`, `farewell`, `died`,
`d.`, `without` completing a removal, `until`/`long enough` ending a scale) and not on the
mirror-image start/arrival/presence words — came out **NEEDS_REVISION at 0.68**: 12/13 hard
negatives (specificity 0.92) but only 3/7 holdouts, 9/9 counterfactuals, never-active
baseline 0.75 ahead of the hypothesis. The pole is very sparse on fineweb — one active
window in 4000 — so held-out scoring (detection 0.50, AUROC 1.00 on that single window) is
uninformative and the probes decide.

Only part of the claimed inventory works. Firing: `That was the last of the firewood`
(2.22), `He waited long enough to hear the clock strike twelve` (1.89) and a long
harbour-register passage (5.16). Failing: `until the lights of the village came into view`
(0.15), the death marker `Thomas Aitken (d. 1819)` (0.68), `The movers emptied the flat
without a word` (0.76) and the departure paraphrase `She waved from the platform as the
train pulled away` (0.68) — all against a 1.20 threshold. In the other direction,
`The board concluded its review in June and ended the fiscal year` fires at 1.29 although
`ended` is not in the claimed set.

So the "ending" direction is partly right — `last` and `long enough` fire, and every
same-token other-sense negative stays low (`last week's`, `turned left`, `two left`,
`leave of absence`, `boots should last`, `without sugar`, `died laughing`, `enough flour`) —
but the trigger inventory is wrong: `until`, `d.` and `without` do not fire, while `ended`
does. The honest next hypothesis is a narrower temporal-finality set (`last`, `ended`,
`long enough`) tested against `until`/`d.`/`without` in matched frames, or a rescan with
more `last`-type text. Self-reviewed (proposer and reviewer both
`external/deepseek-flash[1m]`).
