## Hypothesis

> N546+ is a long-range discourse-topic detector: it fires on a content word (noun or verb) whose meaning fits the topic established by the preceding passage, and its strength grows with how much topical context has accumulated (the word 'debt' in a finance passage reads 0.01 with no context, then 0.15 / 0.20 / 0.30 after 1 / 2 / 3 finance sentences).
