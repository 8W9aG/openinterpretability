## Hypothesis

Membership is lexical, not semantic or frequency-driven: substituting a merely similar domain noun keeps only the weak ' of' response (quality of education 1.6-3.8 vs 10.4), and compositional of-phrases of other shapes stay at the floor (place of birth 0.1-0.9, state of the art 0.2-0.7, speed of light 0.3-0.6, cause of death 1.3-2.4); reordering ('life quality', 'service terms') kills the peak, a determiner after 'of' roughly halves it (quality of the life 4.0 vs 10.4), the hyphenated/glued spellings are the same feature and fire as strongly or stronger (quality-of-life 13.5, ease-of-development 10.0, time-of-flight 7.6, out-of-the-box 6.4, internetofthings 3.5), and arbitrary initialisms mostly stay at the floor (only the scanned stored strings fire: SIL OFL, R.O.C., BOD, BioFOG, nOTScale, POV).
