## Hypothesis

> The pole fires on the indefinite determiner 'some' (and much more weakly on 'any') where it is used as a hedge that leaves the amount, degree or kind of something unspecified - the reading of 'need some help', 'for some time', 'some form of debt', 'some kind of', 'is there some way', 'showed some interest' - and its write at that position is the matching vague-classifier set ('kind', 'sort', 'type', 'form', 'way', 'time') instead of the specific noun the model would otherwise name. It stays at floor on the very same token where 'some' merely picks out particular countable individuals that are met, bought, seen or counted ('I met some friends', 'I bought some books', 'in some cases', 'for some reason'), on a sentence-initial 'Some' with nothing before it, on every other quantifier (several, many, few, a few, certain), and on compounds (someone, something, somewhat, somehow); and because the peak is on the determiner itself only the text before it can matter, so changing the word after 'some' never changes the value.
