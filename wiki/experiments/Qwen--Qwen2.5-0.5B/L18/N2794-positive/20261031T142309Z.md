## Hypothesis

> L18/N2794+ fires retrospectively on the current token when it continues an already-open word or fixed phrase: mid-word fragments (hab, oxic, ochond, ebra, re, h) and the predictable second half of common collocations (but after asked-comma, at after contain, the after on/to, pleaded after defendant, fraud after because-of, commas in lists, the first digit of a round number after at), with strength scaling from ~0.25 for ordinary continuations to 1.35 for highly fused subwords; the same word outside a continuation slot fires far less (the: 0.25 after on vs 0.03 after red; but: 0.50 mid-sentence vs 0.14 sentence-initial).
