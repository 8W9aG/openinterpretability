## Hypothesis

> The negative pole fires on the token that completes the stem of a Latin/Romance word the text is still writing, i.e. the piece on which the word in progress stops being stem material and only an ending can follow ('iter-' in iteración, ' administr-' in administrating, 'eler-' in decelerations, 'ug-' in centrifugation, ' demand-' in French demandent, 'cancel-' in Portuguese cancelou, 'ibr-' in calibrating); it is silent on earlier pieces of the same word while the stem is still incomplete (' centrif' before '-ug-', 'dec' before '-eler-', ' contr' before '-ari-'), on the same strings when they end a complete word ('demand' in 'The union will demand safer conditions', 'inform' in 'Please inform the committee', 'reconsider' in 'We should reconsider the plan'), on whole words that arrive in one token ('iteration', 'administration', 'centrifuge') and on non-Latinate fragments ('fenugreek'). It is retrospective, not lookahead: the peak is fixed by the prefix alone (the same ' demand' piece reads 10.79 after a French prefix and 0.03 after an English one, and deleting the ending after 'reconsider' leaves its 6.33 unchanged), and zero-ablating the pole spreads a small loss over the whole ending family (aggregate KL 2.1e-03, shifted tokens 'at', 'ation', 'ating', 'ar', 'ados', 'atively', concentration below the 0.5 gate) instead of concentrating it on one upcoming token.
