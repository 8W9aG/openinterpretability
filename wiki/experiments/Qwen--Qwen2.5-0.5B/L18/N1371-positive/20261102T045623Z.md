## Hypothesis

> This pole fires at discourse-deictic anchor points where the text refers back to earlier content: the speaker's own prior communications ('as I have mentioned/said in...', 'recall from my previous...'), citations of where something was stated or documented ('mentioned in your edit', 'read in the link', 'as reported by', 'In a related article'), and back-references to prior events ('twenty years previously', 'return to in a minute').
