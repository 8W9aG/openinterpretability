## Hypothesis

> Fires on the first sub-word piece of a multi-token surname or place name: a short capitalised fragment, mostly W-initial (' We', ' Wo', ' Wa', ' W') and also ' Ra', ' Ke', ' Ka', ' Kl', 'ke', ' dela', that sits in a name slot (after a first name, a title such as Mr./Dr., or 'at'/'in' before an institution or place), so the word is still unfinished (Weisberg, Weill, Inverkeithing, Raanan). It stays silent on the same letters as a complete word (the pronoun 'We' at a clause start, single-token names such as Weber, Goldberg, Johnson, Inverness), on later pieces of the name ('ill', 'isberg'), and on other first pieces such as ' Gins', ' Br' or ' St'. A weaker offshoot fires on a pivot token inside memorised boilerplate, e.g. ' via' in a 'Sun-Sentinel via AP' photo credit and '=' in 'initial-scale=1'.
