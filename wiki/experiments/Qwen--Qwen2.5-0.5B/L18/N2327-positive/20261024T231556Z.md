## Hypothesis

It stays silent on the same letters as a complete word (the pronoun 'We' at a clause start, single-token names such as Weber, Goldberg, Johnson, Inverness), on later pieces of the name ('ill', 'isberg'), and on other first pieces such as ' Gins', ' Br' or ' St'. A weaker offshoot fires on a pivot token inside memorised boilerplate, e.g. ' via' in a 'Sun-Sentinel via AP' photo credit and '=' in 'initial-scale=1'.
