## Hypothesis

> The pole detects a locative/state predication headed by a preposition or copula: it fires on ' in'/' at'/' on'/' to be' when that word heads a small-clause complement whose subject is the preceding reflexive pronoun ('find/consider/catch/imagine/place/put oneself in X', 'consider oneself to be X'), on that reflexive pronoun itself, and more weakly (1-3.5) on the preposition of the reduced passive 'X is located/situated/positioned in/at Y'. It is a construction detector, not a token detector: ' herself' fires after 'found' (5.5) but not after 'hurt' (0.0), and ' in' fires after 'found herself' (6.4) but not after 'found the dog' (0.4) or after an event-locative adjunct ('hurt herself in the kitchen', 0.1); a weak separate tail fires on digit continuations of alphanumeric tokens ('P4'+'5', 'sha3'+'8', ~2.0).
