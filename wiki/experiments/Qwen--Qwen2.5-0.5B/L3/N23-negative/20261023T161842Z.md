## Hypothesis

> This pole fires (weakly, max ~0.9, dial-like smooth histogram) when the current token is the locally predictable completion of the preceding one or two tokens: the second half of a frequent bigram or collocation (far->away, greater->than, ruled->that), a list/code continuation (Tokyo,->Berlin; (3->,), or a subword finishing a common word (h->iked, am->oxic, Se->ine, j'->hab).
