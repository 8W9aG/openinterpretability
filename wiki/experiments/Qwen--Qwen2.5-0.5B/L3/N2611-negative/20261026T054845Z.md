## Discussion

<!-- Free-form notes. This page is never regenerated, so edits here are kept. -->

First experiment on this pole (random draw, audit seed 167742255; 200k fineweb-edu tokens
read as 512-token windows; max activation 2.15). Nearly every top peak is the single token
` with`, and the claimed rule is context-gated: active when ` with` is preceded by a comma
or by an indefinite thing NP (`a loan with…`, `an old bridge with…`), baseline when it is a
complement or fixed preposition, follows a definite/relational NP, or opens a clause. The
review supports it at 0.96, well above the token-identity baseline (0.46): 12/13 hard
negatives and 7/7 holdouts pass.

The one failure marks the soft edge of the rule: `The Olympus engine with the cracked
turbine blade` (0.721) is a definite object NP without a comma, which the hypothesis puts
at baseline but which fires. So the "definite NP" exclusion is not reliable; the comma and
indefinite-NP inclusions are.

Two findings from the proposer worth following up: this neuron's positive pole is a broad
` into` detector (mean 1.40 at ` into`), so the pair contrasts `into` against attributive
` with`; and the SwiGLU regimes split cleanly (active cases `g+u−`, inactive `g−u−`). The
opposite pole is therefore a natural next audit.

Steering is weak and ambiguous: ablation reproduces baseline on all five prompts, but the
×1–×2 clamps drift into fill-in-the-blank quiz formats (`It is ________. A. rainy…`) that
the claim does not predict, plus some ` with` continuations. Proposer and reviewer were the
same model, so the record is self-reviewed.
