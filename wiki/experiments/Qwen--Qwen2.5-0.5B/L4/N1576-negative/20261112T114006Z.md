## Hypothesis

> The pole fires on the joint token of a locally-fixed construction: the token at which a familiar template hands over to its fillable part. It peaks on the ' in'/' of' keyword after a 'for (var/let x' header (and on the 'in' membership operator, 'if (x in y)'), on the connector right after a quoted string argument of a DSL call ('describe "..." do'), on the first token of a quoted attribute value just after '=\"', on an attribute name that follows a closing quote inside a tag ('\" xmlns', '\" id', '\" width'), and on ' in' after a defect noun (hole, gap, mistake, error); it stays near zero on the fixed prefix before that joint, on the filler tokens that follow it, and throughout ordinary prose, where the same words (' in' after cat, book, wall, code) do not fire.
