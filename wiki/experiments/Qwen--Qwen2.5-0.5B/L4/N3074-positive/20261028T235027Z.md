## Hypothesis

> Fires, retrospectively, on the token that conventionally completes the phrase underway: the expected collocation, named entity, scheduling or legal-boilerplate term, code or math syntax, or subword completion. Amplitude is graded by constraint: content keywords in matching formal/news gist respond most strongly (fraud, stock, year, via the g+u+ regime), function-word and subword completions more weakly (at, in, ine, world, via the g-u- double-negative regime). The same token outside its conventional context, or an incongruent substitute in the same slot, drives it to baseline or below, which a bare trigger-token list cannot predict.
