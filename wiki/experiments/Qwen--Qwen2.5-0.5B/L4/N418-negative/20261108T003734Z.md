## Hypothesis

> Retrospective detector for an approximate (vague, small) amount phrase: the pole peaks on the vague-quantity head of "a little X", "a few X", "a bit of X" or "a couple of X" -- most strongly on ' little' and on the ' of' that follows ' bit' or ' couple' -- and it tracks the construction rather than the word, since ' little' stays quiet as a size adjective ("the little girl", 0.37), in the negative-polarity idiom ("Little did he know", 0.00) and as a bare quantifier ("little hope", 0.12; "Few people", 0.09). Exact and large quantity words do not fire it ("a lot of" 0.14-0.49, "a piece of" 0.48, "a number of" 0.04, "a dozen" 0.23, "several" 0.00, "many" 0.13, "plenty of" 0.10) and neither do exact numerals ("45 minutes" 0.12, "in 2017" 0.17); every strong (>0.5) activation is in the SwiGLU g+u- regime (the diffuse <=0.5 g-u+ response to ordinary content words is a separate broadcast regime), and these leftovers are outside the claim: determiners and quantifiers right after light verbs ("could use a" 2.85, "learns a" 2.71, "take some time" 1.99, "snap a few" 1.83), the word-piece 'm' in identifiers ("dbmigrate", 4.35), measure nouns such as ' seconds' ("a few seconds" 3.10, which fire even after 'several' and are themselves top token-identity drivers), and numerals in approximative frames ("an hour or two" 2.34, "or three" 2.25).
