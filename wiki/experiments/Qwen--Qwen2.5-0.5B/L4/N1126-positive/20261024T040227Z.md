## Hypothesis

> This pole is a retrospective phrase-medial extension signal: it fires on tokens that continue an open constituent (clause verbs, VP-extending prepositions, modals, clause-linking medial pronouns, medial determiners, descriptive adjectives/adverbs), with strength building over the first few tokens of a clause; it stays near zero on constituent onsets (sentence-initial tokens, e.g. 'The' is silent across 16 occurrences) and on tokens that close a predictable unit (complement nouns like 'school/cake/mat', complement verbs after modals or 'to'). The Jacobian lens shows no coherent upcoming-token set (kurtosis ~3.5, zero lens overlap), so it reacts to read context rather than preparing specific next tokens.
