## Hypothesis

> N2435- is a retrospective span-end detector: it fires on the token that closes a marked span opened earlier in the same line or block - the '>' of a '<...>' type-argument/generic list ('List<Package>', 'std::vector<std::string>'), the closing '*' or '_' of markdown emphasis, the '"]' that ends a quoted JSON/PHP array, the closing '\Vert'/'$' of LaTeX math, a closing tag or ')' in code, and the field terminators '####' at a heading line start, a run of spaces after '#if', and the '=' after a multi-item assignment target. It tracks span closure, not the characters themselves: the same '>' used as a spaced comparison operator ('a > b', '5 > 3'), commas in simple prose lists, and ordinary content words stay near zero, while prose appositive commas ('include, for example', 'The report, which was long') and the idiom 'much of a' are acknowledged residuals that also fire.
