## Hypothesis

> Retrospective: the pole fires on the token that continues or completes a multi-token licensing frame set up by earlier tokens in the same sentence -- e.g. the complement verb after 'ruled that the <noun>', the 'to' closing a 'from'-range, the second verb after '<action> and', a pronoun continuing a greeting opener, or the consequent noun after 'then the' -- with activation driven by those earlier frame tokens (mean attribution distance ~1.6; single-token swaps of the frame opener collapse it).
