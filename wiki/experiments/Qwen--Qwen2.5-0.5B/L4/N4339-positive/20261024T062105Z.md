## Hypothesis

> The positive pole fires on a token that completes a multi-token surface unit instead of starting a fresh word in running prose: (i) a newline that breaks the line inside a clause in hard-wrapped text (e.g. 'reviewing the\nproposal', but not a newline after sentence-final punctuation and not a line break in otherwise unwrapped prose), and (ii) the non-initial element of an uncommon multi-token word, name or label (e.g. 'sville' in Brownsville, 'stones' in brownstones, ' Mile' in Four/Seven Mile, ' Street' in Charles Street, 'content' in 'Article content'). It is near zero on ordinary word-initial content words in flowing prose, on sentence- and paragraph-final newlines, on familiar multi-token names such as New York, Main Street and Wall Street, and on the negative pole's hostile-reception frames ('come under fire'), where the same neuron is instead strongly negative.
