## Hypothesis

> Qwen2.5-0.5B L4/N52-negative is a retrospective detector for a comma that continues an already-started, clause-level comma run: it peaks on the second or later comma of a sentence when that comma sits at a clause boundary or inside the predicate (after a clause, a verb phrase, an adverbial/appositive interpolant, or a fronted phrase), and it stays near baseline on a sentence's first comma - even a fronted clause or fronted adjunct comma such as 'After the thunderstorm, ...' or 'In an evil hour, ...' - and on commas that separate bare single-word list items ('apples, pears, and oranges') or sit inside a subject phrase. Inserting one comma ahead of an otherwise identical comma moves the peak from ~0.1 to ~1.9-2.1 (English) and from ~0.2 to ~1.7-3.5 (Chinese), while a semicolon or period in that slot reaches only ~0.3-0.8 and same-frame word substitutions inside the interpolant leave it unchanged; the Jacobian lens is flat (kurtosis 3.7, 0% overlap with the logit lens) and ablating the pole at its peak barely moves next-token probabilities, so it is a detector rather than a next-token predictor.
