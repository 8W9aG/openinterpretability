## Hypothesis

> This pole fires retrospectively when the current token completes a conventionalized local frame opened by preceding material: a comma closing a fronted adjunct ('far away, there lived') or a list-final item before its conjunction ('one egg, and'), or a complement selected by its head ('Once'+'upon', 'sat'+'on', 'import numpy'+'as', 'through'+weekday, 'ORDER BY'+column). Peak strength is set jointly by the head-plus-peak combination rather than either token alone, so swapping the head ('sat'->'waited') or the peak (','->'and'/'plus', 'upon'->'atop', weekday->'xyz') collapses firing while same-frame substitutions (Sunday->Monday) leave it unchanged.
