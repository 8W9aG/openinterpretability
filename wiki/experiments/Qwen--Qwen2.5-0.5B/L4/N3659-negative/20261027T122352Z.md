First audit of this pole (random draw, audit seed 1741412752; 200k fineweb-edu tokens as
512-token windows; max 2.90). The **stative/generic/passive predicate over an abstract
subject** claim came out **VERIFIED 0.89** — the strongest probe result in this session:
12/13 hard negatives, 6/7 holdouts, 9/9 counterfactuals, every gate passed, sufficiency
0.97 and necessity 0.94 with median next-token KL 3.6e-04. The counterfactuals track almost
exactly (`The same rules apply → govern` predicted 0.94, observed 0.94; `question answered →
phone answered` predicted 0.75, observed 0.75; two controls at 0.02 and 0.00).

The holdouts that carry it are the abstract-subject passives and generic statives the claim
names: `Once the inquiry closed, no further steps were taken` (2.40), `A formal response was
not forthcoming` (1.88), `critically true of the later edition` (1.51), `No objections were
raised` (0.94), `The restriction applies to any vehicle` (1.16), and a long water-authority
passage (1.23). The physical-object and agentive negatives mostly stay low: `the dishes were
done` (0.22), `they took no action` (0.50), `the decision was announced` (0.24),
`the work was sold` (0.17), the `apply the ointment` and code-comment controls.

The two failures are worth keeping in view. `The phone was answered on the third ring` fires
at 1.00 even though the minimal pair `The question was answered → The phone was answered`
reproduces its 0.75 drop — so the natural sentence's continuation (`on the third ring`)
contributes something the isolated pair does not. And `The payment was made two days before
the deadline` (0.27) fails although it is an abstract subject with a general-purpose verb, so
the verb inventory (`make`) is doing work the abstractness story does not capture.

The main caveat is the gap between the probe verdict and the held-out explanation scores:
detection 0.56 (TPR 0.24, FPR 0.12), fuzzing 0.54 (TPR 0.08), embedding AUROC 0.58, with
erratic recall by quantile (0.60 at the weakest). A judge reading only the written claim
cannot find the active windows, even though probes written from the claim pass at 0.89. The
claim is therefore probably narrower than the pole: the reviewer's probes were natural
sentences built to the claim's own inventory, and both failures point at context and verb
selection beyond it. Still self-reviewed (proposer and reviewer both
`external/deepseek-flash[1m]`), so an independent review should be the first follow-up — the
recruitment should use the two failures as probe seeds.
