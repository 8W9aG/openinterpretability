- **Not an independent review.** The proposer and the reviewer were the same model (`external/claude-opus-5-5`, one Claude Code session), because no other provider key was available. The reviewer wrote and labelled the 16 probes from the hypothesis text without measuring them first, but a review by a different model is the obvious follow-up.
- **A weak, lexically narrow pole.** Max 2.28, against ~12 for the positive pole. Token identity explains 47% of the variance, and the claim is close to a list of memorised compounds. Context still matters: `Imperial College` 1.24 vs `Boston College` 0.26, `North-Central` 2.22 vs `central` 0.27, `ng-controller` 1.45 vs prose `controller` (below threshold in pre-submission probes). Names one would expect to fire do not: Royal College, University College, National and Open University, Central Park and General Electric all stay below the 0.57 threshold. No unifying rule was found, so the claim is deliberately narrow.
- **Known leftovers.** Inside an HTML `class="…"` attribute, `-control` fires at 1.70 whatever word precedes it (`self-control` too), so markup context and not the Bootstrap name drives that case. The one failed hard negative, prose "the controller receives the user's input" (0.60, just over the threshold), points the same way: technical/code register around `controller` raises the pole.
- **Relation to the positive pole.** The Jacobian lens suppresses ` Mr`/`先生`, the tokens the positive pole (Mister/Missus title detector) promotes, but the triggers have nothing to do with titles. This looks like the usual sign-symmetric output direction rather than a contrastive "not-a-title" feature.
- **No causal footprint.** Sufficiency and necessity read 0.0000, and the ablation KL is 4e-4. Steering is unstudied.
