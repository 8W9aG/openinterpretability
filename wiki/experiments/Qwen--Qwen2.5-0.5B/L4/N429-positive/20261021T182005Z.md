## Hypothesis

> The positive pole fires on the token that completes a gendered civilian title of address (Mr, Ms, Mrs, Miss, Mister, Madam, Mme, Monsieur; more weakly Madame, Herr, Señor), peaking on the abbreviation period of 'Mr.'/'Ms.'/'Mrs.' (~9-12) or on the title token itself when no period follows (~7-9), whether or not a personal name follows ('Mr. Coffee', 'Mr. Universe', 'Thank you, Mr. President'). Professional or noble titles (Dr ~2.5, Prof ~1, Sir ~1), other abbreviation periods ('p.m.', 'U.S.', 'St.') and bare names stay near zero; its Jacobian-lens output promotes the same title tokens (' Mr', '先生'), so it is a retrospective 'Mister/Missus title just read' detector rather than a predictor of the upcoming surname.
