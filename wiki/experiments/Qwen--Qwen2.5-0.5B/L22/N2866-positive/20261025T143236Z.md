## Hypothesis

> N2866+ is a retrospective 'word-under-assembly' detector: it fires on a token that is only a fragment of a longer word (92% of its activations at strength >= 3 sit one token before the piece that completes the same word, against a 22% base rate over the scan), and its strength mostly tracks how far the preceding, largely long-range context has committed the text to that particular word -- the identical slot in 'the court affirms in part, vacates in part, and ___' peaks at 11.3 for 'remands' but only 1.9 for 'reverses', the same ' rem' token scores 5.9 for 'remanded' after 'the appellate court reversed the judgment' but 1.4 after 'the chef tasted the sauce', and a legal prefix raises a bare ' rem' from 6.8 to 11.5 while a mismatched biomedical prefix cuts it to 3.5.
