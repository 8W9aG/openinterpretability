## Hypothesis

> N2866+ is a retrospective detector that fires strongly on the first piece of a multi-token word (rem|ands, N|MDA, am|oxicillin, h|iked) only when the immediately preceding 2-6 token collocation frame licenses that specific word (affirms/vacates/and frames remands, influx/via frames NMDA, mg/of frames amoxicillin); the same slot completed by another word (reverses, remarks), the same frame with a different onset (biked), the fragment used as a unit, acronym, or code identifier (3 m, REM, mInput), and single-token words with no pending completion (humming as one token, dissolved ion, red ant) all stay weak, as does sub-threshold activity on frequent function words. Distant topic words beyond the local frame do not gate it: swapping far-away content (in-the-slices/after-the-trial, day/week, a biomedical vs legal preamble) leaves firing unchanged, so it reads a locally licensed word-onset state, not long-range commitment and not a specific upcoming token (Jacobian kurtosis 3.3, no coherent promoted set).
