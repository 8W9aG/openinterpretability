## Hypothesis

> Fires on the subject of a clause when that subject is third-person singular and its finite verb comes next: strongest on a pronoun ' He'/' She'/' It'/' he' (or the last piece of a person's name, or a French singular subject like 'sa décision' + ' n'), opening a clause that refers back to a person or thing already under discussion. It prepares a singular verb (is/was/has, French n'est/n'avait) and suppresses plural ' are'/' were'. It stays silent on plural subjects (they, the senators), first/second-person subjects (I, we, you), object pronouns (him, her) and singular noun phrases that are not right before their verb. The token at the start of the text also gets a moderate floor (about 2 to 5).
