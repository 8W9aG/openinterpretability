## Hypothesis

> Fires on a token that the left context makes the end of a third-person SINGULAR clause subject whose finite verb has not come yet. It is strongest (7-13) on a subject pronoun He/She/It/he/she (French Il/qui) that opens a clause and refers back to one person or thing already introduced. It is moderate (4-9) on the last token of a person's name or singular subject noun in subject slot (Gillum, 'sa décision' -> ' n'), and on the comma before a delayed verb ('the old mayor of the town, after years of service,'). It stays below about 3 on plural subjects (they, the senators, laptops), first/second person (I, we, you), object or possessive forms (him, her, his) and nouns that are not subjects. Separately, a text's FIRST token is active by itself: about 3.5-5 for ordinary openers such as The/A/In/My/the, 6-11 for It/He/She/Everyone, but below the line for They/We/You/I or a symbol such as '#'.
