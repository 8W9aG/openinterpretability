> The negative pole is a retrospective detector of unfamiliar, non-whole-word material: it fires on the token where the surrounding text has pulled the model out of ordinary English - a sub-word fragment or short word-initial fragment of a long, rare, technical, transliterated or foreign-language word (Dutch, Turkish, French, Japanese, Latin/Greek genus names, chemical names) that has to be assembled from pieces instead of retrieved as one familiar token. It is near zero on ordinary English words in ordinary prose, and the same token fires or not depending on the surrounding language (' transparent' = 4.3 after 'Het bestuur moet more' but 0.0 after 'The board must become more'), with a secondary peak on the first token of a document (1.5-5.3) and on a few English words that head proper-noun phrases (' Foreign', ' Public').
