> Litigation-procedure neuron: once a court-case context is set up (case caption, opinion or filing naming parties such as the State, the defendant or an appellant), it fires on the token that opens or sets up a party-role or filing term. The same tokens without legal context, and ordinary legal prose away from such slots, stay weak (<~2). Leftovers it does not explain: German articles and negation after a verb (keine/eine, ~7) and Android XML 'android:layout' (~4-7).
