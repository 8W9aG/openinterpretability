## Hypothesis

> This pole marks the cultural sector: it fires on words naming the institutions that produce and transmit culture - schools, colleges and universities, teachers; newspapers, magazines, journals and broadcasters; books and publishers; film, cinema, theatre and museums - while the same-sized, same-frequency institutions of other sectors (bank, hospital, army, factory, ministry, court) and concrete objects (building, road, window) leave it at zero in the identical frame. The drive is passage-level as well as lexical: the same word fires several times harder inside an arts/media/education passage than inside a finance or manufacturing one (identical sentence, 3.84 vs 0.95), and the neuron writes the culture concept forward (zero-ablating it removes 1-2.7 nats from 文化/'cultural'/'art' at every peak and the tokens it loses most are library, film, newspapers, Book), which is what the Jacobian lens is reading.
