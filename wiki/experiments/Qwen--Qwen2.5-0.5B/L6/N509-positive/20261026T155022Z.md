Two boundary problems are worth recording for the next revision. First, the "pay is flat"
carve-out fails inside an offer-letter frame that lists salary, overtime and a company car

`subsidised canteen / childcare vouchers / gym membership` package (1.233) all miss,
against calibration examples at 3–4.6. The concept is right; its generalization to new
wording is still too weak.
