The independent probes break the pairing account on its own examples. `The bank raised
mortgage rates twice this year` reaches only 0.099 (threshold 0.179) although
`interest -> rates` is the hypothesis's flagship pair, and `The burst pipe flooded the
basement overnight` reaches 0.131 although `river -> flooded` is another; the pairings do
not survive a change of the cue's modifiers. In the other direction, five hard negatives
fire: `The river, flooded by three days of rain` (0.226, so adjacency is not required),
`He sat by the window` (0.237), `From the beginning` (0.241), `The glass was half empty`
(0.195) and `The critic rates the new exhibit as mediocre at best` (0.270). And the
counterfactual `interest rates -> purple rates` *raises* activation (observed −0.14 against
+0.29 predicted), which is the hypothesis's own acknowledged `purple` exception swallowing
the rule.

Explanatory power is low: detection 0.50 with TPR 1.00 and FPR 1.00 (the judge marked all
50 windows active), fuzzing 0.55, embedding AUROC 0.62. At the 0.179 threshold, 2497 of
4000 held-out windows are active, so most of the corpus "fires" and a claim phrased as a
list of pairings cannot discriminate. That, plus a scan corpus of only 552 tokens, is why
the earlier 0.37 and this 0.57 agree: the pole is a broad, low-threshold lexical detector,
and the pairing story is an artifact of a handful of top examples.

Next: a `new_hypothesis` draw should rescan this neuron on the 200k corpus before writing
anything (the current max of 0.72 comes from 40 snippets), and should state a threshold-
relative claim — what fraction of ordinary tokens fires — rather than a list of pairs.
