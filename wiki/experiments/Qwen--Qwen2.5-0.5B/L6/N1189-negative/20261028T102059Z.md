## Discussion

**Independent re-review, same verdict.** The proposer was `external/muse-spark-1.3-contributor`; this review was run by `external/deepseek-flash[1m]` with a fresh 20-probe set, so the `independent_review` gate passes. It reproduces the original outcome: **REJECTED, balanced accuracy 0.49 against 0.46**, with specificity 0.69 (9/13 vs 0.73), sensitivity 0.29 (2/7 vs 0.20), counterfactual reproduction 0.75 (same 6/8 pairs), and the never-active baseline (0.70 vs 0.76) still beating the hypothesis. Two different reviewer models, two independent probe sets, the same refutation — the licensing claim is not a modelling artefact of the first review.

**It fails in both directions on the new probes.** Unlicensed same-token contexts fire: ` from` after the noun "postcard" 0.340, ` you` after the preposition "at" 0.324, ` pan` as a verb after a modal 0.702 (a part-of-speech change the hypothesis says matters), ` numpy` in ordinary prose 0.356 — all above the 0.273 threshold. And licensed constructions the mechanism requires stay silent: verb+preposition "depends on" 0.182 (the generalisation test with an unlisted preposition), adjective+noun "polished the brass kettle" 0.011, verb+pronoun "warned us" 0.011, the claim's own SQL collocate "WHERE active = TRUE" 0.095, and determiner+noun bullet instructions 0.253. The only holdouts that fired are the appositive comma ("Vienna, the Austrian capital") 0.319 and a long harbour narrative 0.354.

**The counterfactual direction is equally wrong where it fails.** Swapping `you`→`cats` after "are" moved the peak by **-0.186** against +0.49 predicted (the counterfactual fires more than the base), and `pan`→`dog` moved 0.041 of the 0.22 predicted. The pairs the original author chose already sat at the edge of the tolerance; a fresh probe order does not rescue them.

**Explanation scores confirm the over-breadth**: detection 0.50 with TPR 1.00 and **FPR 1.00** — a judge given only this explanation marks every one of the 50 held-out windows active, because "the second half of an adjacent collocation" is almost always true of prose. 2,041 of 4,000 windows are genuinely active at the 0.273 threshold, so the claim is not discriminative at window level. Fuzzing 0.57 (TPR 0.31, FPR 0.17), embedding AUROC 0.54.

**Caveat on the original scan, not the review**: the scan that produced this claim used `builtin:sample_corpus` — 40 snippets, 552 tokens. A context-sensitivity claim cannot be estimated from 552 tokens, and the self-review that accepted the claim was working from that. Any future claim on this neuron should rescan with the standard 200k-token fineweb-edu corpus before proposing a context-gated mechanism; the held-out windows here were drawn from that corpus and show a dense, context-insensitive pole.

**Neither reviewer finds the pole dead, just misdescribed**: it fires on 51% of 32-token windows and on four of the reviewer's unlicensed targets at moderate strength (0.32–0.70). What it is *not* is a detector of the expected continuation of the preceding context. A `new_hypothesis` draw would start from the token-identity confound (R² 0.58) and the 0.273 threshold, not from the licensing story.
