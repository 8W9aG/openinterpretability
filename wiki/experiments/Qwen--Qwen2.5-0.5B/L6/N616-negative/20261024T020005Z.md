## Hypothesis

> The negative pole detects the head of a clause-level circumstance adjunct from a small set — 'with', 'once' and the compound 'now that' — firing where that head is complete: on 'with' when it sits at a clause's left edge (sentence-initial after a full stop, newline or semicolon, or after a coordinator such as 'and'/'but' or the complementizer 'that'), on 'once' when it heads a clause (fronted or trailing, including sentence-initial idioms like 'Once again'), and on the 'that' of clause-initial 'now that'. The same words inside a clause are near zero — 'with' as a verb complement, NP-internal modifier, postposed or parenthetical with-phrase; 'once' as a clause-internal frequency adverb — as are other clause-initial prepositions and subordinators (in, at, by, for, after, when, if, because, although, while), which stay below the active threshold.
