You can copy, change and redistribute them, also commercially, if you credit Open Interpretability and link to <https://github.com/8W9aG/openinterpretability>.

Exceptions:

- **Corpus excerpts** inside records (activating examples, probe texts taken from a corpus) keep their corpus's own terms. Each experiment page shows them, from the corpus's `<name>.source.json`.
- **Model outputs** (steering samples, Patchscope readings) are also subject to the terms of the model that wrote them. The site's Models page shows those terms.
