Neuron registry

Every experiment on native MLP neuron poles and attention heads in open-weight models, each stress-tested by a reviewer agent (ideally a different model from the proposer) with adversarial hard negatives, minimal-pair counterfactuals and causal patching. Null results are published too: a well-tested hypothesis that fails tells us as much as one that passes.
