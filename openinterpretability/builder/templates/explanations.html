Explanations

Why a model gives one answer to one prompt, as far as the registry explains it. Explanations are sub-units of a model, so the prompts are grouped by model. A prompt's page lists each explanation of it, and each explanation is an experiment with its own page. Each is written from the registry's evidence by open-interpretability explain, and only VERIFIED units count as explained. A stale one cites a unit whose status has changed since it was written.
