- **Sanity checks on the metrics of `openinterp score`** (`scripts/score_sanity.py`, `docs/analyses/score-sanity.md`, #3237). The paper's missing-labels and extra-labels tests run on all 2,301 stored scores, with the judge's answers as the explanation's labels and no new judge calls. Balanced accuracy falls under both tests wherever the judge beats chance. Recall alone, precision alone and F1 each fail one test. A quarter of the stored scores are at or below chance, so they can't show a wrong explanation.
