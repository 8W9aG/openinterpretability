- `scripts/experiment-loop.sh --provider NAME` drives each session with another model provider: DeepSeek, Z.ai GLM, Muse Spark, Gemini or Grok (#3245). Profiles are in `openinterp/data/providers.toml`. They hold the URL, the models and the environment, and say where the key lives, never the key itself. `open-interpretability provider` lists them, and its `exec` runs a command with a profile. `open-interpretability loop --provider` takes the proposer and reviewer from the profile. Each session records the model that really drove it. `docs/providers.md` is the guide.
