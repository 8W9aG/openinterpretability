- Every line that a pull request changes must now be covered by a test, or marked `# pragma: no cover` with its reason (#5855). The threshold is set once, as `[tool.diff_cover] fail_under = 100` in `pyproject.toml`. `make coverage` and CI's diff-cover step both read it. The `test-new-paths` skill says when the pragma is allowed. New tests close the gaps in 178 modules. Of these, 46 missed one or two lines or branches, 89 missed three to ten, and 43 missed eleven to twenty. Package coverage rises from 90.92% to 95.60%, and the floor from 90 to 95.
- The secret scan no longer takes the validity date of a test ssh signer line, such as `valid-before="20200101Z"`, for a key (#5855). `.gitleaks.toml` allows that pattern by line, and its old `[allowlist]` table moves to the `[[allowlists]]` form, which gitleaks needs for two lists.
