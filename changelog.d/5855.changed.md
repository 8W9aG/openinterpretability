- Every line that a pull request changes must now be covered by a test, or marked `# pragma: no cover` with its reason (#5855). The threshold is set once, as `[tool.diff_cover] fail_under = 100` in `pyproject.toml`. `make coverage` and CI's diff-cover step both read it. The `test-new-paths` skill says when the pragma is allowed. This is step 1 of raising the package's coverage to 100%, from 90.92% today.
