The threshold is set once, as `[tool.diff_cover] fail_under = 100` in `pyproject.toml`. `make coverage` and CI's diff-cover step both read it. The `test-new-paths` skill says when the pragma is allowed. New tests close the gaps in 46 modules that missed one or two lines or branches. Package coverage rises from 90.92% to 91.17%, and the floor from 90 to 91.
