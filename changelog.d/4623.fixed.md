- **`explain --record` no longer runs twice on a GPU** (#4623). The figure data subtracted each head's mean, kept on the CPU, from the head's output on the GPU. On MPS that failed after the narration, and the GPU fallback then ran the whole command again on the CPU. So the narrator was asked twice, and the second answer was recorded. The head's mean now moves to the output's device first, as it already did elsewhere.
