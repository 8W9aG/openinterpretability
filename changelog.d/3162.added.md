- **A type check in `make lint` and CI** (#3162). `make typecheck` runs mypy in strict mode, with the pydantic plugin, on `openinterp/core/`. That is the package of schemas that every record depends on. CI runs it as its own step. Its 49 strict errors are fixed with annotations alone, and with no `type: ignore`. `docs/development.md` says how to add the next package.
