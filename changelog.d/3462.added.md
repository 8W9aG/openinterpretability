- **Literature page: "Evaluating Neuron Explanations"** (`docs/literature/evaluating-neuron-explanations.md`, #3462). The paper's missing-labels and extra-labels tests pass correlation, cosine, AUPRC, F1 and IoU. Balanced accuracy fails the extra-labels test on rare concepts. Our scores use balanced accuracy on samples that are half active by design, where it passes. `docs/analyses/score-sanity.md` now says that a score on ordinary text should use another metric.
