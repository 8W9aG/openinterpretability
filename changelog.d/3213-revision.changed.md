- `docs/circuits-and-hubs.md`: a circuit is a cluster of the wiring graph, found by community detection, not by a task (#3213). Its id survives from build to build by member overlap, with a lineage of growth, splits and merges, and merged circuits become sub-circuits. A circuit can hold several task hypotheses. An experiment records the members it tested and goes stale when they change, and the planner then draws a new one. A hub is an entity with no experiments of its own: its standing is its center's.
