- `explain` plans its experiments greedily by joint coverage (#4558). Coverage doesn't add up, because units push the answer both ways. So at each step the plan adds the experiment that raises coverage most, together with the ones already picked. The picks come first in the plan and in `explain --run`, and the plan line names them. On Pythia-70m "The sun rises in the" → " sky", greedy picks reached 50% in two experiments. The old plan, by single gains in attribution order, estimated 203.
