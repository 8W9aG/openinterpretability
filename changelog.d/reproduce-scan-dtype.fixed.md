- **`reproduce` measures the scan evidence in the dtype that the scan pins.** A record can pin a bfloat16 scan and a float32 review, as #3801 does. Before, the review's float32 pin was used for the whole record. So bfloat16 top examples were compared within the float32 tolerance, and drift of up to 1.4% of the scale failed every example. Now the top examples and the corpus sample are measured in the scan's dtype (`provenance.dtype`). The first-token floor reads the whole vocabulary, so the review's runner still checks it. They are compared within the 16-bit tolerance when that dtype is 16-bit. The review keeps its own pin and tolerance. `docs/analyses/half-precision-reproduce.md` gives the rules (#3801).
