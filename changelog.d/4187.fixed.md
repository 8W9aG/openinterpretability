- **`reproduce` adds an absolute floor to the tolerance of a Pythia-70m neuron pole.** Pythia-70m's float32 activations move by up to 0.005 between machines and thread counts, whatever the pole's scale. A negative pole's scale is at most 0.17, so that move failed the tolerance, as on PR #4187. `[tool.openinterpretability.reproduce_floor]` in `pyproject.toml` sets the floor for each model. Pythia-70m's is 0.01, and a 10% edit of a peak still fails. `docs/analyses/neuron-float32-floor.md` has the measurements of 283 records (#4187).
