- **`open-interpretability next` draws circuits** (#4286). A circuit in `data/circuits/` with no experiment gets a `circuit` draw. So does a stale one, whose members changed since its newest experiment, weighted by the share that changed. The draw's steps write the task, propose the hypothesis and review it on another model's probes. `open-interpretability loop` draws again over a circuit draw, since its API agents can't write the task. Hubs get no draw. `choose-next-experiment` lists the `circuit` and `explanation` draws.
