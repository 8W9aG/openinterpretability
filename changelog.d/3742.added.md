- **`open-interpretability explain` measures prompt coverage, and plans the next experiments by what they would add** (#3742). Prompt coverage applies model coverage to one answer. It is the share of the answer's log-probability that comes back when only the explained units keep their values, against random units. The plan gives every pole, head, superposition and circuit that isn't VERIFIED its experiment. An unstudied unit gets an audit with `--selection attribution`, and a studied one gets the planner's heaviest follow-up. The top experiments are ranked by the coverage they would add. The output lists the next `--experiments` (default 10), and says how many experiments reach 50%, 80%, 90% and 99% of the attribution.
