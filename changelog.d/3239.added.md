- **Does VERIFIED predict held-out detection?** (`scripts/verified_vs_detection.py`, `docs/analyses/verified-vs-detection.md`, #3239). Over 2,288 scored neuron-pole records, VERIFIED explanations score 0.040 higher on detection within each judge (95% CI 0.024 to 0.057), and 0.030 higher on fuzzing. The scores fall in the verdict's order: VERIFIED 0.647, NEEDS_REVISION 0.612, REJECTED 0.580. But a score separates VERIFIED records poorly (AUROC 0.59). So the write-up recommends no detection or fuzzing gate yet.
