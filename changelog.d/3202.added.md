- **The untrained-model null** (`scripts/untrained_null.py`, `scripts/measure_unit.py`, `docs/analyses/untrained-null.md`, #3202). The whole pipeline ran, blinded, on 20 GPT-2 small positive poles and their twins in a randomly initialised copy. Trained units verified 9 of 20 times and random ones 2 of 20 (one-sided Fisher p = 0.015). Both random passes were context-length claims, so a follow-up issue adds probe length to the confound baseline.
