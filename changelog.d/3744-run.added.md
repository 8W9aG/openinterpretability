- **`open-interpretability explain --run N` runs the plan's top N experiments until each unit is VERIFIED** (#3744, part A). They go through the experiment loop as normal experiments: claimed, audited, reviewed, opened as PRs and landed. A unit that ends NEEDS_REVISION or REJECTED gets the planner's next follow-up on a later draw, until it is VERIFIED, stopped, or out of `--max-attempts` runs. `loop` and `explain --run` now share the loop's setup (`cli_loop.run_loop`).
