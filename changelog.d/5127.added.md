- `scripts/base_instruct_cka.py` gives each layer's linear CKA between a base model and its instruct model, raw and standardised, against a random-weights null. It also swaps each third of the blocks back to the base model and correlates the poles again. On Qwen2.5-0.5B, the whole layer changes a little, most in the middle third. That change comes partly through the earlier blocks (`docs/analyses/base-instruct.md`, #5127).
