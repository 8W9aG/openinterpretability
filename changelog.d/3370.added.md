- **How to add a model, and a check for it** (#3370). `docs/adding-a-model.md` gives the process: the gate, the choice, the check, the config, the reproduction floor and the release refresh. It also says why a model needs no learned dictionary and no SPD model, with the numbers. The new `open-interpretability model-check <model>` loads the model and checks that the hooks find its layers, MLP, attention and norms. It names the MLP's family and the block's shape, runs one ablation and checks the config. Each configured model passes it.
