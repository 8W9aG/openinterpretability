- **The interference report, part 1** (#5230). `open-interpretability interference --model BASE` takes `--changed MODEL` or `--patch REF`. It compares a changed model with its base:
  - It re-tests every VERIFIED pole's probes, the keep test of `docs/analyses/base-instruct.md`, which now lives in `openinterpretability/interference/keep.py`.
  - It reads every neuron's activation correlation and output column, and the token-level KL and the answers of explained prompts.
  - Each measure also runs against two noise floors, the base against itself and a matched random change, and a change counts only above both.
  - It writes JSONL rows, the footprint of moved neurons (studied or not), and a verdict: *no known interference*, *interference* or *footprint not yet studied*.
