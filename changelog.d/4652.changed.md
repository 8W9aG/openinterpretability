- **The site build uses less memory** (part of #4652). The Parquet export used to hold every record's JSON at once, so it builds its table in batches now. The statistics' private export leaves that column out, and each claim is a hash, not the submission's JSON. On one frozen `wiki/`, the one-job peak fell from 9.2 to 7.4 GiB, and the four-job total from 18.7 to 11.3 GiB. The output is byte-for-byte the same.
