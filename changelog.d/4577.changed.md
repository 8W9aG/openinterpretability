- **The literature map and the models' concept maps have a new look** (#4577). It follows the cancer-research map that the issue cites. Each paper or pole is a dot in its cluster's colour on a soft glow. Each cluster has a numbered badge and a numbered legend, and status stays a shape. A card shows on hover or focus.
  - The literature map embeds the queued papers with the reviews: 156 open literature issues, drawn as small dots.
  - Its papers are laid out with UMAP and grouped by Leiden on a k-nearest-neighbour graph. Each region is named by the index section that most of its reviews are under.
  - Callout cards in the margins name the papers that the repo uses. Hovering over a paper links its three nearest papers.
  - The colours are the dataviz palette's, checked for colour-blind readers in both modes. `cluster_map.py` and `_map_theme.html` hold what the two maps share.
