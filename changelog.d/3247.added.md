- **One heavy job at a time on a machine, when you ask for it** (`openinterp/core/heavy_lock.py`, #3247). With `OPENINTERP_HEAVY_LOCK=1`, every model load and `make test` takes an exclusive `fcntl.flock` on `~/.cache/openinterp/heavy.lock` first, so jobs from different checkouts queue. A waiting job names the process it waits for. Children of the holder don't wait. `scripts/experiment-loop.sh --one-heavy-job` and `openinterp loop --one-heavy-job` turn it on for a loop. It is off by default.
