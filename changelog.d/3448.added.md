- **Literature page: "Beyond Top Activations"** (`docs/literature/beyond-top-activations.md`, #3448). An evaluation of top activations alone measures only recall. On vision neurons, the paper's two methods cut a full evaluation's cost by about 40 times. They are importance sampling guided by a concept model, and a Bayesian model of rater noise. `openinterp score` already stratifies its active windows, but it draws inactive ones uniformly. Issue #3595 tries the paper's sampling there.
