- A new paper, *How do neuron explanations fail as ROME edits pile up at one layer?*. It makes 100 ROME edits in turn at layer 2 of pythia-70m and tests 180 VERIFIED explanations again at 19 checkpoints. The 88 explanations above the layer fade steadily with the weight distance, with no threshold. Random edits of the same norms erase them faster. `scripts/rome_sequence.py` runs the edits, and `scripts/rome_sequence_stats.py` gives the tables (#5362).
