- **Each paper and post links to its X post** (#5840). The deploy posts a paper or a post to X. Then a new job writes the post's URL as `x_url` in the folder's `media/social.json`, and opens a PR. The PR merges by itself once CI passes. The page then shows "Thread on X", as a plain link with no script from X. `social post --record` and `social link` do the work. The deploy job keeps its read-only token, and only the new job writes.
