- **Commands for fix patches** (#5229, part 2). The new `open-interpretability patch` group has `seal`, `show`, `log`, `diff`, `branches`, `branch`, `delete-branch`, `merge`, `revert`, `rebase` and `check` (the zero-start check). `answers review` and `explain` take `--patch` with `--evidence-out`. They run on the patched model and write their result as the fix's evidence, never to the registry. `docs/patches.md` gives the workflow.
