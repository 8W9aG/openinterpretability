- **Breaking.** `explain --target` is now causal coverage: the share of the answer's log-probability that the explained units bring back, with every other unit mean-ablated. It is no longer a share of |attribution|, which counted pushes that cancel. The plan estimates how many experiments reach the target by a binary search of forward passes. On "The quick brown fox jumped over", Qwen2.5-0.5B needs about 418 for 25%, where the |attribution| plan said 55,238 for 80%. `explain --run` measures the coverage after each landed experiment. It counts the coverage only when it beats every random set of the same count. The output and the record lead with coverage, and the record stores its target (#4476).
