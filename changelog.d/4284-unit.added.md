- Circuits are a unit with experiments (#4284). `open-interpretability circuits propose` grows a circuit's wiring cluster by attribution on a task. `circuits review` measures the task hypothesis on the reviewer's holdouts and other tasks, against random member sets, and archives the record in `wiki/circuits/`. The gates are in `docs/PROTOCOL.md` §4, under their own rule version (`CIRCUIT_PROTOCOL_VERSION` 1.0). `PROTOCOL_VERSION` stays 0.9, so no other record needs re-evaluating. `open-interpretability check` recomputes each circuit verdict.
