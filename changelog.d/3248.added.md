- **`openinterp next` explores attention heads** (`openinterp/planner_heads.py`, #3248). After the model draw, an explore draw audits a random head with probability `[tool.openinterp] head_share` (default 0.1). It is a fixed share, not by unit count, because Qwen2.5-0.5B has 336 heads against about 233,000 neuron poles. The kind is drawn from the audit seed, so the planner's other draws don't shift. The claim names the head before the scan. Head draws skip the effect-weighted draw, which is for poles.
