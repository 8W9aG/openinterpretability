- A new paper, *Which neuron explanations survive a ROME edit?*. It edits 10 unrelated facts into pythia-70m at layer 2, one at a time, and tests each of 176 VERIFIED explanations again. No pole at or below the edit layer changes. Above it, ROME flips 25 of 870 readings, against 40 for random rank-one edits of the same norm. `scripts/rome_keep.py` runs the edits, and `scripts/rome_keep_stats.py` gives the tables (#5167).
