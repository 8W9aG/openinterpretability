- **Paper: do neuron explanations survive a sequence of narrow fine-tunes?** `papers/sequential-tuning/` fine-tunes Qwen2.5-0.5B and its instruct model on SST-2, then XSum, then GSM8K. After each stage it re-tests the VERIFIED explanations and scores ARC-Easy (#5125). About three quarters keep after three stages. The first update costs the most, and the benchmark does not track the losses. `scripts/sequential_tuning.py` runs it, and `openinterpretability/interference/tuning.py` holds the fine-tuning and the measures.
