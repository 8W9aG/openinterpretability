- `explain --run` explains the answer again after the run, from the registry as the run left it. The record, the narration, `--json` and the printed output all describe that final state. The record's new `run` field gives the explained share before and after, the experiments that landed, the PRs left open and why the run stopped. A run that stops early, on failures or a rate limit, still writes its record before the command exits with the loop's code. The record goes in the checkout that the command ran from, not the loop's worktree (#4474).
