- **The literature map's cards, regions and colours** (#5798).
  - The margin cards no longer run off the map, and their leader lines no longer cross. There are at most 8 a side.
  - The cards name the reviews with status used that the most pages in `docs/` and `papers/` link to. Reviews in the index's model-architecture and data sections are left out.
  - The "Queued, not reviewed yet" region is gone. Each queued paper sits in its topic's region, because the issue template's stock words are no longer embedded.
  - There are now 8 regions, one per palette colour. Regions that touch never share a hue family. The colouring keeps the validator's colour-blind clashes off the borders with the most links.
  - Regions are more compact: 90% of the dots are nearest their own region's centre, against 75% before.
  - The called-out dots are smaller, with an outline.
