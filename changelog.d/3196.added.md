- **Verdict stability** (`scripts/verdict_stability.py`, `docs/analyses/verdict-stability.md`, #3196). Two more Sonnet reviews of each of the reviewer crossover's 30 submissions, on the identical prompt, give three reviews by one reviewer. Two of them agree on status 67% of the time, and on VERIFIED-or-not 82% (κ 0.61). Same-reviewer noise is 21% of balanced accuracy's variance, twice the reviewer model's 11%, and about 70% of the crossover's unexplained residual. The `specificity` and `beats_confound_baseline` gates cause most of the flips. A follow-up issue holds the protocol choice.
