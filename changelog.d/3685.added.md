- The site shows **superpositions** (#3685). Each one has a page at `models/<model>/L<layer>/S<id>.html`, with its members sorted by weight, its method and its source. A member neuron's page lists the superpositions it is a member of. A member with no experiment of its own gets a short page that lists them. The home, model and layer pages count superpositions on their own line, apart from the verified units and the false-discovery estimate. The count of neurons studied includes the members, and gives the number that only a superposition covers. The coverage percentages do not change. The registry Parquet has two new columns: `superposition_id` and `superposition_members`.
