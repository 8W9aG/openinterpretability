- **A draw ledger, written before each run** (`openinterp/core/ledger.py`, `openinterp ledger`, #3205). `openinterp next --steps` writes each draw to `wiki/ledger/<day>/<timestamp>-<seed>.json`, and the claim step commits it instead of an empty commit. So a run that is dropped or crashes still leaves its draw. `openinterp ledger` reports each draw's outcome (record, stopped or none), and the VERIFIED share of explore draws against all draws and against finished ones. A stopped run now lands its PR with only its ledger entry instead of closing it. `openinterp check` validates ledger files. Random draws' provenance now records whether `--pole` fixed the pole (`pole_forced`).
