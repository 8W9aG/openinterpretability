- Each model page has a concept map of its studied neurons. Each studied pole is a point, placed by the meaning of the text it fires on. A line joins the two poles of a neuron. Points link to their unit pages and show their status by shape as well as colour. Clusters are named only from their VERIFIED members' explanations, by the words that set them apart. `open-interpretability map <model>` builds the map with the new `map` extra, and the maps of the four configured models are in `data/concept-maps/` (#4422).
