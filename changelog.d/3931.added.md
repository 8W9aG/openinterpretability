- **APA style for comments and docstrings.** `make vale` now also checks the APA 7 rules that a pattern can check (`.vale/styles/APA`). They cover "et al.", the comma before a year, "&" against "and", and "e.g." and "i.e." only in parentheses. They also cover "%" after a numeral. The comments that broke them are fixed. The `commenting` skill lists the rules and the ones left out (#3931).
