- **Answer rule 1.1** (#4892). The reviewer writes 2–3 corrupted prompts of its own, and `patching` must pass on each. So the proposer can no longer choose the corrupted prompt after measuring. `patching_null` gates the 75th percentile of the random sets' |share| on each corrupted prompt, not the mean. The review draws the random sets' seed and records it (`answers review --seed` defaults to none). Each answer page shows every gate's margin from its line. Records judged by rule 1.0 keep their verdicts.
