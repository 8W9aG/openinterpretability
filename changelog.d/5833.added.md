- **X threads for papers and posts** (#5833).
  - A paper or a post can hold `thread.yaml`: 2 to 10 tweets, each about one section, with the section's figures. The agent that writes the paper or post writes it in the same PR.
  - `make build` and `make test` check each thread: the length and the number of its tweets, its sections and its links. They also check each figure's alt text and drawn image.
  - The feed carries the thread, and `social post` posts it as one unit, each tweet a reply to the one before. A thread starts only when today's budget has room for all of it. After a failure it resumes from the next tweet, and never posts a tweet twice.
  - `scripts/social_images.py` draws each figure that a thread names. The published paper on the bracket neuron, and the post on `explain`, have threads.
