- `open-interpretability loop` prints a `loop:` line at each stage of an iteration: the draw, the claim, each step, posting, the wait for checks and the merge. Before, it printed nothing between the draw and the `RESULT` line. Long loops with no output now report their progress once a minute. These are the random sets of a circuit review, the gains and mean activations of `explain`, and the unit pages of `build`. `docs/development.md` states the rule (#3142).
