- Every paper is a **preprint** or **published**, and says which in a status line under its abstract. The Research pages show it beside the title. A preprint names the open issues that would still change it. A published paper doesn't change, and later corrections go in an *Addenda* section at its end. Each paper has a `CITATION.cff`. `scripts/papers.py` writes the files and publishes a paper. Its `check`, part of `make lint`, fails on a missing status or citation, or on a changed published paper. All 7 papers are preprints today, each with work left (#3150).
