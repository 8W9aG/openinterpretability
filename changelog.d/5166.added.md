- New paper, *Are rank-1 LoRA directions as explainable as native neurons?* (`papers/lora-units/`). A rank-1 LoRA on every MLP projection of Qwen2.5-0.5B, trained on s1K-1.1, gives units that the audit verifies far less often than native neurons: 1 of 20 `down_proj` directions against 12 of 20 random native poles. The directions are dense, are active on about 17 times as many tokens and fail on the hard negatives. `scripts/lora_units.py` trains the adapter and writes its units, and `scripts/lora_units_stats.py` writes the tables (#5166).
