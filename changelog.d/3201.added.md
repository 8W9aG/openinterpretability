- **Ground-truth calibration with planted neurons** (`scripts/ground_truth.py`, `docs/analyses/ground-truth-calibration.md`, #3201). Twelve GPT-2 small neurons were rewritten to detect known rules. The 7 that passed a held-out gate went through the blinded pipeline. Every explanation named its plant's concept (6 match, 1 partial), and none of the 5 VERIFIED units had a wrong explanation. Two correct explanations got NEEDS_REVISION, both from reviewer labels that misjudged GPT-2's token splits. No plant that needs context passed the gate. `scripts/measure_unit.py` now serves both blinded studies.
