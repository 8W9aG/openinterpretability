- `make perf` (`scripts/perf_bench.py`) measures the wall time, CPU time and peak memory of a scan and a reproduction on GPT-2 small, and of the site build with `--build`. It warns when a measure grows past its tolerance over `docs/analyses/perf-baseline.json`: 1.5 times for time, 1.2 times for memory (`docs/analyses/perf.md`).
