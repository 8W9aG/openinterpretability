- **Explore draws weighted by a pole's effect on the loss** (`openinterp/pole_effects.py`, `openinterp/planner_effects.py`, `openinterp pole-effects`, `docs/analyses/pole-effects.md`, #3486). Attribution patching estimates every pole's effect from one backward pass per batch. It matches exact single-pole ablation with Spearman 0.97 on GPT-2 and 0.85 on Qwen2.5-0.5B. A share of explore draws (`effect_share`, default 0.3) picks a pole by that effect and records `selection: attribution`, so registry statistics stay unbiased. `audit --selection` records how a chosen unit was selected.
