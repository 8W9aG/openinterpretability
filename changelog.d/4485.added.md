- **Until-verified draws** (#4485). One draw in four keeps experimenting on its unit until it is VERIFIED, for up to 3 turns, all on one PR. The share is `UNTIL_VERIFIED_SHARE`, drawn from the plan's seed. A turn after NEEDS_REVISION revises the hypothesis, and one after REJECTED tries a new one. A VERIFIED or STOPPED verdict ends the draw early. `next` and `loop` take `--until-verified/--no-until-verified` and `--max-turns`. `next --continue <slug>` prints the next turn's steps for an agent. The ledger records the mode, and a draw counts once. Each turn's proposer sees every earlier attempt on the unit, through the `supersedes` chain.
