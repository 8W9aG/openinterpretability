- **Papers write maths in LaTeX, and the site typesets it** (#4211). The Research section reads `$...$` and `$$...$$` (`mdit-py-plugins`' dollarmath) and writes MathML at build time (`latex2mathml`), so pages load no script or font for maths. A formula that the site can't read stops the build. The `write-paper` skill has a new *Mathematics* section, and `tests/test_papers.py` fails on maths written as Unicode text. The current papers are converted.
