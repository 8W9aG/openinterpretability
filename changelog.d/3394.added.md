- **Literature page: "Activation Probes Are Reliable With a Handful of Positive Examples"** (`docs/literature/activation-probes-few-positives.md`, #3394). With 1 to 10 positives, probes trained on all the negatives beat probes trained on balanced sets, but not out of distribution. `probe-compare` already trains this way (2,000 positives against 8,000 negatives, class-weighted loss). Issue #3537 holds the follow-up: intervals for rows with few held-out positives, and recall at a 1% false-positive rate.
