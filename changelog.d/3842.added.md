- **Issues record "blocked by" relations, and the next-issue pick skips blocked ones** (#3842). `scripts/issue_state.py --blocked-by <issue> <blocker>` adds Linear's relation and GitHub's native dependency. `scripts/linear_sync.py` keeps the two the same each hour. Linear wins for an issue it names blockers for, and GitHub's blockers fill Linear's gaps. `scripts/next_issue.py` skips a candidate while an issue that blocks it is open, and says which one. The `next-issue` and `work-an-issue` skills say when to record a relation.
