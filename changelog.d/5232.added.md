- **A copy-failure family for fixes** (#5232). `scripts/copy_family.py` builds 855 wrong-copy and 194 right-copy prompts on pythia-70m. Each has measured probabilities and ranks, and greedy and sampled continuations. Seeded splits hold out every train prompt's reverse and every shared-number failure (`docs/analyses/copy-family.jsonl`, `.md`). `copy_family.py review-prompt` writes the frozen-fix hold-out task for a reviewer, who sees no fix and no measure.
