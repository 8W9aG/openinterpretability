- **Changelog fragments** (`changelog.d/`, `scripts/changelog.py`, #3490). Each change adds one file, `changelog.d/<issue>.<type>.md`, and no longer edits `CHANGELOG.md`. Parallel pull requests then never conflict on it. `make lint` checks that each fragment is named well and has a bullet, and that the Unreleased section stays empty. At each release, `scripts/changelog.py collect` writes the version's section and removes the fragments. The release and `work-an-issue` skills, CONTRIBUTING and `docs/development.md` say so.
