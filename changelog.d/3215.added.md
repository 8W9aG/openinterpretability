- A fourth kind of unit, the **superposition**: a feature that one layer spreads over several of its neurons (`docs/PROTOCOL.md` §1, issue #3215). It is a sparse direction over the layer's MLP hidden activations, with strength `max(0, ŵ · a)`. It is audited under the same rule as every unit, so the protocol version doesn't change. `openinterp audit --superposition <file>` scans one from its definition: the model, the layer, the members and their weights, and how it was found. `review`, `reproduce` and `score` work as for other units, and its causal metrics rank it against 64 null directions with as many members. Records go under `<model>/L<layer>/S<id>/`. The site's pages, links and counts (#3685), finding superpositions (#3686) and planner draws (#3687) come next.
