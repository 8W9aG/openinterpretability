- A preprint's abstract has a budget of about 130–220 words, with the one or two numbers that carry its claim (`write-paper`). `scripts/papers.py check` fails a preprint whose abstract is over 220 words, and it counts a formula as one word. Published papers are frozen and skip the check. Ten preprints had abstracts over the cap, and each now keeps its claim and its headline numbers (#6929).
