- **Each experiment has its own page, and unit pages list their experiments** (`openinterp/builder/templates/experiment.html`, `neuron.html`, `hrefs.experiment_href`, #3274). An experiment's page is at the path of its record under `wiki/experiments/`, e.g. `models/<model>/L3/N117-positive/<id>.html`, with links back to its unit, layer and model. A unit page now shows a table of its experiments in the same order (verdict, pole, date, proposer and reviewer, hypothesis), not each experiment in full. Every link to an experiment goes to its page. An old `#<side>-<id>` link on a unit page still works: a small script sends it on to the experiment's page.
