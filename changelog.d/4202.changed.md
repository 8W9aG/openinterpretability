- Papers take the shape of the papers we review (`write-paper` skill). The sections are: abstract, introduction with contributions, related work, method, one section per experiment, discussion, limitations, conclusion and appendices. `tests/test_papers.py` checks the sections and the numbered figures and tables. Papers get their own prose profile: sentences of up to 40 words, with a mean of 25 at most. `neuron-development` is rewritten as the worked example, and #4245–#4250 rewrite the other papers (#4202).
