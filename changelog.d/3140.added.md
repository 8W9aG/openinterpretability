- Each paper on the site has a PDF download (#3140). The build prints the paper with headless Chrome, laid out like a NeurIPS paper. The layout is a 5.5 by 9 inch text block, a serif face at 10 pt, an abstract, numbered sections and page numbers. Animated figures print their final frame. `open-interpretability build --no-paper-pdfs` skips them, and a machine with no Chrome builds none (`$OPENINTERP_CHROME` names one).
