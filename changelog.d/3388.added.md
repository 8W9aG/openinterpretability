- **Literature page: "Scoring Explanations Without Reading Them"** (`docs/literature/scoring-explanations-without-reading-them.md`, `scripts/explanation_free_rules.py`, #3388). The paper finds that evaluation items can be scored by rules that never read the explanation. Its check, run on the registry's 152,691 stored scoring windows, found such a leak in our fuzzing items. "Correct if the marks form one unbroken run" scores 0.579, against the judges' 0.593. Detection is mostly clean (0.536 against 0.602).
