- New paper, *Do neuron explanations come back on the weight average of a base and an instruct model?* (`papers/weight-average-keep/`). On Qwen2.5-1.5B, the weight average keeps 73 of 80 explanations, against 69 for the instruct model. The explanations fail a few at a time along the line between the models. `scripts/weight_average_keep.py` runs the test at each step with a random change of the same size, and `scripts/weight_average_keep_stats.py` writes the tables (#5404).
