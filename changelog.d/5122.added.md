- **Paper: how many of a neuron's weights does its explanation need?** in `papers/pruning-keep/` (#5122). It zeroes the smallest 50%, 75% or 90% of each VERIFIED Pythia-70m neuron's input weights, with a random null. At 90%, 63 of 127 explanations keep by magnitude, against 17 at random. `openinterpretability/interference/pruning.py` prunes a neuron's input rows and restores them. `scripts/pruning_keep.py` runs the keep test.
