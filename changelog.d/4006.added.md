- **Superpositions have their own page and table** (#4006). A superposition's page (`superposition.html`) shows its status, explanation, members and experiments, with none of a neuron's sections. Each layer page has a *Superpositions* table: id, status, members, experiments and explanation. Superpositions are no longer listed in the layer's units table. The members section and the experiments table are now shared partials (`_members.html`, `_experiments.html`). A superposition's breadcrumb names it (`Superposition S<id>`), where it read `Superposition 0`.
