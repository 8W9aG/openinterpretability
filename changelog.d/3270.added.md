- A takedown process for corpus excerpts (`SECURITY.md`, *A request to remove text*). `wiki/takedowns.jsonl` names a passage by hashes of its 8-word runs, never by its text. Each build removes the passage from the site, `registry.parquet` and the Neuronpedia export. `scripts/takedown.py` adds a takedown and removes the passage from the experiment pages. `openinterp check` allows that page edit only when an allowed key signed the takedown file. Records, their signatures and git history don't change.
