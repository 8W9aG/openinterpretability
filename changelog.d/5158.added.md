- A new paper, *Do neuron explanations survive 4-bit NF4 rounding?*. It rounds each linear weight of Qwen2.5-0.5B and Qwen2.5-1.5B to QLoRA's NF4, and tests every VERIFIED explanation again. On Qwen2.5-0.5B, 281 of 362 explanations keep. On Qwen2.5-1.5B, 66 of 75 keep, more than the 58 to 62 that random changes of the same size keep. At the 99% point, the probes drift 6.7 to 10.5 times as far as under bfloat16. `scripts/nf4_keep.py` runs the test, and `scripts/nf4_keep_stats.py` gives the drift, the paired tests and QLoRA's normality test (#5158).
