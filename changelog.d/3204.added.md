- **Retries: how many tries a VERIFIED unit took** (`scripts/retries.py`, `docs/analyses/retries.md`, #3204). 81% of the 570 VERIFIED units took one independent try, 16% two and 2% three. Per attempt, 24.2% of tries end at VERIFIED, against 29.9% of units. A union bound that charges each claim for its tries raises the expected false-discovery rate from 4.2% to at most 5.0%. Units VERIFIED after a retry score 0.04 lower on held-out detection, within judge (95% CI 0.008–0.080).
