- **Can a gate find the copy failures?** `docs/analyses/copy-gate.md` (`scripts/copy_gate.py`) fits text-keyed and mechanism-keyed gates on the copy family. After the last layer, a probe that also learned from corpus text catches 58% of held-out failures. It opens on 5 of 97 right copies and on none of 18,000 corpus tokens. At the layers where a module would sit, no gate is usable (#5233).
