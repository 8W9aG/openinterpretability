- **Pieces cells fill by how much of them is studied** (#3288). A cell's colour is still the best status in it. It now fills from the bottom with the share of its poles that are studied. In a model's bars that is the block's poles, and on a layer map the neuron's two poles. So one studied neuron no longer looks like a whole block. The amount is shown by length, not opacity: faded status colours fell below the colour-blind separation floor (protan ΔE 3.8 at 45% opacity).
