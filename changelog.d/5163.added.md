- A new paper, *Does pruning a few instruct-model neurons bring back the explanations that instruction tuning breaks?*. On Qwen2.5-0.5B-Instruct, it prunes the writes of the MLP neurons with the highest gradient times weight on the KL to the base model. Then it tests every VERIFIED explanation again. Pruning 5 neurons brings back 16 of the 63 explanations that fail on the instruct model, against 0 to 4 for random prunes. It also breaks 26 others and raises the KL, and larger prunes do more harm. `scripts/wrapper_prune.py` runs it, `scripts/wrapper_prune_check.py` checks the score one neuron at a time, and `scripts/wrapper_prune_stats.py` gives the tables (#5163).
