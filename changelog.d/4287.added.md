- Each registry circuit has its own page, `models/<model>/circuits/C<n>.html`. It shows the circuit's members with their status, where it sits, the wiring between members, its lineage and stability, and its sub-circuits. Each task hypothesis is listed with its standing and experiments. A stale experiment names the units that joined or left since it ran. Hub pages show the center's standing, its active share and whether it is default-on. They also show its output norm against its layer, and the circuits its neighbours belong to. The circuits index lists the registry circuits first, and the connected groups below them as *wiring groups* (#4287).
