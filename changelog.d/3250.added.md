- **`openinterp explain "<prompt>"`** (`openinterp/explain.py`, `openinterp/cli_explain.py`, #3250). It attributes the answer's logit to every MLP neuron pole, against each neuron's mean on a corpus sample (cached), and leaves out the first token. It lists the top poles with their registry status, and shows an explanation only for VERIFIED poles. It reports the share of the total |attribution| that VERIFIED poles hold (about 0.1% on Qwen2.5-0.5B today). It checks the top poles by mean-ablating them, alone and together.
