- `open-interpretability circuits identify` gives circuits ids that survive as the registry grows (#4284). It clusters each model's wiring graph with Leiden, and matches the clusters to the stored ids by member overlap. It appends new, grow, split, merge and gone events to `data/circuits/<model>.jsonl`, and the release runs it. Today's registry has four circuits in GPT-2 and one in Qwen2.5-0.5B.
