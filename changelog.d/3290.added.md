- **A concept map of a model's neurons is feasible** (`docs/analyses/concept-map-feasibility.md`, #3290). Each neuron is placed by the meaning of the text it fires on. The study used 821 GPT-2 and 846 Qwen2.5-0.5B studied neurons. A neuron's context vector picks out its own explanation first 11–17% of the time, against 0.12% by chance. Leiden clusters group neurons with similar explanations across layers, at 56% against 22% for random pairs on Qwen2.5-0.5B. The study recommends a map of the studied neurons first, and a full-model map only once that shows use.
