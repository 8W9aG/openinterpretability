- **Literature page: "Many Transcoder Features Are Neurons in Disguise"** (`docs/literature/neurons-in-disguise.md`, #3461). On gated models, 14–59% of late-layer active transcoder features align with one MLP neuron, and only those match the MLP across training runs. Our learned-basis baseline may partly compare neurons with neurons. Issue #3608 measures that share on GPT-2, whose MLP is GELU and which the paper does not test.
