- The inputs that papers, analyses and scripts read from the git-ignored `exchange/` are on the public Hugging Face dataset `sackfield/open-interpretability-exchange`, 2,505 files and 2.4 GB. `data/exchange-manifest.json` pins its revision and each file's SHA-256, and `scripts/exchange.py fetch` downloads and checks them. `scripts/exchange.py check` runs in `make lint` and CI, and it fails when a tracked file cites an unpublished `exchange/` folder.
