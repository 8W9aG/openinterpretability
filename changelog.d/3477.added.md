- **Literature page: "Neurons Speak in Ranges"** (`docs/literature/neurons-speak-in-ranges.md`, #3477). In residual-stream dimensions of BERT, GPT-2 and Llama-3.2-3B, each class occupies its own activation range. Zeroing only that range removes the class with less damage to other classes than zeroing the whole dimension. Not applicable to MLP neuron poles, but range-split modes are one form that #3215 can test.
