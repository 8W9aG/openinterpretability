- **Fuzzing decoys that match the real marks, and an explanation-free baseline on every score** (`openinterp/harness/explanation_scores.py`, #3505). A decoy now copies the count and spacing of the window's real marks, moved to inactive tokens. Before, decoys were scattered random tokens, and "correct if the marks form one unbroken run" scored 0.579 with no explanation, against the judges' 0.593. Each new score records `fuzzing_decoys: matched-shape` and a `rule_baseline` with the best explanation-free rules on its windows. Old scores are kept as they are, with `fuzzing_decoys` unset.
