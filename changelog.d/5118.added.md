- Paper *Does Fisher importance predict which explanations survive instruction tuning?*, in `papers/fisher-keep/` (#5118). A neuron's diagonal Fisher in Qwen2.5-0.5B does not predict whether its VERIFIED explanation keeps on the instruct model. The AUC is 0.475, against a within-layer null of 0.523 (p = 0.21). `openinterpretability/interference/fisher.py` reads the true and empirical Fisher and the activation RMS of every MLP neuron. `scripts/fisher_keep.py` runs the tests.
