- A new paper, *A traffic map of neuron explanations: which sites of pythia-70m does a change disturb?*. It scores each neuron write and attention head of pythia-70m by the gradient of the 176 VERIFIED explanations' probe readings. Then it tests the map with a random change of norm 1 at 118 sites. Over sites drawn evenly across the map, traffic predicts the explanations that flip (Spearman's ρ = 0.66). It predicts the drift of their readings with ρ = 0.80. Most of that is depth. At the top site, one change flips 22 of 176 explanations. `scripts/traffic_map.py` builds and tests the map, `scripts/traffic_dormant.py` reads each neuron's activity, and `scripts/traffic_stats.py` gives the tables (#5231).
