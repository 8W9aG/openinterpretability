- **A size budget for the repository, tracked in CI** (`scripts/repo_size.py`, `docs/analyses/repo-size.md`, #3254). CI reports the tracked size, the file count and the packed size of `.git`, and warns past `[tool.openinterp.size_budget]` (1,000 MB and 20,000 files). It never fails the job. `wiki/` grows by about 170 MB a week. Top examples and scored windows are 70% of each record. The bulky evidence will move to a content-addressed store (below).
- **Slim records, phase 1.** `openinterp/core/evidence.py` can move a record's two bulkiest fields, its top examples and explanation scores, to a content-addressed store. The record keeps their SHA-256 hashes, and loading resolves them. It is off by default (`OPENINTERP_OFFLOAD_EVIDENCE=1`) until uploads exist (#3254).
- **Slim records, phase 2: CI uploads the evidence.** `open-interpretability evidence push` sends a branch's blobs to their own branch, `evidence/<branch>`, so they never enter `main`'s history. CI uploads them to the public dataset `sackfield/open-interpretability-evidence` with `HF_TOKEN`, and `evidence verify` checks every added record. A workflow deletes the evidence branch when its PR closes (#3254).
