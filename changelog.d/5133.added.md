- `scripts/base_instruct_delta.py` reads the weight change of instruction tuning at each pole, and tests it against whether the pole's explanation keeps. On Qwen2.5-0.5B neither measure predicts it beyond depth: AUROCs of 0.60–0.62 fall to p of 0.32–0.82 when the labels are shuffled within each layer. The change is high-rank, and spread over almost every neuron (`docs/analyses/base-instruct.md`, #5133).
